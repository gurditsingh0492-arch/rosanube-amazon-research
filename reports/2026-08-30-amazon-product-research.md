## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Swedish Dishcloth / Cellulose Sponge Cloth Pack (10-pack)

- **Spanish keywords:** *bayeta sueca*, *paño celulosa reutilizable*, *bayeta ecológica cocina*, *swedish dishcloth*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y organización del hogar › Bayetas y paños de limpieza (crosses into Textiles de cocina search intent)
- **Why worth researching:** Cellulose / cotton "Swedish dishcloths" are a true replenishment SKU (used until they crack, then binned/composted). They're **tiny, flat, and light** — near-optimal FBA economics, easy poly-mailer packaging, low return risk. Amazon.es listings are still dominated by generic "eco-styled" packs with poor Spanish copy and cluttered geometric prints, which leaves a clean, muted brand angle open.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify with Helium 10):** The "reusable cleaning cloth" search intent is stable year-round on Amazon.es (part of the broader anti-single-use-plastic trend). Multiple listings appear to hold 3- and 4-figure review counts across generic sellers. Non-seasonal.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — many small European private labels (Swedish, German, Baltic origins), no dominant brand household name. Common price band **€8.90–€16.90 for 6–10 packs**. Review counts commonly 100–1,500 → entry realistic. Warning: the €10 barrier means this only works as a **10-pack** to lift AOV above Rosanube's usual floor.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Prints are loud, English-only, or copy the same US design (fruits/animals).
  - Very few listings state absorbency (mL) or actual dimensions in cm.
  - Care instructions ("dishwasher / microwave / washing machine safe") missing or hidden.
  - Titles keyword-stuffed in English + Spanish mixed → poor conversion.
  - Complaints about the cloth arriving stiff and users not knowing to wet it first.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Muted Iberian-neutral palette (natural, terracotta, salvia, off-white) — no cartoon prints.
  - Publish real absorbency (e.g. "absorbe hasta 15× su peso") and real dimensions in cm.
  - Include a small **first-use card in Spanish/Portuguese/English** ("humedece antes del primer uso").
  - Kraft-band packaging — recyclable, giftable, no plastic clam-shell.
  - Bullet 3 explicitly answers the biggest complaint: "reemplaza hasta 15 rollos de papel de cocina".
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one 10-pack mixed-neutral SKU** at ~€14.90. Later add a 20-pack "familia" refill (~€24.90) and a 6-pack "starter" only if margin proves out.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 7/10
  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (must verify FBA fee doesn't crush a sub-€15 unit)
  - Risk: 9/10 (small, light, non-fragile, low return, no cert complexity)
  - **Total: 38/50**

### 2) Muslin / Gauze Cotton Tea Towel Set (4-pack)

- **Spanish keywords:** *paños de cocina muselina*, *paños gasa algodón*, *trapos cocina algodón fino*, *tea towel muslin*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Muslin/gauze cotton kitchen towels are the **lint-free alternative** to terry and even to waffle. They photograph beautifully, dry fast, and are the tool of choice for straining, covering dough, and glassware polishing — a distinct use-case story that lets Rosanube own a lane next to (not against) the July 1 waffle SKU. Very compressible → excellent FBA economics.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round; not the biggest keyword but grows around baking / holiday cooking searches. Buyer intent is higher-quality-seeking, which supports the €18–€25 price band Rosanube prefers.
- **Competition signals:** Less crowded than terry or waffle. A few Turkish/Portuguese small brands and generic "vintage flour sack" sellers. Common price band **€12.90–€22.90 for 4-packs of 65×65 cm**. Reviews often 30–500 → **low review barrier** but demand also thinner — must be verified with Helium 10.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Confusion between "muselina" (baby swaddle) and "muselina de cocina" — many listings don't clarify.
  - GSM never stated; buyer can't tell "thin & flimsy" from "double-layer gauze".
  - Photos shot flat, no draping over glassware or bread bowl to show use.
  - Complaints about wrinkling and shrinkage (often 8–10% first wash) not disclosed.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Explicitly title as **"paños de cocina de muselina de algodón — doble capa"** to end the swaddle confusion.
  - Publish GSM and shrinkage disclosure ("pre-lavado, encoge <3%").
  - Include short "cómo usar" card in Spanish (colar caldo, cubrir masa, secar copas).
  - 3–4 muted colours only + one natural undyed — position as "the last kitchen towel you'll buy this year".
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **4-pack, 65×65 cm, mixed neutrals** at €19.90. Later add a 2-pack larger 80×80 cm "de horno / de pan" and a 4-pack "gift" holiday edition.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (demand size is the main unknown; wrinkling complaints must be addressed in copy)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 6/10 (verify — smaller keyword than waffle)
  - Competition: 8/10 (much less crowded)
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 8/10
  - **Total: 37/50**
- **Decision:** Further Research

### 3) 100% Cotton Fitted Sheet — Single SKU (90 / 135 / 150 cm)

- **Spanish keywords:** *sábana bajera ajustable algodón*, *sábana bajera 100% algodón*, *sábana ajustable 135*, *sábana ajustable 150*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** A **fitted sheet sold as a single item** (not a full set) is the highest-frequency bedsheet replenishment SKU — customers wear out the fitted sheet 2–3× faster than the top sheet. Selling *only* the bajera lets Rosanube limit variation complexity to size + colour, avoids the "top sheet not included / included" copy confusion that plagues Spanish set listings, and hits a repeat-purchase pattern. Compressible textile, medium FBA tier.
- **Demand signals:** Very large evergreen category on Amazon.es (bajera is a core household consumable). Standalone fitted-sheet listings frequently show 4-figure review counts.
- **Competition signals:** **Mixed** — dominated in part by established Spanish bedding brands (Pikolin, Burrito Blanco, Miracle Home) and by Amazon's own Amazon Basics equivalent. Common price band **€12.90–€24.90 for a single fitted sheet 135×190 cm**. Review counts often 500–5,000. This is a **real competition risk** — see risk score.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thread count / GSM not disclosed or inconsistent with feel.
  - Elastic described as "full elastic" but only around corners in reality → top review complaint.
  - Depth (altura del colchón) missing or in inches → returns from thick-mattress buyers.
  - Colour palette veers into "hotel white + pastel + saturated" only.
  - Spanish copy often machine-translated from EN with size charts still in inches.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Publish real specs**: 144 or 200 hilos, 100% peinado, elastic **all around**, mattress depth range in cm (up to 30 cm).
  - Muted natural palette (arena, salvia, gris piedra, blanco cálido) — avoid pastel.
  - Diagram in image 3 showing elastic route + depth measurement.
  - Sizing chart matched to Spanish mattress standards (90, 105, 135, 150, 160, 180).
  - Optional: sell in a fabric drawstring pouch — turns replenishment into a small "reset your bed" moment.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one colour × three sizes (90, 135, 150)** first. Add 105 + 160 only after 60 days of sales data. **Do not** launch a full set — that's a different research task.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (real competition from bedding incumbents; variation tree can bloat fast)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (biggest issue today)
  - Differentiation: 6/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (Amazon Basics presence squeezes price)
  - Risk: 6/10 (medium — size complexity, colour accuracy returns)
  - **Total: 32/50**
- **Decision:** Watchlist → Further Research after Helium 10 confirms the Amazon Basics share of voice

- **Spanish keywords:** *alfombra baño antideslizante*, *alfombrilla baño chenilla*, *alfombra ducha absorbente*, *set alfombras baño*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombrillas de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Chenille bath mats are a **replenishment** SKU (they mould, they stain, they get replaced every 12–18 months). Selling as a **2-pack in two sizes** (front-of-shower + in-front-of-sink) creates an obvious "why 2-pack" story and lifts AOV above the €25 floor Rosanube needs. Existing listings look almost identical (same white-background photo, same anti-slip PVC dot pattern), which is a photography and palette opening.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round with a small "moving-house / renovation" bump in Sep–Oct back-to-home season. Category top listings show 4-figure review counts.
- **Competition signals:** Very fragmented. No dominant brand. Common price band **€18.90–€32.90 for a 2-pack chenille set**. Review counts 200–3,000. Entry realistic if listing quality is above the average.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Every listing shows the same generic bathroom stock photo.
  - "Antideslizante" claimed with no photo of the PVC dot back.
  - Absorbency claims ("microfibra ultra absorbente") but no drying-time evidence.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Down / Feather Duvet Insert (*relleno nórdico plumón*)

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Rellenos nórdicos
- **Why rejected:**
  - **Bulky** → high FBA size tier → margin destroyed at Rosanube's €15–€35 price band.
  - Return risk is very high (buyers feel weight/warmth and often reject).
  - Down claims trigger animal-welfare compliance (Responsible Down Standard, traceability) which sits outside Rosanube's certification comfort zone.
  - Dominated by Pikolin, Emma, Amazon Basics, Ikea equivalents.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO** — violates the config's "avoid bulky / high return risk / brand-dominated / cert complexity" rules simultaneously.

### B) Bamboo "Eco" Bedsheet Set (*sábanas bambú ecológicas*)

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Juegos de sábanas
- **Why rejected:**
  - "Bamboo" claims trigger EU textile-labelling nuance (viscose from bamboo ≠ bamboo fibre) → real greenwashing / compliance risk.
  - Category is crowded with aggressive discounters running "1000 hilos bambú" claims that Rosanube would refuse to replicate.
  - Full set = 3–5 SKUs × multiple sizes × multiple colours → variation explosion the config explicitly warns against.
  - Margin thin after PPC in a highly PPC-competitive niche.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO** — config rules against organic/eco claims without proof, and variation complexity.

### C) Cotton Bathrobe (*albornoz de algodón*)

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Albornoces (crosses into Ropa)
- **Why rejected:**
  - **Sizing complexity** (S/M/L/XL/XXL + men/women) — config explicitly avoids "too many sizes / colours / variations".
  - **High return risk** — fit and terry weight expectations are subjective.
  - Photography needs models, which the config flags as an avoid.
  - Dominated by big towel/textile brands (Möve, Vossen, Zara Home) at the premium end and cheap terry generics at the low end — no clear middle for a new private label.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO** for now. Revisit only as a single unisex waffle-cotton kimono style if the July 1 waffle bath towel line ever scales.
