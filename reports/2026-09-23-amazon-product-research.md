### 1) Linen-Look Table Runner (Camino de Mesa Aspecto Lino)

- **Spanish keyword:** *camino de mesa lino*, *camino mesa efecto lino*, *runner mesa comedor*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de mesa › Caminos de mesa
- **Why worth researching:** Table runners are the highest-margin, lowest-return-risk item in the "textiles de mesa" sub-tree — no size fit issues, gift-friendly, seasonal cross-selling (Navidad, comuniones, bodas), and buyers select on aesthetic more than on price. "Aspecto lino" (linen-look, poly/cotton blend) removes the wrinkle complaint that dogs pure linen listings while keeping the visual.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Steady baseline demand year-round with an October–December uplift. Autumn/Christmas hosting drives replenishment. Category top listings typically show 500–3,000 reviews, consistent with a mid-density category, not a saturated one.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Dominant players are generic Chinese-origin brands + a few European private labels. Common price band €12–€26 for a single 40×140 cm to 40×180 cm runner; premium linen versions push €30–€45. Review-count barrier for page 1 estimated at ~500 reviews.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Photos on plain wooden tables — no dressed-table lifestyle context.
  - Colour delivered rarely matches image (top review complaint).
  - Only one length listed; buyers with 6- or 8-seat tables abandon.
  - No mention of GSM, weave, or whether it wrinkles.
  - Spanish copy is machine-translated (title stuffed, bullets weak).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 180–220 g/m² for linen-look poly/cotton).
  - "Sin arrugas / fácil planchado" claim only if pre-tested.
  - 3 neutral colours only (natural, salvia, arena) — no seasonal prints.
  - Two lengths from launch: 140 cm and 180 cm.
  - Dressed-table lifestyle photos (Iberian ceramic, terracotta, olive branches).
  - Recyclable kraft sleeve with Spanish/Portuguese/English care card.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch 2 SKUs (140 cm, 180 cm) in 3 colours. Later add a matching **napkin 6-pack** (see product #5) as a cross-sell bundle.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**

### 2) Waterproof Fitted Mattress Protector (Protector de Colchón Impermeable Ajustable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable ajustable*, *funda colchón transpirable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Cubrecolchones y protectores
- **Why worth researching:** A true replenishment SKU with a real functional promise (waterproof, breathable) that customers actively re-buy. Fitted design (elastic skirt) fits standard Spanish mattress sizes (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm). Buyers choose on feel + noise + durability, not on price alone — margin space exists for a quality private label.
- **Demand signals:** High and stable. Baby, pet, elderly-care, and general-hygiene use cases keep demand year-round. Amazon.es Best Sellers in "cubrecolchones" consistently include 5–10 protectores impermeables. Reviews on top listings often 5,000–20,000, indicating a mature but continuously purchased category.
- **Competition signals:** Moderate to high. Pikolin, Belnou, Velfont are present but not overwhelmingly dominant — many private labels rank in top 20. Price band €14–€28 for standard 135×190 cm; premium bamboo/Tencel versions push €30–€45. Review-count barrier is higher than most textiles picks: ~1,500–2,000 reviews for page-1 competitiveness.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Silencioso" claim but reviewers report crinkling (PU film without adequate top-cotton weight).
  - Fit complaints — elastic skirt too shallow for modern 25–30 cm mattresses.
  - Ambiguous top-layer composition (buyers can't tell cotton vs. polyester).
  - Not machine-washable at 60°C despite hygiene positioning (buyer expectation).
  - Weak Spanish size labelling; mattress dimension conventions vary.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real top-layer composition (target 80% cotton / 20% polyester terry, ~150 g/m²) + PU membrane spec.
  - Deep skirt (≥30 cm) documented in title and images.
  - "Lavable a 60°C" documented, plus tumble-dry safe.
  - Silent-membrane claim only if lab-tested by supplier.
  - 5 standard Spanish sizes from launch: 90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single-SKU per size at launch. Later: **protector + 2 pillow protectors** bundle. Avoid colour variations (white only) to keep SKU count and photo cost low.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (return risk higher than towels — fit and noise complaints; also 5 sizes = 5 SKUs of inventory)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (mature category)
  - Differentiation: 6/10 (harder to visually differentiate; wins on spec + reviews)
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10 (5-SKU inventory, return risk moderate)
  - **Total: 33/50**

Textile-only product (no cosmetic claim), aligned with Rosanube's textile brief. Repeat purchase driven by loss/wear; strong sustainability angle without needing organic certification (durability claim, not eco claim). Ships in a padded envelope — lowest FBA fee tier possible.
- **Demand signals:** Growing 2024–2026 as Spanish consumers shift from single-use cotton discs. Search volume trend upward. Category present in both Hogar and Belleza search results, giving cross-category discoverability.

## Do Not Pursue — 3 Rejected Ideas

### A) Weighted Blanket (Manta con Peso / Manta Sensorial)

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Mantas
- **Why rejected:** Heavy (5–9 kg), oversized packed dimensions push into Amazon's large/heavy FBA size tier — fees can swallow 30–40% of price. Return risk is elevated (buyers dislike the weight or heat). Overlapping medical/therapeutic positioning in the category creates claim risk (config forbids therapeutic/medical claims). Dominated by a few strong brands (Gravity, Levia, Bedsure). Fails the "lightweight, easy FBA, low compliance friction" profile.

### B) Silk / Satin Pillowcase (Funda de Almohada de Seda)

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada
- **Why rejected:** Buyers select heavily on "mommes" (silk weight) and expect certified mulberry silk; anything less generates 1-star reviews and returns. High refund risk from colour/shine mismatch to product photography. Dominated by a small group of premium brands (Slip, Blissy) plus a race-to-the-bottom cluster of polyester satin sold as "seda" (misrepresentation risk). Fails the "low return risk" and "low compliance friction" profile — and the €30–€60 top price tier is exactly where returns hurt most.

### C) Electric Heated Throw / Manta Eléctrica

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Mantas › Mantas eléctricas
- **Why rejected:** Electric = out of Rosanube's textile brief and immediately fails the config's "avoid electronics" rule. Requires CE / LVD / EMC certification, GPSR responsible person for an electrical product, and carries fire/liability risk. High return risk (thermostat complaints). Dominated by Beurer, Medisana, Imetec. Not appropriate as a private-label textile launch.
