### 2) Cotton Waffle Bath Mat — 2-piece bath rug set, 50×80 cm + 50×60 cm (contour-free)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón waffle*, *alfombrilla baño panal algodón*, *set alfombras baño algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The Amazon.es bath-mat category is dominated by two extremes — cheap microfiber shag mats with foam backing (return risk from mould and non-slip failure) and premium hotel-style terry mats (heavy, slow-drying). A **flat cotton waffle** mat sits in a real gap: it dries fast, weighs little (better FBA fee tier), is machine-washable at 60 °C, and photographs as a clean, modern bathroom textile. This is a natural cross-sell for buyers of Rosanube's future waffle kitchen towels.
- **Demand signals:** Bath mats are a year-round replenishment SKU (worn, stained, replaced); Spanish apartment buyers regularly search for lightweight, quick-drying options; waffle bath textiles are rising in EU DTC catalogues.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Backing type unclear — buyers can't tell if it slides on tile.

  - Complaints about the mat curling at the edges after wash.
  - Colour drift between listing photo and delivered product.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Weighted Blanket ("manta con peso" / *manta pesada*)
- **Why rejected:** Bulky and heavy → high FBA fee tier and freight cost; high return risk (weight-fit mismatch, "too hot", noise from glass beads); dominated by a handful of established brands (Gravity, Levia, Amazon Basics); therapeutic/anxiety-relief claims risk crossing into medical-device territory under EU rules. Fails the *avoid* list on bulk, brand-dominance, and compliance risk simultaneously.

### B) King-Size Cotton Duvet Cover Set 240×220 cm ("funda nordica 240x220")
- **Why rejected:** Large flat pack → oversized FBA tier and monthly storage cost hurt Rosanube's €15–€35 target lane; segment is dominated by big EU brands (Pikolin, HomeLife, Vivaraise, Amazon Basics) with heavy PPC spend; return rate is elevated on king-size bedding (fit and colour complaints); size/colour matrix balloons variation count. Better to enter bedding through the smaller pillowcase-only SKU first (see Top 5 #5) before attempting duvet covers.

### C) Licensed Character Kids' Bedsheet Set ("sábanas Disney / sábanas Marvel / sábanas fútbol")
- **Why rejected:** Licensed-IP products require a Disney / Marvel / La Liga licence — Rosanube does not hold one, and Amazon.es actively removes unauthorised listings, with counterfeit strikes on the seller account. Even unlicensed "inspired by" prints attract IP takedowns. Fails the *avoid* list on IP/licensing risk. Not appropriate for a private-label first-mover strategy.
