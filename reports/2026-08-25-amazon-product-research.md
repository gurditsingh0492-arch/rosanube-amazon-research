### 2) Cushion Cover 2-pack — 45×45 cm (Funda de cojín)

- **Why worth researching:** Highest-volume decorative textile category on Amazon.es outside bed linen. Buyers replace covers every 1–3 years for styling refreshes, and 45×45 cm is the near-universal Spanish sofa cushion size, so a **single SKU covers ~80% of buyer intent** with no size complexity. Repeat purchase of a matched pair (2-pack) is the natural buying pattern. Autumn is one of two annual demand peaks (interior restyling for the new season).

- **Why rejected:** Race-to-the-bottom category dominated by Amazon Basics, Vileda, Spontex and a wall of €5–€10 generic packs. Very thin margins after FBA + PPC + returns; almost no realistic differentiation lever for private label without inventing environmental claims Rosanube cannot substantiate. Fails config's "weak margin after VAT/FBA/PPC" and "products with very low differentiation opportunity" rules. Also brand-dominance concentration.

### B) Duvet cover 3-piece set / Funda nórdica 3 piezas
- **Why rejected:** Requires supporting **at least 4 Spanish bed sizes** (90, 105, 135, 150, 180) × several colours = 20+ SKUs to be credible → far above Rosanube's target size complexity. Category has established mid-brands (Pikolin, Amazon Basics, Textilonline, HOMESCAPES) with 5-figure review counts, and the top listings are increasingly print-heavy which forces creative / photography spend. Very high return rate driven by colour, fit and fabric mismatch. Also FBA size tier for the largest cover pushes the fee upward, hurting the margin. Compliance is fine, but the size × colour × return-risk combination breaks the "simple textile essentials" profile.

### C) Terry Bathrobe / Albornoz baño rizo
- **Why rejected:** Requires apparel sizing (S / M / L / XL / XXL) × at least 2 colours × two versions (adulto / infantil) — high SKU complexity for a first launch. High weight and bulk (600–900 g packed) → high FBA fee tier and expensive freight. High return risk (fit, weight, colour, hood-vs-no-hood expectation). Dominated by El Corte Inglés and a small number of terry-specialist brands on Amazon.es. Fails the "avoid many sizes/colours" and "avoid heavy/bulky" rules in config.
