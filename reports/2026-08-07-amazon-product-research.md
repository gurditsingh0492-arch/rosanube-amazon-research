### 2) Cotton Kitchen Apron — Cross-Back Strap, Unisex

- **Spanish keyword:** *delantal cocina algodón*, *delantal cruzado espalda*, *delantal unisex lino algodón*

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Explicit **electronics** — outside Rosanube's textile lane and directly excluded by config §Products to Avoid ("Electronics", "Batteries", "Products with unclear legal or safety restrictions"). Requires EU low-voltage + EMC certification, GPSR file, dedicated safety documentation, and carries elevated return + warranty + product-liability risk. Category dominated by established European electrical brands (Beurer, Imetec, Medisana). Even if the fabric portion could be private-labelled, the electrical assembly is a compliance minefield for a Portugal-based textile seller. **Rejected.**

### B) Licensed-Character Kids' Duvet Cover (*funda nórdica Disney / Marvel / Frozen*)
- **Why rejected:** Requires a valid IP/licence agreement with the rights holder (Disney, Marvel, Netflix, etc.). Config §Products to Avoid explicitly lists "Products with strong IP/licensing risk" and "Licensed character products unless legally licensed". Sub-category is fully anchored by official licensees with wholesale contracts Rosanube cannot match. Also brings size complexity (90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 180 cm) and heavy colour/print return risk. **Rejected.**

### C) Latex Mattress Topper (*topper colchón látex*)
- **Why rejected:** Bulky and heavy → very high FBA size tier + inbound freight cost. High return risk (sensitivity to smell, firmness, and thickness — buyers try it for a night and return). Size complexity across 6+ Spanish bed sizes (90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 180 / 200 cm). Requires OEKO-TEX / CertiPUR-equivalent documentation for credibility, and customers expect certificates Rosanube would need to buy in. Combined with brand-dominated competition (Emma, Bedland, Naturalex) this is the classic "big blob of risk" the config warns against. **Rejected.**
