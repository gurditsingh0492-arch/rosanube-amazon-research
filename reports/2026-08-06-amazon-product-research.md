### 1) 100% Cotton Fitted Sheet — Sábana Bajera Ajustable (percal / algodón peinado)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera 100% algodón*, *sábana bajera ajustable 135*, *sábana bajera percal*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are one of the highest replenishment SKUs in the bedroom category — worn out, stained, and replaced far more often than duvet covers. Amazon.es sizes are standardised on 90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm Spanish mattress widths, so the size tree is manageable if Rosanube launches with just the two dominant sizes (90 for single / 135 for standard double). Cotton (percal 200 hilos / 400 TC) is the buyer's proxy for "quality" and is easy to describe honestly.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent daily demand year-round; small uplifts around September (back-to-school / student housing) and Black Friday. Multiple listings show 4-figure review counts in the top-30 slot — a signal of steady, not spikey, volume.
- **Competition signals:** Present but fragmented. A few mid-size Spanish bedding brands (Pikolin Home, ES-Tela, Belnou) anchor the top; many private labels sit in slot 10–40 with 200–2,000 reviews. Price band €12–€25 for 90 cm, €15–€30 for 135 cm.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fibre composition ambiguous ("100% algodón" claimed on cotton-blend product — common complaint driver).
  - Thread count / hilos not stated or inflated.
  - Elastic goes slack after 5–10 washes.
  - Shrinkage after first wash pulls the sheet off the mattress corner.
  - Colour photos differ from delivered colour.
  - Depth (alto colchón) not stated — critical for 25–30 cm modern mattresses.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish honest fibre composition + thread count (target 200 hilos percal, single-ply).
  - **State mattress-depth range explicitly in cm** (target 25–35 cm elastic pocket) — an obvious buyer question most listings dodge.
  - Reinforced 360° elastic; documented pre-shrunk status.
  - Muted Iberian palette (blanco natural, gris piedra, arena, salvia) — 4 colours, not 12.
  - Spanish care label + Spanish fibre label + hanging tag with size diagram.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **single SKU per size** in 4 colours: 90×190 and 135×190 cm. Add pillowcase 2-pack in matching colours later for cross-sell. Delay 105/150/180 sizes until reviews and sell-through are proven — do not over-fragment inventory.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** — main risk is sizing/depth complaints and colour returns.

### 2) Cotton Cloth Napkin Set — Servilletas de Tela Algodón (12-pack)

- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Textiles de mesa › Servilletas

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket — *Manta Eléctrica*
- **Why rejected:** Contains electronics + heating element → falls squarely inside the config's "avoid electronics / batteries / safety-cert products". Requires CE + LVD + EMC + RoHS + REACH + GPSR documentation for the electrical assembly; supplier due diligence is expensive and slow. High return risk (thermostat complaints, overheating fears). Bulky in packed form → higher FBA fee than a pure textile. Category is anchored by established brands (Beurer, Imetec, Medisana). Zero brand fit for Rosanube's "clean textile" positioning.

### B) Memory-Foam Mattress Topper — *Topper Colchón Memory Foam*
- **Why rejected:** Bulky, heavy (5–10 kg for 135 cm), oversized FBA tier destroying margin. Category dominated by Pikolin, Naturalex, Emma-adjacent, plus large Chinese white-label brands with 10k+ reviews. Very high return risk (odour, firmness mismatch, "not as pictured" complaints). High compliance load: PU-foam chemical safety (VOC / REACH), flame-retardant regs, fibre-content labelling. Fails the config "avoid very heavy / bulky / high-return-risk / brand-dominated" rules in one go.

### C) Baby Muslin Swaddle Blanket — *Muselina Bebé Envolver*
- **Why rejected:** Product for children under 36 months → falls under EN 71 toy-adjacent safety rules and stricter GPSR obligations; "safe sleep" liability exposure is high. Category dominated by aden+anais, Little Unicorn, and long-established EU baby brands with strong review moats. Very high return / negative-review risk (parents are the most exacting textile buyer segment). Zero brand fit for Rosanube's home-textile positioning — dilutes the brand. Certificate expectation (OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I for baby, ideally GOTS) mandatory in practice, which the config forbids claiming without documented certificates.
