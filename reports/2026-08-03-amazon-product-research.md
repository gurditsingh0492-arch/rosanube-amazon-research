## 1. Waffle-Weave Kitchen Tea Towels — 6-Pack (Paños de cocina gofre)

**Category:** Hogar y cocina → Textiles del hogar → Cocina → Paños de cocina

**Why worth researching:**
Waffle/gofre weave kitchen towels are a classic repeat-purchase textile essential on Amazon.es. The category is fragmented — no single Spanish brand dominates. Most top listings are terry (rizo) or plain cotton; a waffle-weave pack in a modern neutral palette (arena, salvia, terracota) is a clean differentiation angle.

**Demand signals (qualitative — needs Helium 10 to quantify):**
- "Paños de cocina" is a staple search term with steady year-round demand (not seasonal).
- Multipacks of 4–6 dominate the first Amazon.es page, suggesting customers prefer packs to singles.
- Reviews consistently in the 200–2,000 range for mid-page listings, indicating an accessible category.

**Competition signals:**
- Top listings are a mix of no-name Chinese sellers and a few Spanish/European private labels (Amazon Basics, Blumtal, Utopia).
- No single seller holds >20% of top 20 organic slots.
- BSRs on top listings appear to sit in the 500–5,000 range in the Kitchen sub-BSR (needs Keepa confirmation).

**Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
- Cheap photography, single flat-lay shot on white
- Vague GSM/fabric weight ("premium cotton" with no number)
- Poor Spanish translation on Chinese seller listings
- Small pack sizes (2–3) at high unit prices
- Loose weave that customers complain "leaves lint" or "shrinks after first wash"

**Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
- Publish exact GSM (target 380–450 g/m² waffle)
- Loop hanger + colour-coded stripe for "raw/cooked/hands" use (Marie Kondo–style utility)
- Modern neutral 3-colour palette per pack
- Spanish-language care label + insert card with washing instructions
- Recycled kraft belly-band packaging (photograph-friendly)

**Bundle / variation strategy:**
- Primary SKU: 6-pack single-colour set
- Variation A: 6-pack "trio duo" (2× arena, 2× salvia, 2× blanco)
- Bundle add-on: matching oven mitt pair (future SKU 2)

**Estimated risk level:** Low

**Score /50:** **40 / 50**
- Demand: 8/10 — steady, non-seasonal, repeat-purchase
- Competition: 8/10 — fragmented, no dominant brand
- Differentiation: 9/10 — GSM, weave, palette, packaging all viable
- Margin potential: 7/10 — €12–€22 sale range is tight but bundle economics work
- Risk: 8/10 — low FBA fee tier, no certification risk, easy compliance

## 2. Anti-Stain Linen-Look Rectangular Tablecloth (Mantel antimanchas efecto lino)

**Category:** Hogar y cocina → Textiles del hogar → Comedor → Manteles

**Why worth researching:**
Anti-stain (antimanchas) tablecloths are one of the few table linen categories with visibly weak differentiation on Amazon.es. Spanish households eat at the table daily; a wipe-clean linen-look product hits a real pain point. Most existing listings are polyester with a plasticky feel — a softer poly-cotton blend or true PU-coated linen weave is a clear improvement angle.

**Demand signals (qualitative):**
- "Mantel antimanchas" and "mantel rectangular resinado" are stable evergreen searches.
- Listings 5–15 in the ranking typically show 500–3,000 reviews, suggesting steady sales.
- Multiple sizes (140×180, 140×240, 140×300) driven by real Spanish table sizes — not a fashion signal.

**Competition signals:**
- Dominated by no-name sellers and a few Portuguese/Italian brands (Vinylla, Lovely Casa).
- Amazon Retail not present in most sizes.
- Design-heavy listings often look dated (florals, mediterranean tiles) — modern minimal aesthetic is under-served.

**Common weaknesses:**
- Plasticky hand-feel complaints in reviews
- Colour bleeds after wipe-cleaning
- Sizes often mislabeled (customer expects 140×240, receives 137×236)
- Poor packaging — arrives creased and never flattens

**Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
- True linen-look weave with matte PU coating (not shiny vinyl)
- Accurate size labelling in cm, printed on care label
- 5 clean palette colours: blanco roto, lino natural, gris piedra, salvia, arena
- Rolled (not folded) in a cardboard tube to avoid crease complaints
- Include a Spanish-language "care & wipe" card

**Bundle / variation strategy:**
- Primary: 140×240 mantel
- Variations: 140×180, 140×300, 140×360
- Bundle: mantel + 4 matching napkins (future add-on SKU)

**Estimated risk level:** Medium

**Score /50:** **37 / 50**
- Demand: 8/10 — evergreen, high daily use
- Competition: 7/10 — weak listings, few strong brands
- Differentiation: 8/10 — hand-feel + palette + packaging all improvable
- Margin potential: 8/10 — €18–€35 sale price with reasonable FBA cost
- Risk: 6/10 — Medium: size complexity (4 sizes), PU coating needs REACH check

## 3. Cotton Guest Hand Towels — 4-Pack (Toallas de invitados / manos)

**Category:** Hogar y cocina → Textiles del hogar → Baño → Toallas de manos

**Why worth researching:**
Guest / hand towels are the "easy first launch" of home textiles — small size (30×50 or 40×70), low FBA fee tier, no size variations, and dominated on Amazon.es by either full-bath sets or single towels. A dedicated 4-pack hand-towel SKU at 500–600 GSM in a neutral palette is a clean gap.

**Demand signals (qualitative):**
- "Toallas de manos" and "toallas invitados" are stable searches, especially around Christmas + spring cleaning.
- Multipacks of 4–6 dominate top results — same pattern as kitchen towels.
- Category shows healthy review distribution — mid-page listings have 300–1,500 reviews.

**Competition signals:**
- Fragmented: mix of Amazon Basics, Utopia Towels, Blumtal, small European sellers.
- Amazon Basics is present but its towels get mediocre reviews (thin, poor absorbency).
- Few sellers combine premium GSM + neutral modern palette + Spanish-language listing.

**Common weaknesses:**
- Thin GSM (300–400) marketed as "premium"
- Colour options limited to white / grey / navy
- Absorbency complaints — dyes reduce absorbency and reviewers notice
- Fraying at the border after 10 washes
- No hanging loop or poorly stitched loop

**Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
- 550 GSM combed ring-spun cotton, publish the GSM in the title
- Reinforced double-stitched dobby border with visible cotton loop
- Modern neutral 4-colour palette (blanco, arena, terracota, salvia)
- "Enzyme-washed for first-use absorbency" claim (only if supplier confirms)
- Belly-band packaging with tactile invitation to feel the weight

**Bundle / variation strategy:**
- Primary: 4-pack, one colour
- Variations: 4-pack solid colour × 4 colour options
- Cross-sell bundle: 2× hand towels + 2× face towels combo pack

**Estimated risk level:** Low

**Score /50:** **41 / 50**
- Demand: 8/10 — evergreen, gift-friendly at Christmas
- Competition: 7/10 — Amazon Basics is beatable on quality
- Differentiation: 9/10 — GSM/weave/palette/packaging all in reach
- Margin potential: 8/10 — €18–€28 pack, hand towels are small = low FBA fee
- Risk: 9/10 — smallest FBA tier, no certifications needed beyond fibre label

## 4. Cotton Cushion Cover Set — 45×45 or 50×50, 4-Pack (Fundas de cojín)

**Category:** Hogar y cocina → Textiles del hogar → Salón → Fundas de cojín

**Why worth researching:**
Cushion covers are one of the few Amazon.es textile categories where design and palette drive the buying decision more than price, and where private label can charge €4–€8 per cover (€16–€32 pack). The market is enormous but heavily fragmented, and most top listings look like they were photographed on a phone.

**Demand signals (qualitative):**
- "Fundas de cojín 45x45" and "50x50" are stable, high-volume searches on Amazon.es.
- The category is not dominated by any single brand.
- Seasonality is mild — decor refresh happens spring, autumn, and Christmas.

**Competition signals:**
- Extremely fragmented — hundreds of small sellers.
- Amazon Retail absent from most sub-searches.
- Top sellers often win on price + high review count, not quality or presentation.

**Common weaknesses:**
- Hidden zipper stitching visible and cheap-looking
- Colour on photo ≠ colour on arrival (biggest single complaint)
- Fabric described as "linen" that is 100% polyester
- Too-thin fabric that shows cushion insert through cover
- Only one colour per SKU — no cohesive palette sets

**Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
- Honest fibre labelling: 100% cotton or 55/45 linen-cotton, no "linen-look" wording
- Deep colour accuracy — invest in one calibrated photo shoot
- 4-pack cohesive palette sets (e.g., "salvia trio + arena solid")
- Concealed YKK-style zipper with reinforced stitching
- 220–240 GSM cotton canvas weight

**Bundle / variation strategy:**
- Primary: 4-pack in curated palette (45×45)
- Variations: same palette in 50×50, 60×60
- Add-on: matching table runner (future SKU)

**Estimated risk level:** Medium

**Score /50:** **36 / 50**
- Demand: 9/10 — huge, evergreen
- Competition: 6/10 — very crowded, but weak-quality
- Differentiation: 8/10 — colour accuracy + honest labelling = strong angle
- Margin potential: 7/10 — €16–€28 pack, cost sensitivity is real
- Risk: 6/10 — Medium: colour returns are the main risk, photography investment needed

## 5. Quick-Dry Microfiber Bath Mat with Non-Slip Backing (Alfombra de baño antideslizante)

**Category:** Hogar y cocina → Textiles del hogar → Baño → Alfombras de baño

**Why worth researching:**
Bath mats are a low-variation, high-turnover textile with strong repeat-purchase from renters, students, and gift-buyers. Most existing Amazon.es listings compete on absorbency or thickness, but very few combine quick-dry microfiber, a genuinely non-slip TPR (not PVC) backing, and a machine-washable full-body design.

**Demand signals (qualitative):**
- "Alfombra de baño antideslizante" is a stable evergreen search.
- Category has multiple sub-searches (memory foam, chenille, microfiber) — microfiber is the volume sub-segment.
- Steady demand around university term start (Sep) and spring cleaning (Mar–Apr).

**Competition signals:**
- Chenille mats dominate the top of "alfombra baño" but reviewers complain they take 24h+ to dry.
- Microfiber sub-segment is less crowded and has weaker listings.
- Amazon Basics present but reviews criticise the PVC backing peeling after 20 washes.

**Common weaknesses:**
- PVC backing peels or cracks after washing
- Takes too long to dry, mildew smell
- Colour bleeds onto tile grout
- Non-slip only when new — degrades fast
- Sizes vary wildly vs advertised

**Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
- TPR (thermoplastic rubber) backing rated for 100+ washes at 40°C
- Publish drying-time claim ("dry in 4 hours") — only if supplier lab tests confirm
- Neutral 4-colour palette: blanco, arena, gris piedra, salvia
- Accurate cm sizing on care label + product page
- Two-mat set (small + large) as a bundle SKU

**Bundle / variation strategy:**
- Primary: 50×80 single mat
- Variations: 40×60, 60×90, and set of 2
- Cross-sell: matching hand towel 4-pack (SKU 3 above)

**Estimated risk level:** Medium

**Score /50:** **35 / 50**
- Demand: 8/10 — evergreen with student/rental spikes
- Competition: 6/10 — chenille dominates; microfiber sub-niche weaker
- Differentiation: 7/10 — backing material + drying time are real angles
- Margin potential: 7/10 — €15–€25 sale, medium-tier FBA fee
- Risk: 7/10 — Medium: backing quality risk, REACH check needed on TPR

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A. King-size Duvet Cover Set — 240×220 (Juego funda nórdica king)
**Reason:** Rejected. High-cube FBA fee tier destroys margin, size complexity is severe (135/150/180/200 all common in Spain), colour returns are frequent, and the category is meaningfully brand-dominated (Amazon Basics, Pikolin Home, Ikea alternatives). Also fails the "not too many variations" rule in the config.

### B. Weighted Blanket — 7 kg (Manta con peso 7 kg)
**Reason:** Rejected. Falls into two config-avoid buckets: (1) medical/therapeutic claim risk ("anxiety relief", "sleep aid") that requires substantiation and can trigger listing suppression, and (2) very heavy product with high FBA fee tier and high return risk. Also increasingly dominated by established players (Gravity, Kuddly, Aeris).

### C. Aromatherapy Spa Towel Set with Essential Oils (Set toallas spa aromaterapia)
**Reason:** Rejected. Combining textile with fragrance/essential oil crosses into cosmetic/CLP labelling territory, requiring EU cosmetic responsible person, INCI labelling, and CLP hazard classification for the oils. Return risk is high (allergic reactions), and the "aromatherapy" claim is legally sensitive. Complexity is disproportionate to a first launch.
