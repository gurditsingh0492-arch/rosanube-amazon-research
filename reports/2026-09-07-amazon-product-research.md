### 1) Muslin Multipurpose Kitchen Cloth — 6 or 8-pack (double-gauze cotton)

- **Spanish keyword:** *paños muselina cocina*, *trapos muselina algodón*, *paños cocina algodón grande*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Muslin (double-gauze cotton) cloths are the "professional kitchen / cheese-cloth / bar-blotter / straining / drying" workhorse and a true replenishment SKU. On Amazon.es the top results are dominated by bulk white 60×60 cm packs aimed at bakers — there is a clean private-label gap for a **home** version in a muted palette with a hanging loop and Spanish care copy. Different enough from the July 1 waffle pack to sit alongside it in the same lane without cannibalising.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM / gauze layer count almost never stated (single vs double gauze).

  - Listing angle: "5 usos en la cocina" (colar, secar cristal, envolver pan, cubrir masa, filtrar caldo).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** — 6-pack, 60×60 cm, mixed neutrals, €18.90. Once reviews stabilise add an 8-pack "starter home" and a 2-pack of larger 80×80 cm for straining/preserving.

### 2) Anti-Slip Cotton Chenille Bath Mat — 50×80 cm (single + 2-pack)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño antideslizante*, *alfombra baño chenilla*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Bath mats are a replenishment SKU (mildew, wear, colour fade). Amazon.es is heavy on cheap microfibre "plush" mats that shed and slip; the cotton chenille segment is smaller, higher-priced (€18–€30 for a 50×80 cm), and has repeated complaints about the anti-slip backing wearing off in the washing machine. Rosanube can enter with a documented latex-backed cotton chenille and a real "washable at 40°C, backing survives 30 washes" claim (only if the sample confirms).
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round with a small September lift ("vuelta a casa" restock). Not fashion-trend dependent.
- **Competition signals:** Moderately competitive. A few Spanish brands hold BSR ranks 5–20 in *Alfombras de baño*; long tail is fragmented. Price band **€15–€28** single; **€28–€45** 2-pack. Review counts on incumbents 500–5,000 → non-trivial but not walled.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Complaints about **backing peeling** after 3–5 washes (dominant negative review).
  - Complaints about **shedding** and lint transfer to the floor.
  - Sizes photographed against oversized bathrooms → customers under-estimate size.
  - "Antideslizante" claimed without any surface description or test.
  - Colours photographed under warm studio light, delivered version reads greyer.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Latex-dotted backing (not full-coat film — survives washing better); document wash cycles in the listing.
  - Publish real GSM (target 1500–1800 g/m² for chenille) and pile height.
  - "En tu baño real" lifestyle photo (Spanish tile scale reference next to a 40 cm sink base).
  - 3–4 muted colours (natural, greige, blush, deep green); no primary colours.
  - Include a folded-corner shot showing the dot pattern so buyers can inspect the backing before buying.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch single 50×80 cm at €22.90. Add a **2-pack** (matched colours, one 50×80 + one 45×45 contorno WC) at €36.90 as the hero bundle — this is the differentiator vs. one-size incumbents.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (return risk if backing claim fails; slightly heavier FBA tier than kitchen textiles)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10 (heavier item → higher FBA; sensitive to backing failure returns)
  - **Total: 35/50**
- **Decision:** **Further Research** — must confirm backing durability with a sample wash cycle before any purchase order.

### 3) Percale Fitted-Sheet 2-Pack (bajera ajustable, sin encimera)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera ajustable*, *sábana bajera algodón percal*, *pack 2 sábanas bajeras*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Spanish bed-linen culture does **not** rely on a top sheet (unlike UK/US) — the *bajera ajustable* is the true replenishment SKU. Buyers replace them 1–2× a year and often want two of the same colour to rotate. Yet Amazon.es is full of "juego 3 piezas" sets (bajera + encimera + funda almohada) that force customers to buy the top sheet they don't want. There is a clean gap for a **fitted-sheet-only 2-pack** in the two most-common Spanish mattress sizes (135 cm and 150 cm).
- **Demand signals:** Very steady, non-seasonal. Small September/January lift ("vuelta a casa", "rebajas"). One of the largest textile sub-categories on Amazon.es by absolute review counts.
- **Competition signals:** Category is competitive but fragmented at the "no encimera" niche. Top BSR is dominated by big brands (Pikolin, Burrito Blanco, Amazon Basics) on juegos; the fitted-only slice is much smaller — realistic entry with focused keyword strategy. Price band **€18–€35** for 2-pack percale.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Bajera depth ("altura del cajón") often unspecified — customers with 25–30 cm mattresses complain the sheet pops off.
  - Thread count / weave stated inconsistently (percal vs sateen vs microfibra confused).
  - Colour photos differ from delivered colour (constant complaint).
  - No mattress-corner illustration showing elastic all-round vs. corners-only.
  - Sizes labelled in width only ("135") without stating length.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **All-round elastic** + **30 cm deep pocket** as the headline claim, illustrated with a labelled diagram.
  - Publish real fabric composition (100% cotton percal, 200 threads) and pre-washed status.
  - 2-pack in a single colour (customer buys two of the same) — this is the pain point solve.
  - Muted palette only: white, natural, greige, deep green, indigo.
  - Care-symbol label in Spanish sewn into the seam.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Two SKUs at launch: 135×190 cm and 150×190 cm, each as a **2-pack same colour** at €29.90. Add 90×190 (bunk/single) later once BSR is stable. Do **not** launch a full *juego* — that competes with brands.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (size complexity — 3 mattress widths × 4 colours could balloon; keep it small at launch)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (big brands present, but at the "juego" slice, not the 2-pack bajera niche)
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10 (SKU count discipline is the risk lever)
  - **Total: 34/50**
- **Decision:** **Further Research** — confirm review-count distribution in the "bajera 2-pack" long tail vs. "juego 3 piezas" using Helium 10 Cerebro before committing.

### 4) Linen-Look Wipe-Clean Tablecloth — rectangular 140×200 cm

- **Spanish keyword:** *mantel antimanchas*, *mantel lino efecto lino*, *mantel resinado rectangular*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de mesa › Manteles
- **Why worth researching:** Spanish households eat at the table daily; the anti-stain (*antimanchas*) tablecloth in a linen or linen-look weave is a repeat purchase (1–2× a year, colour swap for spring/autumn). Category is dominated by two visual clichés — heavy printed florals and glossy PVC — leaving a clean private-label gap for a **matte, muted, linen-look** anti-stain fabric that photographs well and fits modern Spanish dining rooms.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round with lifts at September ("vuelta a casa"), December (Navidad), and Easter. Non-collapse seasonality.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Price band **€18–€35** for 140×200 cm. Top BSR mixes generic brands and Spanish textile houses; review counts on incumbents 200–2,500 → realistic. Some very cheap PVC listings drag the average price down but do **not** compete on aesthetic.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Photography with saturated flower prints — no muted / neutral options.
  - "Antimanchas" claimed without any process description (Teflon? resin? wax?).
  - Colours between photo and delivery differ (dominant complaint).
  - Sizes only in one common length (200 cm); no 240 cm option for larger tables.
  - No hem detail photo — buyers can't tell if it's sewn or overlocked.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Muted palette only: natural, greige, salvia, terracotta, deep indigo — 5 colours max.
  - State the anti-stain finish honestly ("resinado ligero, resiste manchas de agua, vino y aceite si se limpia en <5 min") with a documented wipe test in the listing images.
  - Publish real fibre composition (poly-cotton linen-look blend, GSM stated).
  - Show hem detail close-up + a scale reference photo on a 6-seat table.
  - "Facil de guardar" pitch — folds without creasing (linen-look advantage).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one size** (140×200 cm) in 3 colours; add 140×240 cm for larger tables after 60 days. Matching *camino de mesa* (runner) as a natural follow-on SKU, not at launch.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium–High** (colour accuracy is the main return driver; anti-stain claim must be documented or it becomes a review liability)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 7/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 8/10 (higher AOV, low unit weight → good FBA ratio)
  - Risk: 5/10 (colour-return + claim risk)
  - **Total: 33/50**
- **Decision:** **Watchlist** — do not move above this unless a sample confirms colour accuracy under natural + LED light **and** the anti-stain finish survives 10 wipe tests. If both pass, promote to Further Research.

## Do Not Pursue (3 rejects with reasoning)

### A) Weighted Blanket — Manta con peso 7 kg
- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Mantas
- **Why rejected:** Heavy → high FBA fee tier, storage cost, and damage-in-transit risk; borderline "wellness / therapeutic" territory that invites unsafe medical claims; dominated by a handful of large brands (Gravity, Cura, Levia, Bedsure) that hold most reviews; return risk high (customers report it as "too heavy" or "wrong size"). Fails multiple config avoid-list criteria (very heavy, high return risk, weak margin after FBA, therapeutic-claim risk).
- **Config trip-wires hit:** heavy, high return risk, high FBA storage, brand-dominated, medical/therapeutic claim risk.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected** for Rosanube.

### B) Silk-Style Pillowcase (satin/mulberry-look) — Funda almohada satén
- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada
- **Why rejected:** Category is now saturated on Amazon.es with dozens of near-identical satin-look listings at €8–12, competing on price and colour count (often 30+ colours). Any private-label entrant needs a genuine mulberry silk certificate to charge €20+, which pulls this into fashion + certification territory outside Rosanube's simple-textile lane. Common complaints (colour bleeding onto white sheets, "not real silk") make return risk high.
- **Config trip-wires hit:** too many colour variations, weak differentiation, price-war dynamics, unsupported "silk" claim risk.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected** for Rosanube.

### C) Heated Throw Blanket — Manta eléctrica
- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Mantas eléctricas
- **Why rejected:** Electrical product → CE/EU low-voltage compliance, GPSR liability heavy, safety recall risk, plug-standard risk, warranty/return burden, and dominated by established electrical brands (Beurer, Imetec, Medisana). Fails the config's explicit "avoid electronics" rule and creates seller liability that a textile-focused private label should not carry.
- **Config trip-wires hit:** electronics, complicated certifications, safety risk, brand-dominated.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected** for Rosanube.
