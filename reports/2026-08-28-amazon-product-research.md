Selling **just the fitted sheet** (not the 3-piece juego) avoids the sizing/matching complexity that inflates SKU count. Percale 200TC is a specific, credible quality claim that positions Rosanube above the sub-€10 poly-cotton floor without competing against 400TC premium bedding brands.

  - Care label + Spanish fibre composition in cm, wash instructions in ES/PT/EN.
  - Do **not** claim *antibacterial*, *antiácaros*, or *hipoalergénico* (config compliance rule).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **1 SKU** = 150×190 cm fitted, blanco crudo. Add 135×190 cm and 90×190 cm variants in 60 days. Only add matching top sheet / pillowcases once fitted-sheet reviews stabilise — bundling the *juego completo* multiplies size×colour SKUs and blows up FBA inventory.

  - Differentiation: 7/10 (spec honesty + deep-pocket engineering)

- **Demand signals:** Steady, non-seasonal, with a Vuelta al Cole / autumn uplift as students refresh piso alquilado bathrooms. Standard sizes (50×80 cm bath mat, 40×50 cm contour) are well established.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented in the cotton-tufted sub-niche — most Amazon.es top slots on *alfombra baño* are memory-foam or microfibre; **cotton tufted 2-piece sets are thinner on page 1**. - **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Colour bleeding on first wash (frequent 1-star complaint).
  - Backing not stated (natural latex vs. TPR vs. none) → slipping complaints.

- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **1 SKU** = 2-piece set, blanco crudo. Add contour-only single (€12.90) after 60 days to capture the second bathroom / guest bathroom buyer.
