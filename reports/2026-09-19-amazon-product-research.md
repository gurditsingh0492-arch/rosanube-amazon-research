## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Throw Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Electrical product with mandatory CE/RED-adjacent certification, EPR-WEEE registration, and product-liability exposure — squarely violates config §"Products to Avoid" (electronics) and §"Products with unclear legal or safety restrictions". Also dominated by Medisana / Beurer / Imetec at the top of Amazon.es results. Poor Rosanube brand fit.

### B) Bamboo Fitted Sheet Set (*sábanas bambú*)
- **Why rejected:** "Bambú" claim is a regulatory landmine in the EU — fabric labelled as "bamboo" is generally viscose derived from bamboo and must be labelled as viscose (EU Textile Regulation 1007/2011). Making a "bambú" claim without proper documentation risks Amazon listing takedowns and consumer protection complaints. Category is also increasingly anchored by a few big private-label bedding brands. Compliance risk too high vs. upside.

### C) Memory-Foam Pillow (*almohada viscoelástica*)
- **Why rejected:** Not a textile-first product — customer decision depends on firmness / height / neck fit, generating very high return rates (memory-foam pillow returns are historically among the highest in the *dormitorio* category). Bulky polybag → high FBA fee. Category dominated by Pikolin, Emma, Sleepwise. Any "orthopédico" / "cervical" wording pushes into medical-claim risk. Weak differentiation for a private-label first launch.
