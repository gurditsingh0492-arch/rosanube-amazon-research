## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Excluded by config — contains electronics + heating element. Requires CE + LVD + EMC certification, mandatory GPSR responsible-person filing, and specific fire-safety file. Dominated by Imetec, Beurer, and Daga on Amazon.es (10k+ reviews). Very high return risk (thermostat complaints, cord failures). Not a Rosanube brand fit and clearly violates the config's "avoid electronics / batteries / complicated certifications" rule.

### B) Vacuum Compression Storage Bags for Clothing / Bedding (*bolsas de vacío almacenamiento ropa*)
- **Why rejected:** Not a textile — a plastic laminate with a valve. Failure modes are chronic (valves leak within weeks) and drive high refund rates. Category is anchored by cheap Chinese generics racing to €10–€15 for 8–12 pack sets. Requires a specific plastic pump SKU that inflates FBA complexity. Weak differentiation and off-mission for Rosanube's textile brand story.

### C) Bamboo Fitted Bed Sheet Set (*sábanas bambú*)
- **Why rejected:** "Bamboo" and "eco / hypoalergénico" claims are exactly the ones the config forbids without documented certificates (OEKO-TEX, EU eco-label, verified fibre composition), and most bamboo listings on Amazon.es are technically **bamboo viscose/rayon**, which triggers EU textile-composition labelling rules that many suppliers get wrong. Bedsheet SKUs also carry heavy size complexity (5+ Spanish sizes × colours), high return risk from colour/feel mismatch, and price pressure from established Spanish bedding brands. All four factors combined push it into NO GO territory for a first pass.
