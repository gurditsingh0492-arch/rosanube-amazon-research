### 1) Percale Cotton Fitted Sheet — Single SKU, Standard Spanish Sizes (Sábana bajera algodón percal)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera algodón*, *sábana bajera percal 135*, *sábana bajera 150 algodón 100*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** True replenishment SKU — sheets wear out, stain, and get replaced every 12–24 months. Sizes on Amazon.es are **standardised and few** (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm), which is unusual for bedding: a private label can enter with **one size + one colour** and expand only after traction, avoiding the size-explosion trap that killed sábana bajera projects in the past. Percale (not sateen, not "microfibre") is a specific enough sub-niche to escape the €7–€12 microfibre race-to-bottom.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistently in the top-20 of "Sábanas bajeras" throughout the year, with a small back-to-school / autumn refresh lift in September–October. Repeat-purchase behaviour visible in review language ("second time buying", "compré la otra vez el 90 y ahora el 135").
- **Competition signals:** Mid — dominated at the very top by a handful of Spanish/EU bedding brands (Pikolin, Naturals, Confecciones Paula, Amazon Basics) but positions 15–60 are highly fragmented private labels with variable listing quality. Price band €12–€28 for 135 cm cotton percale; microfibre versions are €7–€12 but occupy a different buyer.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fibre stated as "algodón" but composition is actually cotton-polyester blend (customer complaint driver).
  - Thread count / weave type not stated (percale vs. sateen vs. plain).
  - Elastic on all four corners vs. only top/bottom — rarely clarified; buyers can't tell from listing.
  - Deep-mattress fit (>30 cm) rarely offered; many Spanish topper/mattress combos are 28–32 cm.
  - Colours labelled inconsistently (arena / beige / crudo / natural / lino — all used interchangeably).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish **honest thread count** and weave name (100% algodón percal, 200 hilos).
  - Confirm **all-four-corner elastic** in copy + image close-up.
  - Offer a **fit-depth up to 32 cm** clearly stated in title.
  - Neutral palette only — 3 colours max (blanco, crudo, gris piedra).
  - Spanish care label, EU textile fibre label, hanging loop in the packaging insert.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 135 cm × 190/200 cm, crudo. Expand to 150 cm after 20+ reviews. Later: add a coordinated pillowcase 2-pack to pair with the 2026-07-01 pillow protector idea (upsell path). Do **not** launch a full "sábanas + funda nórdica" set on day one — the size / colour matrix explodes.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**

  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 8/10 (low return risk, single-size launch controls variation risk)

### 2) Waffle-Weave Bath Mat (Alfombra de baño waffle / panal)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombra baño waffle*, *alfombra baño panal*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The 2026-07-01 report rejected the **memory-foam** bath mat (odour, mould, race-to-bottom pricing, brand dominance). A **cotton waffle-weave** bath mat is a different sub-niche entirely — flat, quick-dry, machine-washable, and photographs premium. It rides the same "waffle" aesthetic Rosanube already leans into for kitchen + bath towels, so the same colour palette and photography setup can be reused (leverage). Thin sub-category on Amazon.es — mostly generic tufted bath mats or memory-foam.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round; bath mats are a decor + replenishment hybrid. Not seasonal. The "waffle" descriptor is growing on Amazon.es (visible in the July shortlist for kitchen + bath towels).
- **Competition signals:** Thin. Top results for *alfombra baño* are still dominated by memory-foam and chenille tufted formats; the waffle / panal cotton sub-niche has few dedicated listings. Price band €14–€28 for a cotton waffle bath mat 50×80 cm.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Absorbency doubts — same scepticism buyers have about waffle bath **towels** (waffle looks decorative → does it actually absorb?).
  - Non-slip backing absent or unreliable — most cotton waffle bath mats are just flat cotton, sliding on tile is a real complaint.
  - GSM undeclared.
  - Sizes non-standard (some 40×60, some 50×80, some 60×90) — buyers get confused.
  - Set composition unclear (mat vs. mat + hand towel).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish honest **GSM** (target 550–650 g/m² for a proper waffle bath mat).
  - Add a **silicone / latex anti-slip backing** as the documented differentiator — this is the single biggest reason buyers reject flat cotton bath mats. Make it a headline bullet.
  - Standard **50×80 cm** only at launch (matches most Spanish bathroom mat frames).
  - Palette matched to the future waffle bath towel SKU (natural, salvia, terracotta, off-white) so the two SKUs cross-sell as a bath set later.
  - Show a **water-drop absorbency shot** in listing image #3.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch one SKU × 1 size × 3 colours. Once reviews stabilise, bundle with the 2026-07-01 waffle bath towel set → a "Baño Waffle Rosanube" 3-piece kraft-box gift SKU. This is a **direct bundle partner** to a July shortlist item.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (absorbency scepticism, non-slip backing must actually work — sample QC is critical)

### 3) Microfiber Cleaning Cloth Multipack (Bayetas microfibra pack)

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas microfibra*, *paños microfibra limpieza pack*, *trapos microfibra*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y organización › Utensilios de limpieza › Bayetas y paños
- **Why worth researching:** This is the most "boring replenishment" of today's list. High-frequency consumable, standardised sizes (30×30 cm and 40×40 cm dominate), low return risk (nobody returns a microfiber cloth), tiny FBA size tier (light + compact), and Spanish households replace them every 3–6 months. Repeat-purchase behaviour is real. This is the SKU where Rosanube can **learn Amazon.es logistics and PPC** on a low-risk consumable before pushing more expensive textiles.
- **Demand signals:** Very steady daily demand — cleaning consumables don't spike. Search volume is high across multiple related keywords (*bayetas*, *paños microfibra*, *trapos limpieza*).
- **Competition signals:** High but fragmented — Vileda and Spontex anchor the top few slots for branded bayetas, but the **microfibre multipack** sub-niche is spread across dozens of private-label packs (10, 12, 24, 50). Race-to-bottom risk on **12-pack of 30×30** — Rosanube should avoid that exact configuration. Price band €8–€22 depending on pack size.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM / weight not stated → cheap thin cloths dominate but disappoint.
  - Pack composition unclear (are all colours the same use? Are they multi-purpose or "colour-coded" for kitchen / bathroom?).
  - Machine-wash cycles rarely stated (buyers complain the fibres flatten after 5 washes).
  - Packaging is loose polybag with no folded presentation — poor giftability, cheap unbox.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Colour-coded pack** (e.g. 3 kitchen / 3 bathroom / 3 glass / 3 general) with a printed insert in Spanish explaining which colour is for which surface — solves a real hygiene concern of Spanish households.
  - Publish **GSM** (target 300–350 g/m²) and **wash-cycle durability** claim only if supplier can document it.
  - Bind in a kraft cotton band → giftable to new-home-buyers, adds no unit cost.
  - Larger 40×40 cm cloths (not 30×30) — differentiates from the dominant thin-thin 30×30 packs.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single SKU = 12-piece colour-coded 40×40 pack. Do **not** launch multiple pack sizes on day one. Later: add a "kitchen edition" 6-pack that pairs with the 2026-07-01 waffle kitchen towel set at checkout.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (crowded, needs a real differentiator to escape the €8 12-pack pit)
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (unit prices are lower, so pack sizing has to earn the AOV)

### 5) Reusable / Washable Kitchen Roll (Rollo de cocina lavable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *rollo cocina lavable*, *papel cocina reutilizable*, *paños cocina reutilizables lavables*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina (crosses over to Limpieza)
- **Why worth researching:** This is an **emerging niche** on Amazon.es — a roll of 10–20 cotton or bamboo-cotton cloths that replaces disposable paper kitchen roll. Buyers are the sustainability-aware apartment household. Listings are still thin, sparsely reviewed (many <200), and the top few winners are generic Asian imports with weak Spanish copy. This is a differentiation-first, not demand-first, opportunity — the risk is real (see below) but so is the listing-quality gap.
- **Demand signals:** Growing but **not yet mass-market**. Search volume for the head keyword is modest; the buyer is intentional (they searched, they want this). Not a spike-and-crash trend — sustainability is a long trend on Amazon.es. Treat as a slower-burn brand-builder SKU, similar to the waffle bath towel in the July shortlist.
- **Competition signals:** Thin. Handful of dedicated listings, most with <500 reviews, most with generic or machine-translated Spanish. Price band €14–€26 for a roll of 10–20 cloths.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Absorbency doubts — is this really going to replace paper for spills?
  - Fibre composition unclear (some claim "bambú" without proof — compliance risk Rosanube must avoid).
  - Roll mechanic feels gimmicky — many listings show a plastic paper-roll holder that isn't included in the pack, misleading images.
  - Wash-cycle count not documented.
  - "Eco" claims made without any certificate → the exact claims config says to avoid.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Do not use "bambú"** unless supplier documents composition. Sell as **100% algodón waffle / panel** or a documented cotton/viscose blend.

  - Include a printed use-guide card in Spanish (how to wash, how to fold, wash-cycle life).
  - Do **not** include a plastic roll holder — sell as a **stack of 12 folded cloths**, 25×25 cm or 30×30 cm, in a kraft box. Removes the misleading-image problem entirely.
  - Palette matched to Rosanube's kitchen textile aesthetic → future cross-sell with waffle kitchen towels.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch 1 SKU = **12 folded waffle cloths, 30×30 cm, natural**. If traction is real, bundle with the 2026-07-01 waffle kitchen towel 6-pack → "Cocina Rosanube Sin Papel" bundle.

Config explicitly warns against products with too many sizes/colours and the associated inventory risk. Top slots are anchored by strong Spanish/EU bedding brands (Pikolin, Es-Tela, Vivaraise, Amazon Basics) with tens of thousands of reviews — review-entry barrier is very high. Also increases model photography cost (styled bedroom shots). Revisit only after the fitted sheet SKU has proven repeat-purchase and a coordinated pillowcase 2-pack has traction.

### C) Heated / Electric Throw Blanket (*manta eléctrica sofá*)
- **Why rejected:** Electronics — violates the config "avoid electronics" rule outright. High compliance burden in the EU (CE, LVD, EMC, EN 60335, GPSR), high return risk (safety perception, thermostat complaints), high FBA fee (weight + polybag size), high after-sales complaints (electrical faults, warranty claims). Dominated by established EU brands (Beurer, Medisana, Pekatherm, Imetec). Not a Rosanube brand fit — Rosanube is a textile brand, not an appliance brand. Firm NO.
