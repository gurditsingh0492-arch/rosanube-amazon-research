## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Cotton Waffle Bath Mat (60×90 cm)

1. **Product name:** Rosanube cotton waffle bath mat, 60×90 cm, absorbent, thin-profile.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Alfombras de baño.
3. **Why worth researching:** The bath-mat category on Amazon.es is dominated by two very different formats — bulky memory-foam mats (high return risk, complaints about smell/deformation, heavy FBA tier) and thick chenille shag mats (slow drying, lint). Cotton waffle sits in between: thin, quick-drying, machine-washable, aesthetic, packs flat (low FBA fee). It is a natural line extension for Rosanube's textile identity and photographs very well next to the waffle kitchen towel line already in the pipeline.
4. **Demand signals (qualitative — verify with Helium 10):** "Alfombra baño algodón" and "alfombrilla baño waffle" are stable, non-seasonal replenishment queries; bathroom refresh is a year-round Spanish purchase pattern. Bath mats are frequently re-bought (12–24 month cycle) and gifted for house-warmings.
5. **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Many small private labels (Utopia Home, generic Amazon merchants) plus some Spanish home brands. Review counts commonly 50–1,500 for waffle mats specifically; thousands for chenille/memory-foam. Price band €12–€28 for a single 60×90.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - No stated GSM or fabric weight.
   - Only one image of the product folded on white — no in-bathroom lifestyle shot.
   - Colour names in English or auto-translated ("beige" vs "arena" vs "topo" inconsistent).
   - Care instructions not in Spanish.
   - Recurring complaint: "se dobla en las esquinas / no queda plano" (edges curl) → binding quality gap.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Publish real GSM (target 550–650 g/m² waffle terry) and confirmed pre-washing.
   - Reinforced double-stitched border to fix the curling complaint.
   - Neutral Iberian palette only (arena, salvia, off-white, terracotta) — 4 colours, no primary/pastel spam.
   - Non-slip: honest positioning — "requiere alfombra antideslizante debajo" — instead of over-claiming grip.
   - Kraft belly-band pack with Spanish care card.
8. **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **60×90 cm single** in 4 neutral colours €19.90. Later add: **50×70 cm** small size €14.90 and **2-pack mixed neutrals** €34.90. Do not add memory-foam variants.
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (colour perception + edge-curl complaint are the two watch-outs).

### 2) Linen-Look Table Napkin Set — 6-pack, 40×40 cm

1. **Product name:** Rosanube linen-look cotton napkins, 40×40 cm, 6-pack.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de mesa › Servilletas de tela.
3. **Why worth researching:** Cloth napkins are re-entering Spanish households as a sustainability and dinner-party purchase (replacing paper napkins). The niche is small enough to enter with a modest review count but large enough for a 6-pack SKU to sustain sales. Small, light, high-density stack → excellent FBA economics and easy air-freight sampling. Also strongly bundle-able with placemats and table runners for a future "mesa" set.
4. **Demand signals:** "Servilletas de tela algodón" is a steady base query with predictable Q4 uplift (Navidad hosting) — not a seasonal-only product. Wedding/entertaining sub-searches keep it moving in spring.
5. **Competition signals:** Moderately fragmented. Amazon Basics has a set; a handful of small European brands (French/Portuguese-origin) sell linen or linen-blend napkins at €18–€38 for 6-pack. Not dominated. Review counts often 30–800.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - "Lino" claimed but many are 100% cotton or a low-linen blend — real fibre composition not shown.
   - Dimensions inconsistent (30×30, 40×40, 45×45 all sold as "napkins" without clarity).
   - Wrinkles heavily after wash → complaint if the listing implies "iron-free".
   - No care label picture, no Spanish care instructions.
   - Colour bleeding complaints on darker shades.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**

8. **Bundle / variation strategy:** Start with **6-pack single-colour** at €22–€26. Later: 12-pack, 6-pack mixed neutrals, and a matching table-runner cross-listing.

### 3) Reusable "Swedish" Cellulose-Cotton Dishcloth — 10-pack

1. **Product name:** Rosanube reusable cellulose-cotton dishcloth (paño esponja tipo sueco), 10-pack, 17×20 cm.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Limpieza › Bayetas y trapos (crosses to Textiles de cocina).
3. **Why worth researching:** Swedish-style dishcloths (70% cellulose / 30% cotton, absorbent, dishwasher-safe, biodegradable) sit at the intersection of "cleaning cloth" and "kitchen textile". They are a true consumable (2–6 month life), driving repeat purchase — the strongest replenishment profile in this shortlist. The category is younger on Amazon.es than in DE/UK, so the review gap for first movers is smaller.
4. **Demand signals:** Growing search interest for "bayetas suecas", "paños esponja reutilizables", "bayeta cocina biodegradable". Non-seasonal. Repeat-purchase-friendly.
5. **Competition signals:** Small number of dedicated brands (Wettex-type, some Etsy-print sellers), lots of white-label 5- and 10-packs. Prices €9–€22 for a 10-pack. Review counts often 100–2,000.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - Very literal designs (bright cartoon prints) or opposite extreme (plain white) — nothing tasteful in between.
   - Absorbency claims ("15× su peso en agua") not backed by anything.
   - Confusion about washing method (dishwasher top rack vs. washing machine vs. neither) — customers ask this in Q&A.

7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Muted, subtle print series (small salvia leaf, arena stripe, plain crudo) — 4 designs mixed in one pack.

   - Compostable inner sleeve (documented — no "eco" claim without a spec).

8. **Bundle / variation strategy:** Start with **10-pack mixed neutral prints** at €15.90–€18.90. Later: 20-pack refill, 10-pack solid neutrals, and cross-sell with waffle kitchen towel.
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (biodegradability claims must be strictly documented or dropped; otherwise a low-risk SKU).

### 5) 100% Cotton Percale Pillowcase Pair — 50×75 cm (Spanish standard)

1. **Product name:** Rosanube 100% cotton percale pillowcase pair, 50×75 cm, 200-thread-count, envelope closure.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada.
3. **Why worth researching:** Pillowcase pairs are the **entry SKU** into bedding for a private label — the SKU with the lowest FBA cost, lowest return risk, and the easiest variation tree (2 sizes × 4 colours). Whole-bedsheet sets on Amazon.es are brutally competitive (Amazon Basics, Utopia Bedding, LaMondinerie, etc.), but the standalone pillowcase pair is much more fragmented. It also lets Rosanube begin building a bedroom line and a colour language without committing to a full sheet SKU (with all its 90/135/150/160 cm variants) on launch.
4. **Demand signals:** "Funda almohada 50×75 algodón" is a steady query — Spanish standard pillow sizes (50×70 and 50×75) drive replenishment demand independent of full sheet purchases. Non-seasonal.
5. **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Amazon Basics has a percale pair, but most other listings are small brands. Prices €10–€22 for a 2-pack in cotton percale; higher for satin, lyocell, or linen. Review counts often 50–1,500.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - "Percal" claimed without stating thread count (hilos/cm²).
   - Envelope closure vs. zipper vs. buttons rarely made explicit in the title.
   - Fibre composition ambiguous — many "cotton" listings turn out to be poly-cotton (buyer complaint).
   - Colour drift after 5–10 washes — recurring complaint.
   - Size 50×70 vs. 50×75 vs. 45×110 confusion.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Publish real thread count (200 hilos/cm² minimum), fibre composition, GSM.
   - Envelope closure — stated in title.
   - Same 4-colour palette across the whole Rosanube bedroom line (crudo, arena, salvia, gris humo) so cross-buying is easy.
   - Spanish care label + fibre label sewn in (not printed on).
   - Pack in a reusable cotton drawstring bag (documented, no premium claims).
8. **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **50×75 pair** in 4 colours €18.90. Later: **50×70 pair** (same fabric) €18.90 for the other Spanish pillow standard, then a **fitted sheet + pillowcase** bundle once reviews are stable. Avoid full duvet sets on launch.
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (bedding buyers are exacting on fibre honesty and colour accuracy; return risk is manageable but higher than table napkins).

## Do Not Pursue

### A) Memory-Foam Bath Mat

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño.
- **Why rejected:** Bulky and heavy → FBA size tier and shipping cost eat the margin. Very high return / complaint rate on Amazon.es (foam smell, deformation after ~2 months, non-slip backing failing). Competition includes commodity Asian sellers racing to €9–€12. Poor fit for Rosanube's clean-textile identity, and the SKU cannibalises the cotton waffle bath mat above.

### B) Weighted Blanket ("manta con peso" / "manta lastrada")

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Mantas.
- **Why rejected:** Heavy (5–9 kg) → FBA fee tier is punishing; freight from Asia is a big share of unit cost. Category has quasi-medical claims (ansiedad, sueño) that legally require substantiation Rosanube cannot make; GPSR + product-safety file are more complex than a standard textile. Also brand-crowded (Levia, Gravity, Amazon Basics). Bad first-launch profile.

### C) Silk / Satin Pillowcase (mulberry silk claim)

- **Category:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada.
- **Why rejected:** "100% Mulberry silk 22 momme" is heavily faked on Amazon.es — buyers are trained to distrust the claim, and any listing without a real supplier-issued certificate gets punished in reviews. Real 22-momme silk has a supplier cost that pushes retail into a very competitive Ali/DHgate-parity zone. Return risk is high (hair, face-oil marks, hand feel not matching expectations). Fails the "Avoid unless proven" rule for silk-quality claims.
