## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Microfibre Cleaning Cloths — 12 or 24 pack

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas microfibra pack*, *paños microfibra limpieza*, *trapos microfibra cocina*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Productos de limpieza › Bayetas y esponjas (also indexes in Textiles de cocina)
- **Why worth researching:** True consumable — households buy 12+ per year, replacing the whole set every 6–12 months. Very low return risk, very small polybag = lowest FBA fee tier of anything on this shortlist, easy to bundle by colour code (kitchen / bath / glass / dust).
- **Demand signals (qualitative, to verify):** Consistently visible in Best Sellers under limpieza; multi-thousand-review incumbents indicate steady, non-seasonal demand.
- **Competition signals:** Anchored by Amazon Basics, AmazonCommercial and a handful of German/UK private labels. Price band €8–€18 for 12–24 packs. Fragmented long tail below rank 20 with weak listings.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM not stated (300 g/m² feels totally different from 220 g/m² but both are sold as "premium").
  - Colour-coding purpose unexplained — buyers ask "which is for what?" in Q&A.
  - Cheap polybag with no reuse value.
  - Complaints about lint transfer on glass and about smell after few washes.
  - Copy is machine-translated; some listings still use French/German words.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 300 g/m², split terry / flat-weave for glass) and machine-wash cycle count.
  - Colour-code with a printed insert card in Spanish: azul = baño, verde = cocina, amarillo = polvo, rosa = cristal.
  - Reusable cotton drawstring bag instead of loose polybag (visible quality signal, minor cost).
  - Honest "no antibacterial claim" positioning — comply with config §Compliance.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Hero SKU = 12-pack mixed (3 of each colour). Add 24-pack "family" once reviews stabilise. Later: cross-sell with waffle kitchen towel SKU from previous report.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**

- **Competition signals:** Very fragmented — no dominant brand, mostly generic imports with saturated colours and machine-translated Spanish. Price band €12–€25 for a 2-pack, €18–€30 for a 4-pack.

  - Zipper on the wrong side, visible when placed on the sofa.
  - Colour on delivery does not match photo (biggest complaint driver).
  - Fabric described as "lino" but actually 100% polyester.

  - Muted "Iberian home" palette matched to the kitchen-towel line (natural, terracotta, salvia, off-white) — coherent brand aesthetic across categories.

  - Neat kraft sleeve packaging = giftable, cheap to source.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Blackout Curtains (*cortinas opacas térmicas*)
- **Why rejected:** Enormous size variation tree (dozens of width × drop combinations must-stock to avoid returns), heavy per-unit, expensive FBA size tier, high return risk from "colour wrong / length wrong / opacity not opaque enough" complaints. Category is anchored by a handful of large curtain brands with 5-figure review counts. Violates the config's "avoid too many sizes / variations" and "avoid bulky products" rules.

### B) Electric Heated Throw Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Electrical / low-voltage product → falls under config's explicit **Products to Avoid** (electronics / batteries / dangerous-goods risk). Requires CE / LVD / EMC documentation and supplier factory audits. High return / safety complaint rates. Off-brand for Rosanube's textile-only lane. Automatic NO GO regardless of demand.

### C) Bamboo Bed Sheets (*sábanas de bambú*)
- **Why rejected:** Category is over-saturated with "100% bambú" claims that are legally viscose (rayon) under EU labelling rules — a documented enforcement risk. Fibre-composition label must be accurate under EU Textile Regulation; supplier honesty is inconsistent in this niche. High return risk on colour / fit / feel, and sheet sets have deep variation trees (5+ sizes × 5+ colours). Fails the config's compliance-simplicity and low-variation criteria.
