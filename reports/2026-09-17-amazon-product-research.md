### 1) Waterproof Mattress Protector (Fitted, Cotton Top)

1. **Product name:** Waterproof Fitted Mattress Protector, cotton-terry top, TPU membrane back — 90 / 135 / 150 / 160 / 180 cm.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Protectores de colchón.
3. **Why worth researching:** True replenishment SKU with three demand triggers — pregnancy, small children, pets — plus a rental / holiday-let segment. Amazon.es customers replace protectors after leaks, machine damage, and moves. Fits Rosanube's practical-textile positioning; no fashion risk; single colour (white) removes variation complexity; compresses tightly for FBA.
4. **Demand signals (qualitative — verify with Helium 10 / Keepa):** Steady non-seasonal demand with a mild uplift Sep–Nov as students / renters restock. Multiple listings show 4-figure review counts. Consistent Amazon.es search suggestions: *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable 150*, *funda colchón impermeable rizo*.
5. **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Pikolin, Belnou, Pikolin Home and a few Spanish bedding brands hold the top slots, but a long tail of private-label sellers ranks on niche sizes (90, 105, 135) and the "extra-deep pocket" angle. Price band €12–€28 for standard sizes.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - Waterproof membrane material not disclosed (TPU vs PVC vs PU) — buyers now specifically search for TPU.
   - Depth / pocket height rarely stated in cm, causing return complaints on thick mattresses.
   - "Silent / noiseless" claim made without evidence — reviewers complain about a plastic crinkle.
   - Photos are flat product shots on white; no bedroom context, no cut-away showing the layer construction.
   - Spanish copy machine-translated; fibre composition often missing.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Publish the exact construction: **100% cotton terry top (200 g/m²) + TPU membrane (0.03 mm) + skirt polyester elastane**, with a labelled cross-section image.
   - State pocket depth clearly (e.g. **hasta 30 cm de altura**).
   - Include a small "cómo lavar" Spanish care card inside the pack.
   - Neutral kraft outer sleeve + minimal Rosanube band — giftable for baby-shower / new-home occasions.
   - Explicit *no PVC, sin ruido plástico* honest phrasing.
8. **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one hero SKU = 150×190 cm** (Spanish standard double). Add 90×190, 135×190, 160×200, 180×200 sequentially once reviews land. Later variant: 2-pack pillow protectors (see Product 5) as a cross-listing bundle.
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (return risk mostly comes from wrong size ordered, not product quality — mitigated by a clear size chart).

### 2) Chenille Non-Slip Bath Mat (Single, 50×80 cm)

1. **Product name:** Chenille bath mat, ultra-absorbent microfiber pile, TPR non-slip backing — 50×80 cm.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño.
3. **Why worth researching:** Bath mats are a fast replacement item (mildew, wear, moving houses, gift-for-new-home). Chenille is currently the visibly rising sub-style on Amazon.es vs. traditional cotton loop mats — fluffier, dries fast, photographs well, machine washable. Small footprint, low weight → very FBA-friendly.
4. **Demand signals:** Consistent all-year demand with a modest September–October uplift (post-holiday home refresh, back-to-university flats). Search terms *alfombra baño chenilla*, *alfombra baño antideslizante*, *alfombrilla baño absorbente* all populated.
5. **Competition signals:** Very fragmented. Almost entirely private-label sellers; no clear Spanish brand owns the sub-niche. Price band €10–€22 for a single 50×80 cm mat, €18–€30 for a two-piece set (mat + contour). Review counts on top listings 500–5,000+.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - "Antideslizante" claim frequently fails — reviewers report the mat slides on tiled floors within days.
   - Backing material undisclosed (some are PVC dots, some full TPR — big quality difference).
   - Colours in photos vs. delivered often mismatched (grey looking blue, beige looking pink).
   - Machine-wash claim without stated max temperature or spin speed.
   - Photos too "flat" — no bathroom lifestyle context, no shot of the underside.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - **Full-surface TPR backing** (not sparse dots) — call it out with an underside photo.
   - Real dry-time and absorbency claim only if lab-tested; otherwise, honest wording ("seca al tacto en X min tras uso normal").
   - Muted "clean Iberian bath" palette: off-white, greige, sage, dusty terracotta — max 4 colours.
   - Machine-wash 40 °C stated on the front image.
   - Kraft belt-band packaging → giftable, low SKU cost.
8. **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Hero SKU **50×80 cm single, greige**. Add 3 more colours once reviews build. Introduce a 2-piece bundle (50×80 + 50×50 contour) as an upsell — but only after single is validated, to avoid the U-mat toilet-contour complaint pattern.

- **Why rejected today:**
  - **Bulk and weight** (6–9 kg) → very high FBA fulfilment fee tier and expensive freight from Asia. Kills margin.
  - **Health-adjacent claims** (sleep quality, anxiety relief) create regulatory / listing-compliance risk on Amazon.es.

  - Multi-size + multi-print variation tree explodes SKU count and inventory risk.
