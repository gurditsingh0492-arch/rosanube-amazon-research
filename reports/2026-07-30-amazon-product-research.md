### 1) Cotton Kitchen Apron with Adjustable Neck & Front Pockets (Adult)

- **Spanish keyword:** *delantal cocina algodón adulto*, *delantal cocina mujer hombre*, *delantal ajustable con bolsillos*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Delantales
- **Why worth researching:** Cotton adult aprons are a true replenishment + gifting SKU on Amazon.es (Reyes, Día de la Madre / del Padre, Navidad, wedding gifts, cooking-class starter kits). Fragmented listings, weak Spanish photography, and a price band that comfortably lands in Rosanube's €15–€25 sweet spot for a single unit or €25–€35 for a 2-pack "his + hers" set.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent presence in *Textiles de cocina* category top pages; steady year-round replenishment plus predictable gifting spikes (May, June, November, December); Spanish home cooks + hobby-baking segment is large.
- **Competition signals:** Very fragmented — a mix of generic Asian private-label listings and small European brands. Reviews often 50–1,500. Few strong Spanish-native brands own the "adjustable, unisex, cotton, pockets" positioning.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fabric composition often stated as "cotton" with no GSM or blend detail; complaints about thin, transparent fabric.
  - Neck strap non-adjustable → returns from taller/shorter customers.
  - Only 1 pocket or badly stitched pockets.
  - English-only titles and bullets, or machine-translated Spanish.
  - Colour photography washed out; no lifestyle shots.
  - No care label / no Spanish fibre composition label mentioned.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 220–260 g/m² cotton or 65/35 cotton-poly for durability).
  - Adjustable metal buckle at neck + long waist ties (fits ~150–195 cm).
  - Two front pockets + hidden towel loop.
  - "Clean Iberian home" palette: natural, terracotta, salvia, off-white, charcoal — not saturated primary reds.
  - Bilingual care + fibre label (Spanish first).
  - Lifestyle photos in a Spanish kitchen (tile, cast iron, wooden cutting board).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch SKU = **1 apron, neutral colour, unisex adjustable**. Later add a **2-pack "pareja / his+hers"** (€26–€32) and a **1 apron + 2 waffle kitchen towels gift bundle** (ties into the July 1 waffle-towel shortlist for cross-listing synergy).

### 2) Microfibre Dish-Drying Mat for Kitchen Counter (Absorbent, Machine-Washable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra escurridor platos microfibra*, *tapete escurridor cocina absorbente*, *alfombrilla secado vajilla*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Menaje de cocina › Escurridores → cross-listed to Textiles de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** A rising kitchen-accessory subcategory in Spain — replaces or complements plastic dish racks in small apartments. Small, lightweight, cheap to ship, easy to bundle in packs of 2. Currently dominated by look-alike no-brand listings with mediocre Spanish copy — clear white space for a private-label with sharper positioning and honest specs.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Kitchen counter organization is a durable Amazon.es sub-trend; small Spanish apartment kitchens make this a practical, non-seasonal repeat purchase (staining, discoloration, replacement every 6–12 months).
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented, mostly no-name Chinese private-label listings; review counts often 200–3,000 on top listings; typical price €9–€18 for single, €14–€25 for 2-pack. Few Spanish-native brands.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Microfibre" claimed with no fibre split (polyester/polyamide %) or GSM.
  - Photos show one size; buyers complain "smaller than expected".
  - Complaints about slipping on the counter, curling edges, mildew smell after weeks.
  - Cheap-looking colours (saturated pink/blue) — no neutral, kitchen-friendly palette.
  - Spanish copy is often machine-translated and lists English-only care instructions.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real dimensions (target 40×46 cm and 50×60 cm), real GSM, real fibre split (typical 80% polyester / 20% polyamide).
  - Anti-slip silicone backing OR clearly state "no backing — reversible" (pick a lane and be honest about it).
  - Neutral palette (natural, sage, warm grey, off-white).
  - Machine-washable at 60°C — real spec, printed on the sewn-in Spanish care label.
  - Kitchen-context lifestyle photos.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch SKU = **2-pack, 40×46 cm, neutral mix (natural + sage)** at €16.90–€19.90. Later: **1× 50×60 cm "large" single** and a **"kitchen starter" trio** with 1 mat + 2 waffle towels.

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda tabla planchar acolchada*, *funda tabla plancha universal*, *funda para tabla de planchar antiadherente*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cuidado de la ropa › Fundas de tabla de planchar
- **Why worth researching:** A quiet replenishment SKU. Ironing board covers wear out visibly (burn marks, staining, loose elastic) → forced replacement every 12–24 months. ## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Weighted Blanket / Manta Ponderada / Manta con Peso

- **Why rejected:**
  - **Bulky and heavy** (typically 5–9 kg) → high FBA fee tier, expensive to ship, damage risk in transit.
  - **Big-brand dominance** (Bedsure, Gravity, Amazon Basics) with review moats in the thousands.
  - **Implicit health/therapeutic claims** (anxiety, sleep, autism) that touch medical-claim rules Rosanube must avoid.
  - **High return risk** — customers reject weight not matching expectation.
  - **Weak brand fit** — Rosanube is clean, practical home textiles, not therapeutic sleep products.
- **Decision:** **NO GO**. Add to `rejected_products.md` if that file exists.

### B) Beach Towel / Toalla de Playa Grande

- **Why rejected:**
  - **Highly seasonal** (May–August spike, quiet the rest of the year) — cash-flow and inventory-planning nightmare for a first-year private-label.
  - **Brand dominance** — Desigual, Benetton, Disney/licensed, plus IP-heavy football club merchandise. High IP risk.
  - **Race to the bottom** on generic microfibre beach towels (€7–€12).
  - **Bulky** and heavy → FBA fee erodes margin.
  - **Print-heavy** category → needs expensive artwork / model photography that Rosanube's neutral aesthetic doesn't fit.
- **Decision:** **NO GO** (revisit only with a clearly differentiated Turkish-cotton or peshtemal-crossover angle, tied to the July 1 peshtemal shortlist).

### C) Heated / Electric Throw Blanket / Manta Eléctrica

- **Why rejected:**
  - **Electronics + textile hybrid** — falls outside the Rosanube textile lane and outside the "avoid electronics" rule.
  - **Certification complexity**: CE, LVD, EMC, GPSR — significantly more compliance work than pure textile.
  - **Return + safety risk** (wiring faults, short circuits, complaints about uneven heating).
  - **Category dominated** by Imetec, Beurer, Medisana, Beper — strong brand moat.
  - **Seasonal** (October–February peak) → the same cash-flow risk as beach towels, inverted.
- **Decision:** **NO GO** — hard exclusion per config's "avoid electronics".
