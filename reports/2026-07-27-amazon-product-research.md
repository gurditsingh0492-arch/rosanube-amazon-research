### 2) Microfiber Cleaning Cloth Pack — 12-pack, colour-coded

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas microfibra pack*, *paños microfibra limpieza*, *trapos microfibra 12 unidades*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y organización › Trapos y bayetas
- **Why worth researching:** A pure "boring high-repeat" replenishment SKU. Very low return risk, tiny FBA footprint, unaffected by seasonality, unaffected by fashion. Ideal SKU to test Rosanube's Amazon.es listing / PPC / freight / EPR-Spain plumbing without exposure to colour or aesthetic returns. Weakness: low unit price → pack economics must be tight.
- **Demand signals:** Very steady daily demand. Category is essentially a kitchen/bathroom consumable — customers buy multi-packs 1–3× per year.
- **Competition signals:** Heavily fragmented. Amazon Basics + Vileda / Spontex + dozens of generic multi-packs share the top. Common price band **€8–€16** for 10–12 packs — margin discipline required.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM (fabric weight) rarely stated; buyers can't distinguish thin from premium.
  - Complaints about lint on glass, colour bleeding, chemical smell, static after 2–3 washes.
  - Colour choice is decorative rather than functional (all same colour → confusion about which is for the toilet).
  - Plastic polybag packaging with no Spanish care label — bad EPR-Spain signal and easy to beat.
  - Poor Spanish copy; often mistranslated from Chinese seller templates.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Documented GSM (target **280–320 g/m²**) and edgeless / laser-cut border for lint-free glass work.
  - **Functional colour coding** (4 colours × 3 units each): Yellow = cocina, Blue = baño, Green = cristales, Grey = uso general — printed key on the packaging.
  - Recyclable kraft sleeve (no plastic) — supports EPR-Spain narrative and photographs premium next to competitors' plastic bags.
  - Spanish-language care label + composition; explicit "no usar suavizante" instruction.
  - Honest "reusable up to 300 washes" claim only if supplier tests support it.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch 12-pack colour-coded as hero. Add a 6-pack "starter" (€8.90) and an add-on 4-pack of edgeless glass cloths later. Do **not** launch 24-pack until repeat-purchase behaviour is proven.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (return risk); **Medium** (margin risk from low unit price → all-in cost must be ≤€3 landed for 12 units).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (crowded but weak individual listings)
  - Differentiation: 6/10 (functional colour coding + kraft packaging is defensible but not exclusive)
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (tight — depends on supplier price)
