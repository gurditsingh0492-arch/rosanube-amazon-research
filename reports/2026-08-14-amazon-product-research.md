   - Cushion covers ship flat and light, so FBA fees stay small and margins hold up at €18–€26.

8. **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:**
   - Launch **one hero SKU**: 2-pack mixed neutrals (1× natural + 1× greige), €22.90.

### 2) Cotton Bath Mat with Anti-Slip Backing — 50×80 cm

1. **Product name:** Rosanube 100 % cotton tufted bath mat, 50×80 cm, latex anti-slip dot backing.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño.
3. **Why this product is worth researching:**
   - Bath mats are replenishment (users replace after ~12–18 months, or when they visibly grey).
   - The Amazon.es top of category is split between synthetic memory-foam mats and cheap microfibre — a **quality cotton tufted mat at €16–€22** is genuinely underserved, and it fits Rosanube's "clean, comfortable, reliable" positioning.
   - It ships folded flat, staying inside FBA "Standard-oversize (light)" tier — margin remains usable.
4. **Demand signals (qualitative):**
   - "Alfombra de baño algodón" and "alfombrilla baño algodón" consistently return listings with 500–3,000 reviews across several sellers → steady demand.
   - Seasonally flat; slight uplift during "vuelta al cole" / apartment moves (Sep) and post-Reyes bathroom refreshes (Jan).
5. **Competition signals:**
   - Top slots dominated by synthetic memory-foam (a different product) and Amazon-basics-style microfibre.
   - Cotton segment is fragmented; few sellers publish weight/GSM or absorbency data.
   - Price band €12–€24 for 50×80 cm, €18–€32 for 60×90 cm.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - Latex backing peels or "melts" after 5–10 washes (repeated complaint).
   - Mat slides on tile even with anti-slip claim.
   - Sheds cotton lint for weeks.
   - Colour fades quickly.
   - No published GSM / pile height.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Publish real weight (target ≥ 1,400 g/m² tufted cotton) and pile height (≥ 18 mm).
   - **PVC-free anti-slip dot backing** (documented, not claimed) — supplier must certify.
   - Pre-washed to reduce initial linting; state it explicitly.
   - 3 colours only: off-white, greige, dusty rose.
   - Care instructions in Spanish, warning against high-heat drying (main cause of latex peeling).
8. **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:**
   - Start with a **single-mat SKU** 50×80 cm greige at €19.90.
   - Add a 2-pack (small 40×60 + main 50×80) as a "juego baño" at €29.90 once reviews stabilise.
   - Avoid 3-piece toilet-mat contour sets (variation and return complexity).
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (return risk if backing fails, so supplier QA is critical).
10. **Score /50:**
    - Demand: 8/10
    - Competition: 6/10
    - Differentiation: 7/10
    - Margin potential: 6/10
    - Risk: 6/10

## Do Not Pursue — 3 Products Rejected Today

### A) Weighted Blanket (Manta con Peso / Manta Sensorial)
- **Category:** Hogar › Ropa de cama › Mantas.
- **Why rejected:**
  - Very heavy (5–10 kg) → high FBA fulfilment fee and storage cost, kills margin at the €40–€70 price band buyers expect.
  - High return risk (weight preference is very personal, sizing sensitive to the user's body weight).
  - Frequent listings make **therapeutic / anti-anxiety / autism** claims — the config forbids these unless documented, and the safer positioning strips the product of its unique buying trigger.
  - Falls outside the "lightweight or medium-weight, easy to ship" ideal-profile clause.

### B) Fitted Bed Sheet, Single SKU (Sábana Bajera Ajustable)
- **Category:** Hogar › Ropa de cama › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras.
- **Why rejected:**
  - Enormous variation complexity — Spanish beds run 80, 90, 105, 135, 150, 160, 180, 200 cm width, plus mattress depth variations. Requires 10+ SKUs just to be competitive.
  - Dominated by big EU home-textile brands (Pikolin, El Corte Inglés own-brand, Amazon Basics) at aggressive price bands.
  - Colour-fading and pilling complaints are chronic → high return risk.
  - Not a first private-label launch. Only revisit as a **line extension** once other bedroom SKUs are established.

### C) Adult Terry Bathrobe (Albornoz Rizo Adulto)
- **Category:** Hogar › Baño › Albornoces.
- **Why rejected:**
  - Sizing risk (S/M/L/XL/XXL × unisex vs men vs women fit) violates the "low-size-complexity" rule.
  - Bulky, heavy → oversized-FBA fees eat margin.
  - Return rate on apparel-adjacent items is materially higher than flat textiles.
  - Dominated by hotel-supply brands and a few premium spa brands on Amazon.es; the mid-market slot is a race-to-the-bottom.
  - Requires model photography (also forbidden by the config unless justified).

## Common Compliance Notes (apply to all 5)

- EU textile fibre composition label — required, in Spanish (plus Portuguese/English acceptable additions).
- Care label with washing symbols — required.
- Country of origin — required on invoice and product label.
- GPSR: EU responsible person — Rosanube's Portuguese entity likely qualifies but must be confirmed by an EU compliance check.
- EPR packaging registration in Spain — required for any packaging placed on the Spanish market.
- OEKO-TEX, organic, hypoallergenic, antibacterial, medical, therapeutic, orthopedic, flame-resistant, chemical-free, eco-friendly — **do not use in any listing** unless a valid supplier certificate is on file (config §Compliance).

- All five SKUs ship flat / small-parcel — no oversized-tier exposure except the bath mat, which may sit at "Standard-oversize (light)" and needs an FBA Calculator check before pricing.
