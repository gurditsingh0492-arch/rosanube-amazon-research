Category has a lot of low-quality Chinese white-label listings with weak Spanish copy; branded EU alternatives (e.g. big linen houses) sit at €35+, leaving a €15–€25 gap.
- **Demand signals:** High and stable — bathroom textiles are one of the most consistent replenishment sub-categories on Amazon.es. Top ASINs typically show 4-figure review counts.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### R1) Weighted Blanket (Manta con Peso)

- **Spanish keyword:** *manta con peso adulto*, *manta pesada ansiedad*
- **Why rejected:**
  - **Heavy + bulky** → oversized FBA fee tier destroys margin at the €35–€60 selling point.
  - **High return risk** — buyers order the wrong weight (5 / 7 / 9 kg), sensory expectations disappoint, textiles-with-fill are hard for Amazon FBA to repackage → losses on returns.
  - **Compliance grey zone** — many listings imply therapeutic / anxiety / sensory benefits, which is exactly the type of unverified claim `research_config.md` bans.
  - **Brand risk** — a small number of larger EU / US brands (Levia-style, Gravity-style) dominate the "premium" tier; race-to-bottom private labels dominate the €35 tier.
  - **Poor Rosanube fit** — not a "clean, practical, everyday" home textile; more of a wellness gimmick.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected.** Add to `rejected_products.md`.

### R2) Silk Pillowcase (Funda de Almohada de Seda 100%)

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda almohada seda 22 momme*, *funda almohada seda pelo piel*
- **Why rejected:**
  - **Claim-heavy category** — buyers expect "anti-frizz", "anti-aging", "hair protection", "hypoallergenic". All of these are the exact claims `research_config.md` says not to make without documentation.
  - **Momme-weight fraud risk** — the market is saturated with listings claiming "22 momme" or "25 momme" silk that is actually satin polyester; a real 22-momme mulberry silk case has a landed cost near €12–€18, and Rosanube would need OEKO-TEX / mulberry origin documents to differentiate credibly.
  - **Brand-dominated** — Blissy, Slip, and a handful of Amazon private labels with 10k+ reviews own the top-of-search. Review-barrier for a new SKU is punishing.
  - **High return risk** — colour and "silky feel" perception varies; polyester-satin substitutes flood the category and depress trust.
  - **Poor Rosanube fit** at launch stage — better fit for a Phase 3 bedroom line once the brand has trust.
- **Decision:** **NO GO for 2026.** Revisit only if a verified OEKO-TEX / mulberry silk supplier appears in the pipeline.

### R3) Printed Beach Towel with Licensed Character / Football Club Design

- **Spanish keyword:** *toalla playa Real Madrid*, *toalla playa Disney niño*, *toalla licencia oficial*
- **Why rejected:**
  - **IP / licensing risk** — licensed characters and club crests require formal licensing agreements. Selling without a licence is a rapid path to Amazon suspension and legal exposure. `research_config.md` explicitly excludes "Licensed character products unless legally licensed".
  - **Seasonality** — beach towels concentrate demand in May–August; storage cost in low season eats margin.
  - **Brand dominance** — official-licence sellers (CTI, Cerdá, etc.) already occupy the shelf; a private label without a licence cannot compete on the licensed keyword.
  - **Return risk** — colour, print alignment, and "feels rougher than in the photo" are frequent review complaints.
  - **Poor Rosanube fit** — noisy, printed, kid-oriented product profile clashes with the calm neutral brand identity.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected.** Add to `rejected_products.md`.
