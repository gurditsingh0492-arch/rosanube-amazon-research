### 1) Waterproof Fitted Mattress Protector (Rizo / Terry-back)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable ajustable*, *funda colchón rizo impermeable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** A boring, high-repeat, high-necessity SKU. Every household with a child, a pet, or a mattress warranty is a customer. Sizes on Amazon.es are standardised around the six Spanish mattress widths (80 / 90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 180 × 190/200 cm), and buyers rarely return correct-size deliveries. Sits inside Rosanube's core bedroom textile lane and pairs later with duvet covers and pillow protectors.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent daily demand year-round; visible uplift around university housing intake (Sep) and Black Friday (Nov). Top listings routinely carry 4-figure review counts, confirming volume.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — a mix of Spanish/EU bedding brands (Pikolin, Belnou, Naturals) at the top, but slots 5–30 are private-label with wide-open review gaps. Common price band **€12–€28** for a single-size fitted protector.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Impermeable" claimed but backing is a noisy TPU film → complaint driver.
  - Sizing errors (advertise 90×190, ship 90×200) — recurring 1-star reviews.
  - Marketing claims *antiácaros* / *hipoalergénico* / *antibacteriano* without any certificate on the listing → both a customer trust and a Rosanube-compliance red flag.
  - Weak Spanish copy; sizes listed in imperial or with ambiguous "cama de 135".
  - Elastic corners give out after 6–10 washes.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish a real construction spec: cotton rizo (terry) top + breathable TPU backing (documented weight in gsm).
  - Reinforced 360° all-elastic skirt, deep pocket labelled in cm.
  - Sizes and depths in cm + Spanish mattress-language ("cama 90", "cama 135") on both listing and packaging.
  - Do **not** claim antiácaros / hypoallergenic / antibacterial. Stick to "impermeable, transpirable, ajustable" — verifiable and defensible.
  - Neat Spanish care label + 2-year seam warranty statement.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one hero SKU** = 150×190/200 cm (most common Spanish double) in white. Second wave: add 90 and 135 cm variants + a 2-pack in 90 cm (kids' beds).
