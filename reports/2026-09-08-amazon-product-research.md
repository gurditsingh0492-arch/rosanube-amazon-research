## Executive Summary

Today's five picks stay firmly inside Rosanube's core lane — bedroom, dining, laundry, and bathroom textile essentials — and share these traits:

- **Steady, non-seasonal replenishment demand** (bedding protection, table linen, laundry accessories are used year-round, with a mild autumn/Christmas uplift for table linen).
- **Fragmented Amazon.es competition** — a long tail of small private-label sellers, few dominant EU brands in the target sub-niches, weak listings that Rosanube can out-execute.
- **Preferred price band €12–€35** and simple size/colour variation trees (max 3 sizes × 4 muted colours) → low SKU complexity.
- **Low compliance friction** — standard EU textile fibre labelling + care label + GPSR responsible person + EPR Spain packaging. No hypoallergenic / antibacterial / organic / medical claims used without documented certificates.
- **Manageable FBA economics** — all five are lightweight or small, low breakage risk, low return risk, no dangerous goods.

Season note: 8 September sits at the start of the Spanish "back-to-home" season (school restart, kitchen refresh, autumn duvet swap) — a reasonable moment to line up bed and table SKUs for late-October / November launch, but only after supplier and FBA numbers are verified.

None of the five below are recommended for a purchase order today. All are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist** pending Helium 10, Keepa, and supplier quotes.

## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Zippered Pillow Protector — 2-pack (50×75 cm and 50×80 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda de almohada con cremallera*, *protector de almohada impermeable*, *funda protectora almohada 50x75*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada › Protectores de almohada
- **Why worth researching:** A protector under the decorative pillowcase is a true replenishment SKU (worn out, stained, replaced) and a common "just add one more" bedroom purchase. It sits under the main pillowcase, so buyers care about fit, silence (no crinkling), and the zip — not about print or fashion — which is exactly Rosanube's competence zone. 50×75 and 50×80 are the two dominant Spanish pillow sizes, so the variation tree stays tight.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent presence in Ropa de cama sub-categories; frequent inclusion in "compraron también" bundles alongside sheet sets and mattress protectors. Repeat-purchase behaviour visible in review language ("compré otro par").
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — Pikolin, Velfont, and a handful of Spanish bedding brands hold visibility at the top, but the mid-tail is small private labels with 100–1,500 reviews. Price band **€12–€22 for a 2-pack** in cotton; €18–€30 in cotton + PU waterproof membrane.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fibre composition in English only ("100% cotton" without *"100 % algodón"*).
  - "Antiácaros" / "hipoalergénico" claims with **no** OEKO-TEX or lab certificate shown → review complaints and returns.
  - Zip described as "silent" but customers report noise and snagging.
  - Photos of the pillow *inside* the cover — nobody shows the zip close-up or the seam finish.
  - Sizing confusion between 50×70 (French), 50×75, and 50×80 (Spanish standard).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Cotton percale shell, **plain** — no membrane claim unless a documented PU laminate and OEKO-TEX certificate are on file.
  - Publish real fabric weight (target 120–140 g/m² percale) and thread count in the listing.
  - YKK or equivalent branded zip with a fabric guard flap; close-up macro photo of the zip and seam.
  - Sizes clearly labelled *"Estándar español 50×75"* and *"Almohada larga 50×80"*.
  - Kraft sleeve packaging with Spanish/Portuguese/English care label.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Start with **one SKU** = 2-pack, 50×75, natural white. Add 50×80 as a second SKU once reviews stabilise. Later, a "cama de matrimonio" 4-pack at €24.90.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (main risk: buyer expectation on "antiácaros" claim we will *not* make).

  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10

### 2) Rectangular Stain-Resistant Tablecloth — 140×240 cm (6–8 seats)

- **Spanish keyword:** *mantel rectangular antimanchas*, *mantel resistente manchas 140x240*, *mantel mesa 6 personas*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Cocina y mesa › Manteles
- **Why worth researching:** The 140×240 cm rectangular tablecloth fits the standard Spanish 6–8 seat dining table — the size Amazon.es buyers search most in this format. Autumn (October–December) is the strongest window: back to indoor dinners, All Saints, Navidad, Reyes. Stain-resistant (Teflon / hydrofuge treatment) is now the customer-expected default, but many listings do **not** explain the treatment or wash care clearly.
- **Demand signals:** Seasonal uplift Oct–Jan, but with a steady year-round base. Reviews frequently mention purchase for "regalo de Navidad" and "mesa Nochebuena" — giftable angle is real.
- **Competition signals:** Moderate. A few strong sellers dominate the top (Vinilos Casa, Amazon Basics private label, La Mallorquina, Es-Tela), but the mid-tail is fragmented; review counts in the 200–2,000 range for private labels. Price band **€15–€28** for a plain-colour 140×240; €20–€35 for jacquard.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Antimanchas" claim without saying whether it is a coating or the weave.
  - Sizing shown only in the title; buyers unsure which side is 140 vs 240.
  - Ironing/washing instructions absent → return risk after first wash.
  - Colours off vs. the photos (a common review complaint).
  - Wrinkles straight out of the packet, no pre-pressing.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Named** finish: state that stain-resistance is a fluorine-free hydrofuge treatment (verified on supplier TDS) and give the wash-cycle tolerance (e.g. 40 °C, no bleach). If not fluorine-free, do not claim it.
  - 3 muted "Iberian home" colours only: crudo (natural), salvia, terracotta suave. No busy prints.
  - Corner-mitred hem and reinforced seam; photograph the hem detail.
  - Table diagram in the A+ content showing 140×240 on a 6-seat vs 8-seat table.
  - Recyclable kraft belly-band with folding pattern to reduce first-wrinkle complaints.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch 140×240 cm × 3 colours. Add matching napkin 4-pack (40×40) as a related SKU (see #5) rather than as a bundle at first — avoids Amazon variation confusion.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (colour accuracy risk, seasonal spike risk if launched too late).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 34/50**

### 3) Mesh Laundry Wash Bag Set — 5- or 7-pack (mixed sizes)

- **Spanish keyword:** *bolsas para lavadora ropa delicada*, *bolsa lavado sujetadores*, *bolsas malla colada set*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Lavandería › Bolsas para colada / Accesorios de lavandería
- **Why worth researching:** Small, light, low-cost per unit → **excellent FBA economics** (small-envelope size tier likely). Genuine replenishment demand (bags tear, get lost, are gifted to first-apartment renters). Fits Rosanube's "practical home textile essentials" positioning without needing photography talent — a set laid flat with tags visible is enough.
- **Demand signals:** Non-seasonal. Steady visibility in Lavandería sub-category, boosted by "wash bras / delicates / knitwear" search intent that grows in autumn.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented and mostly generic Chinese-imported sets. Review counts wide (50–5,000) but ratings often only 4.0–4.3 — real quality complaints. Price band **€8–€18 for a 5–7 pack**. Config's "avoid under €10 unless pack economics excellent" — this is a legitimate exception because supplier cost is very low and pack size can be tuned to hit €13.90.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Zip pull tucks are missing → zip opens in the wash, ruins the point of the bag.
  - Mesh is too coarse (delicates snag) or too fine (detergent doesn't circulate).
  - No suggested use per bag size ("what size for a bra? for a jumper?").
  - Cheap plastic zip head that rusts after 5 washes.
  - Poor packaging — arrives as a crumpled polybag.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Corrosion-resistant zip** with an internal tuck flap that clips shut → tested-to-30-washes claim (only if documented on a real sample test).
  - Sew-in Spanish label per bag with the recommended use ("Sujetadores 15×20", "Jerséis 30×40", "Sábanas 50×70").
  - Two mesh grades: fine mesh for lingerie, medium mesh for knitwear — coloured piping to distinguish at a glance.
  - Muted colours (grey, off-white) instead of loud pastels — matches a Rosanube-branded laundry room.
  - Neat recyclable kraft box, not a polybag → the giftable "apartment starter" angle.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 7-pack mixed (2× fine small, 2× fine medium, 2× medium large, 1× extra-large for duvets), €13.90. Add a slim 3-pack lingerie-only edition at €8.90 later, but only if the 7-pack proves unit economics on FBA.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** — low FBA fee, low return risk, low compliance load.

  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 6/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (only if landed cost is genuinely low; verify)

### 4) Cotton Bath Mat / Bath Rug — 50×80 cm (non-plastic backing)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra de baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño rizo*, *alfombra baño antideslizante algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombrillas de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The cotton (or cotton + latex-dot-back) bath mat is a mid-price replenishment item that ties naturally to Rosanube's bath-towel line. 50×80 is the highest-volume size on Amazon.es. The category is dominated at the cheap end by thin microfibre mats (weak product) and at the top by hotel-supply brands — the middle-ground "thick cotton rizo, honest GSM, non-slip backing" position is undefended.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round; small uplift Oct–Nov (bathroom refresh) and Jan (post-Reyes replacements).
- **Competition signals:** Moderate. A few visible sellers (Möve, Vossen, Bianca, Amazon Basics), but the mid-tail is a mix of generic imports. Price band **€12–€25** for cotton 50×80.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Alfombra antideslizante" without saying **how** it is non-slip (latex dots? rubber backing? nothing?).
  - GSM/weight not stated → thin, disappointing product.
  - Complaints about lint shedding for the first 5 washes.
  - Colour bleeding onto light-coloured tile grout (verified in reviews).
  - Photography on hotel-style marble that does not match Spanish bathroom reality.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Cotton rizo, target 1,600–1,900 g/m² total weight for a plush feel — publish the number.
  - Backing: silicone/latex dot grid **specified on the listing** (grid density in dots/dm²).
  - Pre-washed to reduce first-wash lint; state it.
  - 3 colours only: crudo, gris piedra, azul niebla — no bright colours (bleeding risk).
  - Bathroom-context lifestyle photo on Spanish/Portuguese tile.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single SKU 50×80 to start. Add a matching contour mat (WC surround) later only if the first SKU proves margin.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (colour-bleed and backing complaints are the top return drivers in this category — needs a good sample before scaling).

## Do Not Pursue (rejected today)

### A) Weighted Blanket / Manta de peso (5–9 kg)

- **Why rejected:** Bulky, heavy → high FBA fulfilment fee tier destroys margin at Amazon.es price bands. High return risk (weight preference is very personal). Frequent listing claims about "reduce ansiedad / mejora el sueño" cross into medical/therapeutic territory the config explicitly forbids without documented evidence. Dominated by 4–5 brands (Gravity, Layla, Levia, generic Amazon Basics). **Verdict:** NO GO for Rosanube 2026 lineup.

### B) Silk Pillowcase / Funda almohada seda (mulberry silk)

- **Why rejected:** Dominated by established beauty-adjacent brands (Slip, Blissy, Amazon-native mid brands) with heavy PPC. Buyer expectations are very high (real 22 momme mulberry, hand-rolled hem, OEKO-TEX). A cheaper "silk feel / satin" substitute triggers refund waves. Compliance & marketing-claim risk (mulberry / momme count / hypoallergenic) is exactly the kind Rosanube's config says to avoid without documented proof. **Verdict:** NO GO — brand-dominated + high customer-complaint risk.

### C) Baby Muslin Swaddle Blanket / Muselinas bebé pack

- **Why rejected:** Baby category on Amazon.es carries the heaviest compliance load — EU GPSR responsible person, product safety file, EN 71 (if positioned as toy-adjacent), possible OEKO-TEX baby (STeP) expected by buyers, strict labelling. Category dominated by Aden+Anais, Tommee Tippee, ByBoom, Motherhood. Return/complaint risk on baby products is materially higher. Outside Rosanube's stated "home textile essentials" positioning. **Verdict:** NO GO — compliance load + brand dominance + off-brief.
