## Executive Summary

Today's five candidates all sit inside Rosanube's textile core (bath, kitchen, table, living-room). They share:

- **Steady, non-seasonal demand.** All are replenishment or decorative essentials, not fashion trends.
- **Fragmented competition on Amazon.es.** No single brand dominates; many small private labels with mediocre listings.
- **Clear, executable differentiation levers.** Honest GSM/fibre disclosure, Spanish-first copy, muted "clean Iberian home" palette, kraft-band packaging, cross-sell with previous shortlist SKUs.

### 2) Cotton Cushion Cover Pack (Fundas de cojín 45×45, 2-pack, textured)

- **Spanish keyword:** *fundas de cojín 45x45*, *fundas cojines algodón 2 unidades*, *fundas cojín decorativas sofá*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Salón › Cojines y accesorios › Fundas de cojín
- **Why worth researching:** Cushion covers are a decorative, high-repeat, low-return-risk textile with **simple sizing** (45×45 is the Spanish default; 50×50 and 30×50 are secondary). They photograph well, extend Rosanube's home-textile brand into the living room without needing model photography, and stack well with the future table-linen line.
- **Demand signals:** Steady, non-seasonal, with a decoration-refresh uplift in spring and early autumn; recurrent gifting for housewarmings.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented but crowded. Top pages mix Amazon Basics + many small private-label design brands + Etsy-style aesthetic sellers. Price band €10–€22 for 2-packs; €18–€35 for 4-packs. Review counts range widely (50 → 3,000).
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Composition claimed as "algodón" but delivered as polyester or mixed — top complaint driver.
  - Zippers described as "invisible" but visibly cheap in photos.
  - Pack composition unclear (2 identical vs. 2 different vs. 2 same-colour different-texture).
  - Sizes described only in cm without noting whether it fits a 45 or 50 cushion insert.
  - No care label / no fibre composition disclosed at listing level.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish **exact composition** (e.g. "100 % algodón, tejido panamá 220 g/m²") and hidden YKK-style zip.

  - Explicit sizing note: "para relleno 45×45 o 50×50 cm (funda 45×45)".
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **1 SKU** = 2-pack, 45×45, hero colourway. Add 50×50 and 30×50 lumbar as separate SKUs after review traction. Future bundle: 2 cushion covers + 1 table runner + 4 napkins = "Salón + Mesa Rosanube" gift box.

### 3) Microfibre Cleaning Cloth Pack (Bayetas de microfibra, 12–24 uds, colour-coded)

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas microfibra pack*, *paños microfibra limpieza casa*, *bayetas microfibra cocina baño*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza del hogar y menaje › Trapos y paños de limpieza (also indexes into Textiles de cocina for kitchen sub-use)
- **Why worth researching:** A **true replenishment** SKU — customers reorder every 3–6 months. Extremely simple to source, tiny packed volume → excellent FBA economics, low return risk. Fits the "practical textile essential" line in the config. A colour-coded pack (kitchen / bath / glass / dust) is a small-but-real listing angle that most Amazon.es packs do not execute clearly.
- **Demand signals:** Very steady, year-round; large search volume on the generic term; buyers often buy in multi-packs.
- **Competition signals:** Crowded and price-competitive. Amazon Basics, Vileda, and several generic Chinese brands anchor the top; price race down to €7–€12 for 10–20 packs. This is the biggest concern.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Pack colours random / not tied to a use (so the "colour-coded" benefit is lost).
  - GSM not stated; some 200 g microfibre sold next to 300 g microfibre with no signal to the buyer.
  - Complaints about linting, chemical smell out of the polybag, and cloths going stiff after few washes.
  - Poor packaging (thin polybag) → looks cheap out of the box, hurts brand perception.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Colour-coded by room** with a printed Spanish legend on the packaging ("Azul = cristales, Verde = baño, Amarillo = cocina, Rosa = polvo").

  - Small, reusable kraft/cotton pouch instead of polybag → visible upgrade for +€0.30 COGS.
  - Care label + Spanish washing instructions ("hasta 60 °C, sin suavizante").
  - Anchor at €12.90 for 12 uds, positioned above the €7 commodity zone but below Vileda pricing.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **12-unit pack** as the hero SKU. Later add a 24-unit "casa completa" pack. Cross-sell with the waffle kitchen towel SKU as a "Kit Limpieza Cocina".
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** — margin is the biggest question given commodity pricing pressure; volume and repeat-purchase behaviour can compensate, but only if COGS is low and PPC is disciplined.

  - Competition: 4/10 (heavy price pressure)

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Fitted Bed Sheet (*sábana bajera ajustable*)
- **Why rejected:** Sizing complexity is a killer for a first launch — Spanish market needs 90, 105, 135, 150, and 180 cm bases, each in multiple depths (25 / 30 / 35 cm). SKU proliferation exceeds config rules ("avoid products with too many sizes, colours, or variations"). Additionally the category is anchored by strong incumbents (Pikolin, Belnou, Amazon Basics) with 10k+ reviews and aggressive price positioning. High return risk from size-mismatch (buyers order the wrong base size and blame the seller). Better to revisit as pillowcase-first expansion once the pillow protector SKU (from 2026-07-01) stabilises.

### B) Muslin Baby Swaddle Blanket (*muselina bebé, mantita muselina*)
- **Why rejected:** Baby textiles carry heavier compliance load (GPSR + REACH scrutiny for baby-skin-contact items + strong buyer expectation of OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I). Per config: "do not use the claim" unless certificate is documented — meaning we would be forced to compete without the exact claim buyers filter on. High return risk (parents return anything that "smells", "sheds", or is "not soft enough"). Also crosses out of Rosanube's stated adult-home-textile brand fit. Revisit only if a certified Portuguese/Turkish supplier is found and Gurdit explicitly opens the baby lane.

### C) Fabric Shower Curtain (*cortina de baño de tela*)
- **Why rejected:** Two structural issues. **Returns**: buyers complain heavily about mildew, waterproofing failure, and eyelet tearing — return rate on this category is materially higher than towels or mats. **Category economics**: Amazon.es page is anchored by cheap PEVA/polyester curtains at €10–€15 and by a few premium brands at €35+, leaving a squeezed middle where Rosanube would need to compete. Also crosses adjacent to hardware (hooks, rings) which pushes SKU complexity. Better to keep bathroom exposure inside the towel + mat line where Rosanube already has a plan.
