## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Fitted Bed Sheet — *sábana bajera ajustable*
- **Why rejected:** Size complexity is extreme on Amazon.es — 90, 105, 135, 150, 160, 180 cm width × 190/200 cm length, plus different mattress depths (up to 30 cm). Each SKU explodes into 12–18 variants. High return risk (wrong size, elastic doesn't fit, colour mismatch). Dominated at the top by Amazon Basics + a small set of Spanish bedding brands with 10k+ reviews. Violates the config rule "Products with too many sizes, colours, or variations".

### B) Terry Bathrobe — *albornoz rizo adulto*
- **Why rejected:** Clothing with S/M/L/XL sizing → fit-related returns are structurally high. Seasonal skew (autumn/winter). Heavy (bulky FBA size tier). Dominated by a mix of hotel-supply brands and beauty-adjacent labels with strong review moats. Rosanube brand fit is decent but return economics are hostile for a first launch. Violates config caution around "Products needing many sizes" + "Bulky".

### C) Waterproof Mattress Protector — *protector colchón impermeable ajustable*
- **Why rejected:** Same size-complexity problem as fitted sheets (90 → 180 cm × depth variants), and additionally requires "waterproof" performance claims that carry a compliance and return risk if a customer's mattress gets wet. Category is anchored by Amazon Basics, Pikolin, and a few established bedding brands with strong retail presence. Even if margin looked acceptable, review-barrier and complaint pattern make it a poor first launch. Marginal Rosanube brand fit.
