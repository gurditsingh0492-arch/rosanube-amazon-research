### 1) Muslin Kitchen Cloths (Paños Muselina de Algodón) — 4-pack

- **Spanish keyword:** *paños muselina cocina*, *trapos algodón muselina*, *paños queso muselina*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Muslin cloths are a quiet workhorse — used for straining, covering dough, polishing glass, wrapping herbs, wiping surfaces. They photograph well (soft drape, honest weave), are ultra-light for FBA, and sit adjacent to the crowded terry/waffle segment without competing head-on. Spanish home cooks are increasingly buying them post-MasterChef-España-style content.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified in H10/Keepa):** Steady, non-seasonal search volume for *muselina* in Amazon.es; category depth suggests several hundred units/month at the top of the search. Kitchen-textile buyers are known replenishers.
- **Competition signals:** Present but not concentrated — a handful of baby-muslin brands cross-list into kitchen, plus generic 2/4-pack listings. Review counts often 100–1,500 in the top 20. Price band €9–€18 for 4-packs, €13–€22 for 6-packs.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM / fabric density not disclosed.
  - Baby-muslin repurposed into "kitchen" without any relayering (customers complain "too thin, wrings out").
  - White-only, no colour or edge-binding differentiation.
  - Titles English-first; Spanish machine-translated.
  - Fraying edges after 3–5 washes.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 120–160 g/m² for a double-gauze construction).
  - **Double-gauze**, not single, so it actually absorbs — the biggest complaint fix.
  - Overlocked or bias-taped edge (documented, photographed close-up).
  - 50×70 cm — larger than the common 45×45 cm; more useful.
  - Neutrals palette (natural / stone / sage) + one "cocina blanca" pure white.
  - Kraft belly band with 4 real uses illustrated in Spanish.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch a single SKU = **4-pack mixed neutrals, 50×70 cm double-gauze**, ~€16.90. Later add 2-pack "starter" (€10.90) and a 6-pack "cocina completa" (€22.90). Avoid printed designs at launch.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**

## Do Not Pursue — 3 Rejected Ideas

### A) Electric Heated Blanket (Manta Eléctrica)
- **Rejection reason:** Electronic textile → EU low-voltage directive, EMC, CE marking, and a real safety file are mandatory. Return-risk elevated (electrical faults, thermostat complaints). Category dominated by Beurer, Imetec, Medisana with strong review moats. **Violates the config's "no electronics / no complicated certifications" rule.** Reject.

### B) Weighted Blanket (Manta con Peso / Manta Terapéutica)
- **Rejection reason:** The best-selling angle is "ansiedad / insomnio / autismo" — a **wellness/therapeutic claim** that requires medical device positioning or heavy disclaimers under EU rules. High return risk (weight preference mismatch). Bulky and heavy → high FBA fees erode margin. **Fails the config's "avoid medical/therapeutic claims" and "avoid oversized/heavy" rules.** Reject.

### C) Silk / "Mulberry Silk" Pillowcase
- **Rejection reason:** "100% mulberry silk" claims require verifiable certification (invoice + fibre test) or Amazon suppression risk. Highly counterfeit-heavy niche. Colour and momme-weight complaints drive returns. Compliance and IP tracing burden is high for a first launch. Add to watchlist only, not a first-6-months product. Reject.
