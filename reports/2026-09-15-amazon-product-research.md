## Do Not Pursue (3 products investigated and rejected)

### A. Weighted Blanket / *Manta con Peso Terapéutica*
- **Why rejected:** Adjacency to "therapeutic" / "anti-anxiety" claims puts the listing at real risk of Amazon compliance flags and buyer complaints when the claimed benefit is not felt. Very heavy (5–10 kg) → high FBA fee tier, high freight cost per unit, high return rate driven by weight-mismatch expectations. Dominated by a handful of established brands with 10k+ reviews. Multiple sizes × multiple weight variants explode the SKU count. **Fails config's "no medical claims", "not bulky", "not high return risk", and "not brand-dominated" rules.**

### B. Silk / "Mulberry Silk" Pillowcase
- **Why rejected:** True mulberry silk requires certified origin claims (momme weight, 6A grade) that Rosanube cannot verify without lab tests and paperwork. Cheap "silky satin polyester" alternatives dominate the low-price band and drag reviews down for the whole keyword. Colour-match complaints on satin/silk are constant. High return risk. **Fails config's "avoid unproven claims" and "high return risk" rules.**

### C. Bamboo Bedding Set / *Juego de Sábanas de Bambú*
- **Why rejected:** "Bamboo" bedding is almost always viscose/rayon derived from bamboo pulp — EU fibre labelling forbids calling that "bamboo" without qualification, and the OEKO-TEX / eco claims typical of the category are exactly the ones the config says not to use without proof. Category is heavily contested by a small number of larger private labels running aggressive PPC. **Fails config's "avoid unproven organic/eco claims", "products dominated by brands", and "complicated compliance" rules.**

## Compliance Snapshot (all 5 picks)

- **EU Textile Regulation** — fibre composition label required in Spanish on each unit. Applies to all five picks.
- **Care label** — wash symbols + Spanish text on each unit.
- **Country of origin** — printed on label / packaging.
- **GPSR** — EU responsible person / traceability documentation required.
- **EPR Packaging Spain (Ecoembes)** — registration for packaging material used.
- **REACH** — dye and finish declarations from supplier.
- **Polybag suffocation warning** — required if using standard polybag inner packaging.
- **No claim** of *organic*, *OEKO-TEX*, *hipoalergénico*, *antibacterial*, *chemical-free*, *eco-friendly*, or *Made in Portugal* unless a valid certificate / documented origin is on file. Muslin (#5) and any "linen" (#1) fibre content must be **exactly** as labelled — random test buys by Amazon happen.
