## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Bathroom Rug Set — 2-piece contour set (rug + toilet contour)

- **Spanish keywords:** *juego alfombra baño 2 piezas*, *set alfombra baño antideslizante*, *alfombra baño contorno wc*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Bath mats are a true replenishment SKU (they get moldy, worn, washed, replaced every 12–24 months). The **2-piece set** (rectangular mat + U-shaped WC contour) is a mid-price band that many single-mat sellers ignore — buyers browsing "juego alfombra baño" often see a mix of loose singles vs sets, so a well-photographed 2-piece bundle stands out.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent presence in the Alfombras de baño sub-category top results; multiple listings with 3-figure to 4-figure review counts; year-round demand with a mild lift at Rebajas / back-to-school flat-move season.
- **Competition signals:** Highly fragmented. Common price band €14–€28 for a 2-piece set. Top ranked listings often mix generic brands (RIVEA, Carpet Studio-style shops) with cheap unbranded Chinese imports; few strong EU brands own the sub-niche.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Anti-slip backing described but not shown in photos.
  - Pile height / GSM never stated.
  - Colours photographed under warm light — real vs displayed colour mismatch is the #1 review complaint.
  - Sizes given only in cm without a floor/room reference shot.
  - Machine-washable claim without a temperature/drying instruction.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real specs: pile height (target ~15 mm), GSM (~1600 g/m² micropoly or 100% cotton chenille as premium variant), latex-free TPR anti-slip backing shown in a close-up photo.
  - "Real light" photography — one shot in daylight, one in bathroom LED — to preempt colour-mismatch returns.
  - Neutral palette that matches Spanish bathroom tiling (blanco roto, arena, gris piedra, verde salvia).
  - Spanish-language care label + drying instruction printed on the sewn-in label.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 2-piece set (50×80 cm mat + 50×50 cm WC contour) in 3 neutral colours. Later add a 3-piece "premium" (mat + WC contour + toilet lid cover) at €24.90.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (dimensional / anti-slip return risk is real if backing quality slips)

### 2) 100% Cotton Percale Fitted Sheet (sábana bajera ajustable) — single-SKU launch

- **Spanish keywords:** *sábana bajera 100% algodón percal*, *sábana bajera ajustable 150*, *sábana bajera 90 algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Sábanas y fundas de almohada › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are the highest-replenishment item in bedding (worn thin, stained, replaced every 12–18 months). "Percal 100% algodón" is a **quality signal** Spanish buyers actively search for as a step up from generic microfibre. Selling **only the fitted sheet** (not a full set) sidesteps the complex sizing/matching of duvet-cover sets and reduces variation count dramatically.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** "sábana bajera" is a broad, evergreen keyword; long-tail *percal 100% algodón 150* is more specific and where a private-label brand can rank. Top listings often show hundreds of reviews per SKU across 3–4 sizes.
- **Competition signals:** Semi-fragmented. Zollner, Utopia Bedding, Amazon Basics, and Textilhome-style shops occupy the "microfibre bajera" space at €8–€14. The **cotton percal €15–€25** band is thinner and more enterable. Big Spanish incumbents (Pikolin Home, Belnou) exist but rarely dominate any single SKU.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thread count / GSM stated but rarely proven with material inspection photos.
  - "Percal" claimed on listings that are actually poly/cotton — a well-labelled 100% cotton competitor can win on truth.
  - Elastic all-round vs corners-only is unclear in most listings.
  - Depth of pocket (altura de colchón) missing or in fine print — the #1 return reason.
  - Colour range huge (10+ colours) → inventory bloat for the seller with no clear winner shown.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Launch **one size × one colour** first (150×190×30 cm, blanco) to prove demand before adding SKUs.
  - Publish real thread count (target **200 hilos** minimum, 100% peinado cotton).
  - Photograph the deep-pocket elastic on a real 30 cm mattress with a corner shot.
  - Spanish-first bullets, cm-only measurements, mattress depth in the title.
  - Neutral packaging with cotton drawstring bag (reusable) — giftable at zero perceived extra cost.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Phase 1 = 1 SKU (150×190×30 blanco). Phase 2 add 90×190 (individual) + 135×190 + 105×190. Phase 3 add 3 colours (blanco / arena / gris). Never launch >6 SKUs total in year 1.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (size fit is the return-risk lever — deep pocket + honest measurements mitigate)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (cotton COGS + freight weight is the pressure point)
  - Risk: 6/10

### 3) Reusable Swedish Dishcloth (paños esponja / bayetas suecas) — 10-pack

- **Spanish keywords:** *bayetas suecas reutilizables*, *paños esponja suecos*, *bayeta esponja algodón celulosa*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Textiles de cocina › Bayetas y paños de limpieza (also cross-listed in Limpieza del hogar)
- **Why worth researching:** Swedish dishcloths (cotton + cellulose, compostable, replace 15+ paper-towel rolls each) have moved from an eco-niche into mainstream Spanish kitchens over the last 2 years. **Small, light, cheap to ship, high-margin, repeat-purchase** — a near-ideal FBA profile. The sustainability story writes itself without needing certified claims.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Growing search interest ("bayeta sueca", "paño reutilizable cocina") in H10 Magnet trend curves is expected; listings with 500–3,000 reviews already exist; Instagram/TikTok "home cleaning" content in Spain has normalised the format.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented, mostly small brands (Wettex, Ecoetico, Ecoliving-style) and unbranded 10-packs. Top price band €9–€18 for a 10-pack. Few dominant EU-brand incumbents; Amazon Basics is not (yet) in this micro-niche.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Cellulose:cotton ratio not stated (buyers can't judge absorbency claim).
  - Printed designs are cluttered / cartoonish — a minimalist Iberian palette is missing.
  - Absorbency claim ("15x más que papel") given without a demo photo.
  - Compostability claim without documentation → EU claim risk if unproven.
  - Pack quantity inflation (advertised "20 pack" is actually 20 half-pieces).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - True 10-pack, full-size (17×20 cm), 70% cellulose / 30% cotton stated on pack.
  - 3 muted patterns per pack (solid arena, thin salvia stripe, off-white) — no cartoons.
  - Instead of "compostable" (requires certification), say **"apto para compostaje doméstico"** only if supplier provides a compost cert; otherwise "biodegradable" / "libre de plástico".

### 4) Cotton Pillow Protector — zippered, 2-pack (protector de almohada con cremallera)

- **Spanish keywords:** *protector de almohada con cremallera algodón*, *funda protectora almohada 40x70*, *funda protectora almohada 50x70 impermeable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Protectores › Protectores de almohada
- **Why worth researching:** Pillow protectors are the classic **invisible replenishment SKU** — bought once, replaced every 12–18 months, gifted to family, sold in packs. Low emotion, low aesthetic-return-risk (nobody sees them), high repeat rate. The 2-pack format is the sweet spot on Amazon.es.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** "protector almohada" is an evergreen head keyword; multiple 2-pack listings have 1,000+ reviews; Spanish buyers pair the search with mattress moves, allergy prep, and pet-in-bed households.
- **Competition signals:** Mixed. Utopia Bedding, Todocama, and Pikolin Home Basic dominate the €8–€14 microfibre segment. The **100% cotton + optional waterproof membrane** €15–€22 band is thinner and where a private-label brand can differentiate.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Impermeable" claimed without stating PU/TPU membrane material or waterproof rating.
  - Sizes 40×60 / 40×70 / 50×70 all sold in the same listing → buyers pick wrong size and return.
  - Photos show a pillow inserted, not the empty protector — the cremallera quality is invisible.
  - Loud noise when sleeping = the #1 review complaint on waterproof versions.
  - No mention of allergen / dust-mite barrier (the actual reason many buyers shop).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Two SKUs at launch: **cotton-only 2-pack** (breathable, no membrane, quiet) + **cotton + TPU membrane 2-pack** (waterproof, still low-noise) — clearly labelled so buyer self-selects.
  - Publish the membrane spec (100 g/m² TPU, silent-grade) with a "noise test" GIF-style image sequence.
  - Two sizes only (**40×70** — Spanish standard, and **50×70** — European standard) to avoid the "wrong size returned" trap.
  - YKK-style zipper close-up shot on the listing.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** 2-pack single-SKU launch (40×70, cotton no membrane) at €14.90. Add waterproof 2-pack at €19.90 within 60 days. Never sell as a 1-pack.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (main risk = "impermeable" claim precision — must be worded carefully)

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### 1) Weighted Blanket (manta pesada / manta con peso)

- **Why rejected:** Bulky (7–12 kg), oversize FBA tier destroys the fee economics. High variation complexity (weight × size × cover fabric). Dominated by established brands (YnM, Levitex, Bedsure). High return risk (buyers over-estimate or under-estimate the weight they need). Marginal health claims (anxiety, sleep) sit in an EU claim-risk zone.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO** — fails on FBA suitability, competition, and margin. Also directly listed as an "avoid" category (bulky, heavy, dominated by big brands).

### 2) Silk / "Satin" Pillowcase (funda almohada de seda / satén)

- **Why rejected:** Category is dominated by well-funded brands (Blissy, Slip, Kitsch, Zimasilk). "Real mulberry silk momme count" is a claim battlefield with active buyer scepticism and easy counterfeit accusations. Beauty-adjacent claims (skin/hair benefits) push it toward cosmetic-claim risk. Fits Rosanube's textile lane but not its **practical / clean / everyday** brand voice.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO** — brand-fit mismatch + brand-dominated competition + claim risk.

### 3) Hooded Adult Bathrobe (albornoz con capucha)

- **Why rejected:** Apparel-style variation complexity (S / M / L / XL / XXL × 3+ colours = 15+ SKUs before you sell one unit). High return risk (fit, sleeve length, hood shape). Seasonal skew (winter demand spikes, summer flat). Requires model photography (extra launch cost). Zollner, Utopia, MICHI, and Casa Colori occupy the mid-price band strongly.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO** — fails on variation complexity, return risk, seasonality, and photography cost.
