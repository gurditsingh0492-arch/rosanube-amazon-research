### 1) Swedish Dishcloth Multipack (Bayetas Suecas, 10-pack)

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas suecas*, *paños suecos cocina*, *bayeta celulosa reutilizable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y hogar › Bayetas y trapos › Bayetas de cocina (with strong cross-over to Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina)
- **Why worth researching:** Bayetas suecas (70% cellulose / 30% cotton, one dishcloth ≈ 15 rolls of kitchen paper) are the fastest-growing "eco-swap" SKU in Spanish kitchens — a repeat-purchase, low-price, low-risk consumable that pairs perfectly with Rosanube's clean-home aesthetic and can be printed cheaply for design differentiation. Slot 1–20 on Amazon.es is a mix of Nordic-brand small players and generic Chinese multipacks; a clean, Iberian-branded set with real Spanish copy is a realistic entry.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent, non-seasonal daily search demand; multiple listings with 3–5k+ reviews; steady presence in the Limpieza y hogar top results; giftable at Navidad and casa nueva.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. A handful of Nordic/UK small brands anchor the top, but most of positions 5–40 are private labels with 100–1,500 reviews and €7–€16 price band for 6–10 packs. Review-entry is realistic in 3–6 months.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Pack composition unclear (10 identical vs. 10 different prints — a top review complaint).
  - Cellulose/cotton ratio not stated, or stated only in English.
  - "Biodegradable / compostable" claimed without certification.
  - Prints are cluttered, saturated, "IKEA-cheap" — not aligned with a calm Iberian home.
  - Machine-translated Spanish copy, no dimensions in cm.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real composition (70% cellulose / 30% cotton) and dimensions (typically 17×20 cm).
  - Muted, coordinated colourway (natural, terracotta, salvia, off-white) — one 10-pack that matches Rosanube's towel palette.
  - Honest compostability language: "compostable in domestic composting after cutting" — no unproven eco-labels.
  - Kraft belly-band packaging with Spanish care instructions (max temp wash, dishwasher-safe on top rack).
  - Clean listing photos showing a Spanish tiled kitchen, not a white-cube studio.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 10-pack coordinated neutrals, €14.90. Later add a 20-pack "hogar completo" for €24.90 and a seasonal 10-pack "Navidad" edition with two festive prints inside a neutral pack.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**

  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (very light + very compact FBA)

### 2) 100% Linen Tea Towel Set (4-pack, 45×70 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *paños cocina lino*, *trapos cocina 100% lino*, *paño lino europeo*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Linen tea towels are the "premium tier" of the paños de cocina niche — higher price point (€22–€38 for a 4-pack vs. €10–€18 for cotton), less crowded, and a natural fit for Rosanube's Iberian-home positioning. Linen is genuinely more absorbent, more durable, lint-free (a top complaint on cotton), and dries faster — every one of these is an honest, listing-ready differentiator.
- **Demand signals:** Steady niche demand year-round, small but growing; heavy overlap with the "hostess gift" and "kitchen upgrade" buyer. Not seasonal.
- **Competition signals:** Thin. A few premium European brands (Belgian/Portuguese linen names) at €35–€60, then a big gap to €12–€20 "cotton-linen blend" products that mis-sell themselves. A real 100% linen 4-pack at €24.90 sits in a defensible middle.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Linen" listings that are actually 50/50 cotton-linen or ramie blends — compliance and trust risk that Rosanube can turn into an advantage.
  - Origin claimed vaguely ("European linen") without invoice-backed proof.
  - Complaints about stiffness on arrival — competitors don't pre-wash / stone-wash.
  - Only one colour, or a 12-colour tree that dilutes stock.
  - No dimensions in cm; no GSM stated.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Real** 100% European (Belgian/French flax) linen — invoice on file for compliance and copy.
  - Stone-washed / softened on arrival (documented, single-line claim).
  - Publish GSM (target 180–220 g/m²) and dimension (45×70 cm) in Spanish.
  - 3 muted colourways only: natural, salvia, terracotta.
  - Hanging loop + reinforced hem — small, documented quality signals.
  - Kraft sleeve packaging → giftable without extra unit cost.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 4-pack mixed neutrals at €24.90. Add a 2-pack "starter" (€14.90) after 50 reviews, and a linen apron + 2 towels gift bundle (€34.90) for Navidad.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (linen sourcing quality is real risk — needs sample + composition certificate)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 6/10 (smaller niche than cotton, but higher price)
  - Competition: 8/10 (thin at the honest-100%-linen mid tier)
  - Differentiation: 9/10 (composition, GSM, softening, palette, packaging — all provable)
  - Margin potential: 8/10 (premium price, light shipping)

### 3) Cotton Chenille Bath Mat (50×80 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño chenilla*, *tapete baño algodón absorbente*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The top of the Amazon.es *alfombra baño* results is dominated by cheap microfiber "shaggy" mats (frequently complained about for shedding, slipping, and going flat after 3 washes). A **100% cotton chenille** mat is the direct, honest upgrade — heavier, more absorbent, machine-washable at 40°, doesn't shed microfibers into the drain. It's a clean, Rosanube-shaped product with a real complaint to solve.
- **Demand signals:** Very steady, high-volume; bath mats are replenished every 12–24 months and gifted at casa nueva. Not seasonal.
- **Competition signals:** High-volume category but very fragmented in the honest-cotton-mat sub-slice. Microfiber shaggy dominates €8–€15; cotton chenille sits at €18–€32 with far fewer sellers. Review counts on the cotton sub-slice are usually 100–1,500, entry is realistic.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Anti-slip backing that peels off / disintegrates after 5–10 washes (repeated top complaint).
  - "Cotton" mats that are actually cotton-poly blends.
  - No GSM/weight stated.
  - One dimension only (usually 50×80), no offering for the small Spanish bathroom (40×60).
  - Colours photograph very differently from delivery.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Real 100% cotton chenille pile on a **stitched-in** anti-slip base (not glue-dot backing), documented.
  - Publish weight (target 1,300–1,600 g/m²) and dimensions in cm.
  - 4 muted colourways: natural, off-white, salvia, terracotta.
  - Lifestyle photos in a real Spanish bathroom (small footprint, tile).
  - Care label + Spanish fibre composition + wash-at-40° instruction on a woven tag, not a stuck-on sticker.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 50×80 cm in 4 colours (single-colour packs). Add 40×60 cm "piso pequeño" size after 100 reviews. A 2-piece bath mat + contour mat set is a possible V2, but only after FBA fee math is confirmed — bath mats can push into a bigger FBA tier.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (colour-accuracy and anti-slip complaints are the two most common return reasons — both fixable but need sample verification)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (heavier than kitchen textiles, watch FBA tier)
  - Risk: 6/10

- **Spanish keyword:** *camino de mesa lino*, *individuales mesa tela*, *mantel individual y camino lino*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de mesa › Caminos de mesa (+ Manteles individuales)
- **Why worth researching:** Table linen on Amazon.es is dominated by either cheap PVC placemats or high-end European brands. A coordinated **runner + 6 matching placemats** in linen (or linen-look cotton blend) at €29.90–€34.90 sits in a real gap. Bundles are a strong Rosanube play because the category is fragmented, the customer is buying "a set for the dinner table" (not one item), and the AOV/margin arithmetic works better than a single item.
- **Demand signals:** Steady demand with clear peaks around Navidad, Pascua, and communions; year-round baseline exists. Table linen is a repeat-purchase category (stains, upgrades, seasonal changes).
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Most competitors sell only the runner OR only placemats — very few honest "runner + 6 matching placemats" bundles at this price point. Review-entry is realistic.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Sold as separate SKUs — customer has to hunt for a matching set (common review complaint).
  - Sizes stated in inches or unclear; runner too short for Spanish 6-seater tables (need ~180 cm).
  - "Linen" that's actually polyester or a cotton-poly blend — not stated honestly.
  - Colours vary wildly from photo to delivery.
  - Only iron-only care — many customers want machine-washable.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Honest** fibre composition (either real 100% linen, or a documented "55% linen / 45% cotton" linen-look) — never called "lino" without proof.
  - Runner sized for Spanish 6- and 8-seat tables (180×40 cm and 220×40 cm).
  - 6 placemats sized 45×30 cm to match.
  - Machine-washable at 30° — documented and prominent.
  - 3 muted colourways: natural, salvia, terracotta.
  - Kraft belly-band packaging with a "set the Spanish table" one-page illustrated guide inside.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** **This IS the bundle** — the differentiation is that competitors don't sell it as one SKU. Launch 180 cm runner + 6 placemats at €29.90 in 3 colours. Add a Navidad edition (natural + salvia mix) and a 220 cm runner + 8 placemats V2 for larger tables.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (colour accuracy + linen composition honesty are both real risks; carton size and FBA fee also need verification for the runner)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 6/10 (smaller niche than kitchen/bath, some seasonal skew)
  - Competition: 8/10 (nobody is doing the honest coordinated bundle well)
  - Differentiation: 9/10 (the set format IS the differentiation)
  - Margin potential: 8/10 (higher AOV, bundle math is friendly)
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 37/50**

## Do Not Pursue (Rejections)

### R1) Weighted Blanket / Manta con peso

- **Why rejected:**
  - Very heavy (5–10 kg) → oversized FBA tier and prohibitive fulfilment fees on Amazon.es.
  - High return risk (buyer size/weight preference is very personal).
  - Category dominated by established brands (Gravity, Layla, YnM) with big marketing budgets.
  - Frequent implicit "therapeutic / anxiety / sleep-aid" claims — compliance risk Rosanube should not touch without a medical file.
  - Poor Rosanube brand fit (not a calm, everyday-textile item).
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected**

### R2) Bamboo Bedsheet Set (Sábanas de bambú)

- **Why rejected:**
  - "Bamboo" fibre is almost always **regenerated viscose from bamboo** — EU Textile Regulation requires it to be labelled "viscose" or "lyocell", not "bamboo". Multiple Amazon.es listings mislabel this and face compliance action. Risk for Rosanube to inherit.
  - Bedsheets require many size variants (90, 135, 150, 180 × 190/200 cm × fitted/flat/pillowcase) → oversized SKU tree and cash-flow risk.
  - Category dominated by big brands (Pikolín, Bassols, Casa) with fabric-quality reputations.
  - High colour-accuracy return risk.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected**

### R3) Kids' Character-Print Duvet Cover (Funda nórdica infantil personajes)

- **Why rejected:**
  - Almost every high-selling design carries a licensed character (Disney, Marvel, Bluey, Frozen, etc.) — impossible to enter without a real licence.
  - Non-licensed "generic" designs sell poorly vs. the licensed leaders.
  - Fashion/trend-driven demand — cycles fast, hard to forecast.
  - Bedding sizes (90, 105, 135, 150) add SKU complexity.
  - Explicit IP/legal risk conflicts with Rosanube's private-label safety rules in `research_config.md`.
- **Decision:** **NO GO / Rejected**
