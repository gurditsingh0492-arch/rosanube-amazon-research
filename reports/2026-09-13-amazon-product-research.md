### 2) Quick-Dry Diatomite-Alternative Bath Mat — Cotton Chenille, Non-Slip Backing

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño antideslizante*, *alfombrilla baño chenilla algodón*, *alfombra baño absorbente*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Diatomite / stone bath mats had a strong run but generate returns (cracking in shipment, cold underfoot, look industrial). A **cotton chenille** bath mat with a **latex-dot non-slip back** solves the same problem (fast-dry, absorbent, safe on tile), is soft underfoot, and ships flat. It is a repeat-purchase item (customers replace every 12–24 months) and easy to differentiate on colour / density / edge finish.
- **Demand signals:** Bath mats are a permanent, non-seasonal category on Amazon.es with a small Sep–Nov uplift as households refresh bathrooms before winter. Consistent daily demand across the €15–€30 band.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. A handful of mid-sized brands (mostly generic Chinese private labels re-branded for Amazon EU) hold the top slots; slots 5–30 are wide open to a well-photographed private label. Review counts commonly 200–3,000.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Non-slip backing described but not shown up close.
  - GSM / pile height not published.
  - Complaints about non-slip dots detaching after 3–5 washes.
  - Complaints about the mat being much smaller than expected.
  - Machine-translated Spanish copy; sizes in cm buried in bullet 4.
  - Photography almost always studio-only (no bathroom context).

  - Publish real dimensions (50×80 cm target), pile height, GSM, and dot-count of the non-slip backing.

  - 3 muted colours only (blanco roto, gris piedra, salvia) — no beach-towel colours.

  - Include a small paper insert with a "cuidado de tu alfombra" guide in Spanish/Portuguese/English.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as single 50×80 cm at €19.90. Later add a **2-piece set** (large 50×80 + small 40×60 for in front of the sink) at €29.90 — a differentiated bundle almost no competitor offers cleanly.

- **Rejection reason:** Attractive at first look, but 100 % linen bedding is a **high-return category** on Amazon.es due to wrinkle complaints, colour drift between batches, and fitted-sheet size mismatch (Spanish bed sizes 90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 180 cm × mattress depth = many SKUs). Cost per set is high, working capital heavy, PPC very expensive, and margin is thin after returns. Fails the "not too many variations" and "low return risk" rules.
