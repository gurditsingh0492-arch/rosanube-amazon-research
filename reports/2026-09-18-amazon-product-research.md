## Do Not Pursue (rejected today)

### A. Weighted blanket (manta con peso)
**Reason:** Heavy (6–10 kg) → oversized FBA tier destroys margin; return risk high (too heavy / too light complaints); saturated by established brands with 10k+ reviews; therapeutic/wellness marketing claims are a compliance grey zone in EU without documentation. Bad fit for a first Rosanube launch.

### B. Diatomaceous stone / memory-foam bath mat (alfombra baño piedra)
**Reason:** Diatom stone versions are fragile (breakage-in-transit returns), and marketing leans on absorbency/anti-mould claims that are hard to substantiate. Memory-foam versions are dominated by heavy Chinese sellers racing to the bottom. Both violate the "avoid fragile" and "avoid claim-heavy" rules in the config.

### C. Novelty-print beach towel (toalla de playa estampada)
**Reason:** Highly seasonal (April–August peak), fashion-trend dependent, IP/licensing risk on prints, needs many designs to compete → too many SKUs and photography cost. Config explicitly warns against seasonal, fashion-trend, and licensed products.
