Today's shortlist stays inside Rosanube's core lane — kitchen, bath, bed, and table textiles — and biases toward the config's *Ideal Product Profile*: lightweight, easy to bundle, non-seasonal, low return risk, low compliance friction, and priced in the €15–€35 band.

None of the five is recommended for a purchase order today. All are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist** pending Helium 10, Keepa, supplier quotes, and an FBA calculator run.

## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Cotton Muslin Multi-Purpose Kitchen Cloths — 10-pack

- **Spanish keywords:** *muselinas de algodón cocina*, *paños muselina 100% algodón*, *paños cocina finos multiuso*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Muselinas are a genuine everyday-Spanish-kitchen replenishment SKU — used to cover masa, colar caldo, envolver pan, secar copas, cubrir amasado. They ship flat, weigh almost nothing, and forgive small production defects. The Amazon.es space is populated by generic "12-pack white muslin cloth" listings clearly translated from English and merchandised for baby wipes — leaving a clean opening for a proper *kitchen-first* Spanish-copy positioning.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified in Helium 10):** Consistent year-round replenishment demand; overlap with the "cocina slow / repostería casera" audience; giftable at Reyes/Navidad without being seasonal-only. **Needs manual verification** via Helium 10 Xray / Cerebro on the Spanish keywords above.
- **Competition signals (qualitative):** Fragmented, mostly generic private label. Typical listings are 6- or 12-packs at €10–€20, most sold as "baby muslin" repurposed for kitchen. Few dedicated kitchen-muselina brands with proper Spanish copy. Review counts commonly range low-hundreds to low-thousands — realistic entry.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Marketed as "baby cloth" and only mentioning kitchen use as an afterthought.
  - Composition unclear (100% cotton vs. cotton-poly not disclosed).
  - No GSM stated; buyers complain about "too thin, transparent, useless for straining".
  - Sizes only in inches, or vague ("large").
  - Images shot on white background — zero kitchen context.
  - Machine-translated Spanish titles ("paño de la muselina").
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 90–120 g/m²) and pre-washed status.
  - Kitchen-first Spanish copy: *muselinas para colar, cubrir, secar, envolver*.
  - 45×45 cm and 65×65 cm mixed pack — the two sizes Spanish home cooks actually use.
  - Natural, écru, salvia neutrals — not printed cartoons.
  - Kraft belly-band with 6 real kitchen uses illustrated (icon strip, not stock photos).
  - Spanish care label + fibre composition + hanging loop.
  - Do **not** claim *organic*, *OEKO-TEX*, or *antibacterial* unless a certificate is on file.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 10-pack, mixed neutrals, mixed 45/65 cm. Later add a 6-pack "starter" at ~€13.90 and a 12-pack "repostería" edition with a bread-cover diagram card.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (very light, compact FBA; but low ASP demands high AOV per pack)
  - Risk: 9/10 (small, cheap sample, low return, low compliance)
  - **Total: 39/50**
- **Suggested decision:** **Further Research** — priority pick for Helium 10 + Keepa + supplier quote today.

### 2) Reusable Cotton Facial Rounds — 16-pack with Cotton Laundry Bag

- **Spanish keywords:** *discos desmaquillantes reutilizables algodón*, *discos algodón lavables*, *toallitas desmaquillantes reutilizables*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Accesorios de baño textiles (also cross-lists into Belleza — Rosanube should stay in *Hogar y cocina*)
- **Why worth researching:** A textile bathroom accessory, not a cosmetic — so it stays inside Rosanube's low-risk lane and avoids the Belleza compliance overhead. Reusable rounds are a growing replacement for cotton-pad disposables (which have been rising in price in Spain) and appeal to the same "sostenible sin ser militante" buyer who buys peshtemal towels and cotton bags. Ships in a tiny polybag → excellent FBA economics on a €12–€18 ASP.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Repeat purchase (people re-buy after 8–12 months once they discover the format). Non-seasonal; slight Q1 uplift with New-Year routines. Overlaps with the "menos residuos" and "rutina skincare minimalista" audiences that are visible on Amazon.es.
- **Competition signals (qualitative):** Present but fragmented. Many 16-pack listings at €9–€16; the top slots are held by small labels, not brand giants. Review counts range from low-hundreds to low-thousands.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Bamboo-viscose sold as "bamboo cotton" — misleading composition.
  - No GSM/fabric type disclosed.
  - Rough edges from cheap overlock; buyers complain about *irritan la piel*.
  - Laundry bag either missing or too flimsy to survive a wash cycle.
  - Titles clearly machine-translated from English.
  - Green/leaf visuals with unverified *eco* / *bio* / *biodegradable* claims — a compliance trap Rosanube must not repeat.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Two fabric sides — soft cotton velour + waffle cotton — documented in Spanish, no vague *bambú*.
  - True 8 cm diameter (many competitors are 7 cm and receive complaints).
  - Sturdy cotton laundry bag with a drawstring and reinforced seam — sized to hold all 16 rounds.
  - Neutral palette (écru, salvia, terracotta) — no pastel prints.
  - Neutral kraft box, giftable as a "detalle" for €12–€15.
  - Care label + fibre composition in Spanish; **no** eco / bio / biodegradable claims unless certified.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single SKU = 16 rounds + laundry bag. Later add a "duo" version (16 rounds + waffle face cloth) at €19.90. Skip printed patterns.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**

### 3) Ribbed-Cotton Kitchen Apron + Tea Towel Bundle

- **Spanish keywords:** *delantal cocina algodón*, *delantal y paño cocina conjunto*, *delantal unisex cocina bolsillo*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Delantales
- **Why worth researching:** Aprons are their own sub-niche on Amazon.es and often ship as **single** items with underwhelming photography. Pairing a well-made *delantal* with a matching *paño de cocina* is a differentiation Rosanube can own on Amazon.es because almost no listings do it well in Spanish. The bundle raises AOV into the €22–€30 band (config sweet spot) while the physical footprint stays flat and light for FBA.
- **Demand signals (qualitative):** Non-seasonal core demand + strong Q4 gift bump (Navidad, Reyes, "regalo para el que cocina en casa"). Aprons are also a repeat-purchase item because kitchen aprons stain and wear out — but review-wise they behave more like a giftable than a replenishment SKU. **Needs Helium 10 + Keepa verification.**
- **Competition signals (qualitative):** Fragmented — most listings are cheap generic aprons at €8–€14 with weak fabric and vague pockets. Very few bundled *delantal + paño* SKUs in the €20–€30 band. Some IKEA / Zara Home presence, but Amazon.es itself is not brand-dominated for this sub-niche.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thin poly-cotton that stains after one meal.
  - Pockets sewn on but flopping open or falling off.
  - Adjustable neck strap breaks after few washes.

### 4) Percale Cotton Fitted Sheet — Standard Spanish Sizes (90 / 105 / 135 / 150 cm)

- **Spanish keywords:** *sábana bajera algodón percal*, *sábana ajustable 135 algodón 200 hilos*, *sábana bajera cama individual algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are one of the few bedroom SKUs where Rosanube can enter without competing with brand giants who own the sábana-set market. Selling a **single-piece** *bajera* (not a full set) reduces variation complexity, ships flat, and hits the €18–€30 band comfortably. Spanish mattress sizes are standardised (90 / 105 / 135 / 150) so the size tree is manageable — this is precisely the "low-size-complexity textile essential" the config prefers.
- **Demand signals (qualitative):** Very steady daily demand — bedsheets are replaced 1–2× per year per household on average, and fitted sheets wear out fastest. Non-seasonal. **Needs Helium 10 + Keepa verification** especially per-size split.
- **Competition signals (qualitative):** Amazon Basics, Utopia Bedding, and a few Spanish brands hold the top. But the sub-niche of *percal ~200 hilos, algodón 100%, precio medio* is more open than the polyester / microfibra bottom or the 400-thread premium tier. Review counts range widely; a new listing needs ~200 reviews to be competitive on page 1.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Algodón" claimed but composition is cotton-poly.
  - Elastic all-around vs. elastic-corners-only not disclosed.
  - Deep-pocket depth in cm not disclosed — buyers complain sheet doesn't fit *colchón alto / topper*.
  - Colour differs from photo.
  - Shrinkage after first wash (worst review category).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **100% cotton, 200 hilos** — publish clearly, not just in bullets.
  - All-around elastic + gusset to fit up to 30 cm mattress depth — call it out with a diagram.
  - Pre-washed to control shrinkage; disclose shrinkage tolerance in Spanish care label.
  - Muted, "clean Iberian home" colours only (natural, blanco roto, gris perla, salvia).
  - Correct EU Textile Regulation fibre label + Spanish care label + country of origin.
  - Kraft belt with cm dimensions printed large — remove the #1 review complaint at unboxing.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch one size (135×190 cm, matrimonio-individual bridge) in 3 colours. Add 90 and 150 as separate SKUs after first 200 reviews. **Do not** launch a 3-piece set on day one (too many variations).
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (bedsheet buyers are picky about fit and colour; return risk is higher than kitchen/bath).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (medium — brand giants are present)
  - Differentiation: 6/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (larger unit, higher FBA fee tier, competitive pricing)
  - Risk: 6/10

### 5) Linen-Look Placemats 6-pack + Coordinated Napkins 6-pack — Table Bundle

- **Spanish keywords:** *manteles individuales lino*, *manteles individuales tela set*, *servilletas de tela lino conjunto*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de mesa › Manteles individuales / Servilletas
- **Why worth researching:** The Spanish table-textile market is dominated by two poles: cheap PVC / oilcloth placemats on one end, and IKEA / Zara Home fabric sets on the other. In between there is a real gap for a mid-price **coordinated fabric bundle** (6 placemats + 6 napkins) at €22–€30 in muted colours. It ships flat, has zero size complexity, and every household hosts 6–8 people at some point (Navidad, cumpleaños, comidas familiares). Fabric placemats are also more repeat-purchase than they look because they wear stains over time.
- **Demand signals (qualitative):** Steady with strong Q4 (Navidad, Nochebuena, Nochevieja) and a smaller Q2 uplift (comuniones, bodas casuales, terraza). **Needs Helium 10 + Keepa verification** — the Q4 bump means Keepa 365-day view is essential to distinguish stable demand from seasonal-only.
- **Competition signals (qualitative):** Fragmented but growing. Most listings are 6-pack placemats or 6-pack napkins **separately** — few sell a coordinated set. Common price bands: placemats €12–€22 for 6, napkins €10–€18 for 6. A bundle at €24.90 has a clear positioning story.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Lino" claimed but composition is 100% polyester "linen-look".
  - Dimensions not in cm, or wrong (a 30×40 cm placemat is too small for a full dinner plate).
  - Ironing required but not disclosed.
  - Fraying edges from raw-cut poly.
  - Colours saturated and photo-boosted — buyers complain about mismatch.
  - Napkins sold too small (35×35 cm — reads as cocktail size).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - True cotton-linen blend (50/50 or 70/30) — published composition.
  - Placemats 33×48 cm (Spanish dinner-plate friendly).
  - Napkins 42×42 cm (real dinner size, not cocktail size).
  - Mitered corners + double-fold hem — no fraying.
  - Coordinated colourway (natural + terracotta, natural + salvia, blanco roto + gris) — 3 palette SKUs, not 12.
  - Pre-washed to reduce first-wash shrink.
  - Spanish care label + fibre composition; kraft belly-band with a "6 personas" table-setting photo.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as **one bundle SKU per colourway** (6 placemats + 6 napkins). Do not sell placemats and napkins separately at launch — the bundle is the differentiation. After 200 reviews, consider a 4+4 "pareja" bundle for smaller households.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (colour-accuracy risk; ironing complaint risk; Q4 seasonal skew must be verified on Keepa).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 7/10
  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (higher AOV, flat & light)
  - Risk: 6/10

- **Suggested decision:** **Further Research** — Rosanube's best *bundle* opportunity today; validate Keepa 365-day pattern before committing.

## Do Not Pursue — Rejected Today

### A) Weighted Blankets (*mantas con peso, mantas de gravedad*)

- **Reason:** High shipping cost (typically 5–9 kg — pushes into oversized FBA size tiers on Amazon.es), high return risk (buyers frequently return over weight expectations and heat retention), medical-adjacent buyer expectations that flirt with *sueño, ansiedad, hipersensibilidad* claims Rosanube cannot substantiate. Config explicitly avoids "medical / therapeutic" claims and "very heavy products".
- **Decision:** **Rejected** — outside Ideal Product Profile.

### B) Silk Pillowcases (*fundas almohada seda*)

- **Reason:** Sub-niche is dominated by strong brands (Blissy, Slip, and a growing tier of premium Spanish sellers). Buyer expectations on *mommes / mume* silk grade and colour accuracy drive high return rates. Fits the config's "products dominated by big brands" and "high return risk" categories.
- **Decision:** **Rejected** — brand-dominated + return-risk.

### C) Electric Heated Blankets (*manta eléctrica*)

- **Reason:** Combines textile + electronics + heating element — triggers CE / LVD / EMC compliance, requires a documented safety file, plug/cable certification, and is banned per config's *Products to Avoid* list (electronics, batteries adjacent, chemical/dangerous-goods risk when heating elements fail). Q4 seasonal spike is real but the compliance burden is disproportionate for a first Rosanube launch.
- **Decision:** **Rejected** — compliance and safety risk outside brand scope.
