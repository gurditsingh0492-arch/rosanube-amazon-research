## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric heated blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Contains electronics + heating element → violates config rule "Avoid electronics / batteries / products with unclear legal or safety restrictions." Requires low-voltage electrical certification (LVD 2014/35/EU), EMC directive, thermal-cutoff safety file, and Amazon EU electrical-safety compliance dossier. High return risk (customers report thermostat failure). Also dominated by established brands (Beurer, Imetec, Daga). Poor fit for Rosanube's textile brand voice and first private-label launches.

### B) Cashmere throw blanket (*manta cachemir*)
- **Why rejected:** High cost per unit → forces sell price above the Rosanube preferred band (€15–€35), or crushes margin at €35. Dominated by luxury and premium brands (Zara Home, El Corte Inglés house label, Bassols) — differentiation on Amazon.es requires cost-prohibitive brand equity. Fibre origin and mulesing-adjacent supply chain increases compliance and reputational risk. High return risk on colour/feel/authenticity. Fails margin, competition, and risk gates simultaneously.

### C) Children's licensed-character duvet cover (*funda nórdica personajes infantil*, e.g. Disney/Marvel/Bluey)
- **Why rejected:** Requires legal IP licence — violates config rule "Licensed character products unless legally licensed." Even generic character-inspired designs invite Amazon IP takedowns and legal action from rights-holders. Category is otherwise anchored by official licensees (CTI, SDL) with distribution deals. High return risk on children's bedding (size confusion, colour). No realistic path for a private label without licensing costs that destroy margin.
