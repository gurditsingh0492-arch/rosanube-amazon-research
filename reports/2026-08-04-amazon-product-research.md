- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Textiles de mesa › Servilletas de tela
- **Why worth researching:** Reusable cotton napkins ride the "no-more-paper-servilletas" shift in Spanish households, but they are still a small, replenishment-friendly item (staining, wear). They pair naturally with the linen-look table runner already on the July 1 watchlist to build a "Mesa Rosanube" bundle without launching more heavy SKUs. Very simple sizing (typically 40×40 cm or 45×45 cm), one-piece manufacture, low FBA weight tier, low return risk.

  - "Linen" / "lino" claim on 100% polyester is common — a review-complaint driver.

### 2) Waterproof Fitted Mattress Protector (Protector de colchón impermeable ajustable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable rizo*, *protector colchón 135 impermeable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** Boring, high-repeat, standard Spanish mattress sizes (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm) — five clean size variants, no colour SKUs. Sold to parents, guest-room owners, students, and short-term-rental hosts (Spain has a big turístico segment). Low aesthetic risk (colour doesn't matter — it's white or off-white). High replenishment: expect replacement every 2–3 years or after staining.
- **Demand signals:** Very steady daily demand; not seasonal. Category consistently populated with 4-figure-review listings. Search volume for *protector colchón impermeable* + size suffixes is meaningful year-round.
- **Competition signals:** Present but fragmented. A few generic brands anchor the top; below the top 10 there is a long tail of private labels with 100–1,000 reviews and improvable listings. Price band ~€12–€28 for a single, depending on size.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Rizo" (terry) top surface but reviews report thin, plastic-crinkly feel.
  - Elastic gives up after few washes — the biggest complaint driver.
  - Sizing errors (135×190 shipped when 135×200 was ordered).
  - Claims like *antiácaros* and *hipoalergénico* used without certificates → compliance risk we must avoid.
  - Multiple listings do not label whether the sides are elasticated or a full skirt (buyer confusion).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Real terry-cotton surface + PU membrane (documented composition on the label).
  - Reinforced elastic skirt with stated skirt depth (e.g. up to 30 cm) — photograph the skirt in the listing image.
  - Guarantee exact Spanish mattress sizes; label all dimensions in cm, in Spanish.
  - **Do not** claim *antiácaros / hipoalergénico / antibacterial* unless a valid certificate is supplied. Per config §Compliance Rules.
  - Include a simple washing-instructions insert in Spanish, showing max wash temperature and dryer setting.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:**
  - Launch **only 135 cm** first (the most sold Spanish size) at ~€19.90 to prove listing + logistics.
  - Add 90, 150, 180 cm once the 135 SKU has stable reviews.
  - Later: **2-pack for hosts / student rooms** at higher AOV.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** *(elastic + waterproof claim are the two areas where quality complaints appear; controllable via sample QC)*

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Throw Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Contains electrical components → excluded by config §"Products to Avoid" (electronics, dangerous-goods risk). Requires CE / LVD / EMC compliance and battery/electric-appliance safety documentation. Category is anchored by established appliance brands (Imetec, Beurer, Medisana). Return risk is high (heat setting complaints, cable issues). Poor Rosanube brand fit. **NO GO.**

### B) Bamboo Bed Sheet Set (*juego sábanas bambú*)
- **Why rejected:** Two hard problems in one product. (1) "Bambú" on Amazon.es is frequently disputed by regulators as viscose-de-bambú, not true bamboo fibre — misleading claim risk, EU Textile Regulation issue. (2) Bed sheet sets on Amazon.es Spain require **many** size + colour variations (90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 180 × 3–5 colours × 2–3 depths) — a SKU tree that violates the config's "not too many variations" rule. Very high return risk from colour, size, and feel complaints. **Rejected.**

### C) Baby / Infant Hooded Bath Towel (*toalla capucha bebé*)
- **Why rejected:** Baby / infant textiles bring specific safety expectations (OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I, EN 71 for anything a baby chews on, allergy sensitivity). Buyers demand certificates that Rosanube does not currently hold and cannot claim per config §Compliance Rules. Very high review sensitivity — a single negative safety review sinks a baby-textile listing. Also brand-anchored (Cambrass, Interbaby, Baby-Nest). **NO GO for now.** Reconsider only if Rosanube holds valid OEKO-TEX Class I certificates from the supplier.
