### 2) Waffle Guest / Face Towel Pack (Toallas de invitados waffle, 6-pack)

- **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Toallas de invitados / Toallas de tocador
- **Spanish keywords:** *toallas invitados waffle*, *toallas tocador algodón*, *toallitas cara algodón pack*
- **Why worth researching:** Guest towels are small (typically 30×30 cm or 30×50 cm), light, flat-packed — the best-possible FBA economics inside the towel family. They extend Rosanube's waffle-weave positioning into a subcategory that is far less crowded than full bath towels, with a natural "guest bathroom / powder room" gifting angle. Also a repeat-purchase SKU as guest towels wear or stain out of a set fast.
- **Demand signals:** Steady, year-round, with a modest lift around Navidad and Bodas / Comuniones season (spring). Not a mass-volume category but a very brand-friendly one.
- **Competition signals:** Thin. Most Amazon.es results in "toallas invitados" are terry, not waffle; the waffle sub-niche is currently underserved. Price band €14–€25 for a 4–6 pack. Review counts on top competitors are often <500 — a realistic entry barrier for a new private label with strong photography.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM undeclared or unrealistically high for the weave.
  - Poor lifestyle context — mostly white-background product shots, no powder-room styling.
  - Set composition inconsistent (mixed sizes shipped as "6-pack").
  - Complaints about lint on first use and fraying at the loop.
  - Colours skew saturated (bright red, royal blue) — a gap for the "neutral, calm home" buyer.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 380–430 g/m² waffle) and pre-washed status.

  - Muted palette matched to future waffle bath towel SKU (waffle bath towel is already on the 2026-07-01 watchlist → future cross-sell).
  - Kraft-band packaging with Spanish care instructions.
  - Explicit "for hand-drying / face / guest bathroom" listing angle to set expectations and avoid absorbency complaints from bath-towel buyers.

### 4) Reusable "Unpaper" Kitchen Cloth Pack (Bayetas de cocina reutilizables lavables)

- **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y menaje del hogar › Bayetas y paños / Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Spanish keywords:** *bayetas reutilizables cocina*, *paños cocina lavables reutilizables*, *toallas papel reutilizables algodón*
- **Why worth researching:** A rising eco / zero-waste sub-niche on Amazon.es: cotton or bamboo cloths sold as a lavable alternative to paper towels, often in 6-, 10-, or 12-piece packs, sometimes with snaps to form a "roll" replacing kitchen paper. Small, flat, light — ideal FBA. Strong differentiation potential via material honesty (cotton flannel vs. microfiber vs. bamboo) and packaging, and a real, repeated customer pain (paper-towel cost + waste) that Rosanube's "clean, practical, everyday" positioning speaks to naturally.
- **Demand signals:** Growing but not yet mass-market — a slower-burn, brand-builder SKU with a defined, loyal buyer segment (eco-conscious households, families with kids, sustainable-home content viewers). Steady year-round.
- **Competition signals:** Manageable. Most existing listings are generic imports with weak photography and unclear material claims. A few small brands own the top slots but with review counts often <1,000. Price band €12–€25 for a 10–12 pack.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Bambú" claimed but real composition is cotton-viscose or polyester — customer complaint driver, and a compliance risk for Rosanube unless the label is verifiable.
  - Snap-together "roll" is gimmicky and reviewed as fragile — most buyers cut them off and use them as flat cloths.
  - Absorbency claims not demonstrated.
  - Poor Spanish copy — many listings translate directly from English "unpaper towels" without a Spanish framing.
  - Colours skew childish (rainbow prints); no adult, calm option.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Honest material label — cotton flannel or cotton-terry, no bamboo claim unless documented.
  - Skip the gimmicky snap-roll — sell as a **flat 10-pack**, 25×25 cm, unfussy and adult.
  - Publish real GSM (target 200–260 g/m²) and wash-cycle rating.
  - Neutral palette matched to the rest of the Rosanube kitchen family.
  - Spanish-first listing copy — position as "sustituto lavable del papel de cocina" clearly, with a real washability + drying-time claim.
  - Kraft box with a printed Spanish "cómo usarlo" card (1-page use guide, no glossy insert cost).
- **Suggested bundle or variation strategy:** Hero SKU = 10-pack flat cloths, 25×25 cm, mixed neutrals. Add a 6-pack "starter" for €12.90 and (only after volume proves it) a 20-pack "family" size later. Cross-bundle with waffle kitchen towels for a "Zero-waste kitchen" mini-pack.

### 5) 100% Cotton Percale Pillowcase Pair (Fundas de almohada percal, 2-pack)

- **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas de almohada
- **Spanish keywords:** *fundas de almohada algodón percal*, *funda almohada 45x110 pack 2*, *fundas almohada algodón 100%*
- **Why worth researching:** Pillowcases only (not full sheet sets) are the simplest and lowest-return-risk entry into Amazon.es bedding. Spanish pillow sizes are standardised (mostly 45×110 cm for the standard Spanish rectangular pillow, plus 45×90 cm and 45×75 cm variants), so the size tree is manageable — 2 SKUs, not 20. Percale (200+ TC crisp cotton weave) is a well-recognised buyer term and lets Rosanube compete on fabric honesty against listings that vaguely say "algodón" without weave or thread-count. Repeat-purchase item (replaced every 12–24 months).
- **Demand signals:** High, steady, year-round. Pillowcases are consistently near the top of "Fundas y protectores de almohada" searches on Amazon.es.
- **Competition signals:** Heavier than the other four picks — several large bedding brands and Amazon Basics anchor the top slots with 5k+ reviews. Private-label opportunity exists in slots 5–30, and specifically in the "honest percale, muted colour, Spanish sizing 45×110" position. Price band €10–€22 per 2-pack.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Weave / thread-count not stated; "sábana suave" instead of real specs.
  - Sizing errors — advertise 45×110, deliver 50×75 (recurring complaint).
  - Colour differs from photo, especially in whites/creams.
  - Poor closure — envelope flap too short and pillow slips out.
  - Wrinkles heavily after wash — need to explicitly market percale as "crisp, not slippery" (satin is a different SKU) so buyers self-select correctly.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real thread count / GSM (target 200+ TC, 110–130 g/m² cotton percale).
  - Deep envelope closure — 20 cm inner flap, documented with a diagram in the listing image.
  - Confirmed Spanish sizing 45×110 cm; label and packaging in Spanish.
  - Muted palette only (natural, off-white, sand, sage, slate) — skip saturated colours where colour-accuracy complaints spike.
  - Do **not** claim *hipoalergénico*, *antiácaros*, or *fresco térmico* — no certificate, per config compliance rules.
- **Suggested bundle or variation strategy:** Hero SKU = 2-pack, 45×110 cm, natural. Add sage and slate colours after reviews stabilise. Only later add 45×90 cm and 45×75 cm variants once the 45×110 is winning. Do not launch a full sheet set until pillowcases have proven listing + fulfilment + return economics.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Full Cotton Duvet Cover Set (*juego funda nórdica algodón*)
- **Why rejected:** Highest sizing complexity in bedroom textiles — Spanish duvets come in 90, 105, 135, 150, 180, 200 cm variants, each pairing to different pillowcase counts and sizes. That is 6+ base SKUs before colour, before pillowcase count. Return risk is very high (colour accuracy on large fabric surface, and "too small/too big for my duvet" errors). Category is dominated by large bedding brands with strong review moats. Fails the config rules on "products with too many sizes, colours, or variations" and "products dominated by big brands". Revisit only after pillowcase pair (pick #5) has proven Amazon.es listing + return economics.

### B) Electric Heated Throw Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Electronics — outside Rosanube's textile lane and outside the config "Products to Avoid" list explicitly (electronics, products requiring complicated certifications, batteries-adjacent). Requires EU low-voltage / EMC / GPSR-heavy certification, plus flame-resistance and thermal safety documentation. Very seasonal (only sells Nov–Feb). Heavy and bulky → higher FBA tier. Also concentrated in a few known EU brands with strong retail presence.

### C) Round Mandala Beach Towel (*toalla playa redonda mandala*)
- **Why rejected:** Fashion-trend product (per config "products to avoid"). Almost entirely seasonal (May–Aug), with a price race to €8–€12 in the low season that destroys margin. Weak fit with Rosanube's clean/practical/reliable brand feel; heavy PPC competition. High colour-accuracy return risk. Print licensing risk on many mandala designs unless the artwork is original and documented.
