### 1) Waterproof Mattress Protector — Single Bed 90×190 cm

1. **Product name:** Rosanube Waterproof Mattress Protector — fitted, cotton-terry top, TPU backing, single 90×190 cm.
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Protectores de colchón.
3. **Why worth researching:** True replenishment SKU (buyers replace every 1–3 years, often two per household). Sizes on Amazon.es are highly standardised (90, 105, 135, 150) — small variation tree. Low return risk because expectation is functional, not aesthetic. Rosanube can lead with a **soft cotton-terry top** (vs the crinkly PU market default) and precise Spanish sizing — both are frequent complaint themes on incumbent listings.
4. **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent daily volume in *Dormitorio* replenishment. Non-seasonal (small uplift Sept–Oct back-to-school / student housing, minor lift pre-Navidad guest-bed prep). Frequent 4-figure review counts on mid-tier listings suggests healthy monthly velocity.
5. **Competition signals:** Fragmented. A handful of established bedding brands anchor slot 1–4; slots 5–30 are private labels with 500–5,000 reviews at €12–€22 for size 90. Manageable review barrier if the listing is honest and Spanish-native.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - "Plastic feel", crinkly, noisy, hot — the #1 complaint theme.
   - *Antiácaros / hipoalergénico / antibacteriano* claims made without supporting certificates (compliance risk — see config).
   - Size mismatch (advertised 90×190, ships 90×200 or 100×200).
   - Elastic loses tension after a few washes.
   - Poor Spanish copy; care instructions in English only.

   - **Cotton-terry surface** (not smooth PU) with documented composition and GSM.

   - Reinforced 360° elastic; state elastic width (e.g. 15 mm).
   - Care label + fibre composition in Spanish (EU Textile Regulation) + hanging loop.
   - Do **not** claim hypoallergenic / antibacterial / antiácaros — per config, avoid those claims without certificates.
8. **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **single SKU = 90×190 cm** to test listing, PPC, and returns. Once reviews stabilise, add 105×190, 135×190, 150×190 (one parent listing, four variants). Later a **2-pack** for the "guest bed / student" buyer.

2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Alfombras de baño.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Cotton Duvet Cover Set (*juego funda nórdica algodón*)
- **Why rejected:** Extremely high variation complexity — Amazon.es duvet buyers expect 3–5 bed sizes (90, 105, 135, 150, 180) × 2–3 configurations (funda + 1 vs + 2 almohadas) × multiple colours. That's a 30–60 SKU tree before a single sale. Category is anchored by strong bedding brands (Amazon Basics, Pikolin, and several established Spanish home-textile brands) with 10k+ reviews. High return risk from colour, size, and fabric-feel mismatches. Photography, styling, and PPC budget needed far exceed a first Rosanube launch. Per config §Products to Avoid: "Products with too many sizes, colours, or variations" and "Products dominated by big brands".

### B) Kitchen Apron (*delantal cocina algodón*)
- **Why rejected:** Sizing / body-fit ambiguity (one-size-fits-all works only if strap engineering is right — a real complaint driver). High creative-cost burden (needs on-body / lifestyle model photography per config's own warning) — the SKU photographs poorly on a mannequin and expensively with a real model. Category on Amazon.es leans into pattern/print differentiation, pushing toward fashion-adjacent aesthetic risk. Weak repeat-purchase behaviour compared to a paño / servilleta pack. Better as a **bundle add-in** later (apron + waffle towel + muslin) than a standalone launch.

### C) Fitted Cotton Bathrobe (*albornoz algodón adulto*)
- **Why rejected:** High-return-risk apparel with size complexity (S/M/L/XL/XXL × men/women × 2–3 colours), heavy per-unit (600–1,000 g cotton terry → higher FBA tier), and category is anchored by hotel-supply brands with strong pricing. Return rates on bathrobes are notoriously high (fit / colour / feel expectations). Per config §Products to Avoid: "Products with too many sizes / colours / variations", "high return risk", and "very heavy / bulky products with high FBA cost". Explicit config expansion rule bars pyjamas/leggings/thermals but keeps apparel very cautious — a bathrobe fails that filter cleanly.
