## Executive Summary

Today's shortlist stays inside Rosanube's core home / kitchen / bathroom / bedroom textile lane, and adds a first careful step into **table linen** and **bedroom protection**. Common thread across the five:

- **Replenishment or utility purchase** — no fashion-trend dependency.
- **Fragmented Amazon.es competition** — many small private labels, few dominant brands in the sub-niches selected.
- **Weak listings to exploit** — machine-translated Spanish copy, no stated GSM / fabric weight, cluttered mixed-colour packs, and unverified "eco" claims.
- **Preferred €15–€35 price band**, simple 2–4 SKU variation trees.
- **Low compliance friction** — EU textile fibre labelling + care label + GPSR + EPR Spain. Rosanube deliberately avoids *organic / hypoalergénico / antiácaros / antibacterial* claims unless a certificate is on file.

None of the five are recommended for a purchase order today. All are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist** pending Helium 10 (Xray + Cerebro), Keepa 365-day chart, and a real supplier quote with sample.

### 1) Pure-linen tea towel set (4-pack, 45×70 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *paños de cocina lino*, *trapos cocina lino puro*, *paño lino 100%*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Kitchen towels are already a proven replenishment SKU on Amazon.es (last month's #1 pick, waffle-weave, sits in the same shelf). *Pure linen* is a distinct premium sub-niche that solves specific customer complaints about cotton waffles — linen dries faster, does not shed lint, softens with washing, and photographs with a natural, upscale look that supports the Rosanube "clean Iberian home" identity. It also has natural gifting appeal (Reyes, weddings, housewarming) without any seasonal cliff.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent presence of *lino* keywords in Amazon.es kitchen-textile long-tail; several established European small brands (French/Portuguese/Baltic origin) sit in the top 30 with 4-figure review counts, confirming buyer awareness. Search intent is intentional (buyers *choose* linen over cotton), which supports pricing power.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — no Amazon Basics presence in pure-linen kitchen; a handful of small EU brands anchor the top slots at €22–€38 for 3–4 packs. Long-tail slots (30–100) are dominated by generic private labels with weak listings. Review counts often 50–800 for mid-listings → **enterable**.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "100% linen" claimed but GSM not stated (linen buyers care about weight — 180–240 g/m² is the sweet spot).
  - Images shot on cluttered backgrounds; no photo of a folded stack or texture close-up.
  - Colours labelled in English only ("natural", "sand") with poor Spanish translations.
  - Complaints about wrinkling (a *feature* of linen, poorly explained in listings).
  - Pack composition unclear (4 identical vs. 4 different).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 200–230 g/m² stone-washed linen).
  - Stone-washed / pre-softened finish disclosed clearly — resolves the "too stiff" complaint.
  - Muted, single-family palette (natural, salvia, terracotta, off-white); avoid saturated primaries.
  - Explain wrinkling as a natural characteristic in the A+ content (buyer education = fewer returns).
  - Sewn-in hanging loop + kraft belly-band packaging → gift-ready without extra unit cost.
  - Spanish + Portuguese + English care label (Rosanube is a Portugal-based seller shipping to Spain — the multilingual label is honest and low-cost).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 4-pack mixed neutrals, 45×70 cm. Later add a 2-pack starter (€19.90) and a giftable 4-pack "Navidad" edition (kraft box, same product).
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (main risk is landed cost — pure linen is more expensive than cotton, so margin math needs to be re-run before ordering).
- **Score /50:**

  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (premium price band supports margin *if* supplier cost is right)

### 3) Waterproof fitted mattress protector — Spanish sizes 90×190 cm & 150×190 cm

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *funda colchón impermeable ajustable*, *cubrecolchón impermeable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** True replenishment SKU — protectors are replaced every 1–3 years, often triggered by a specific incident (spill, kids, pets). Purchase intent is *high* and *urgent*, which supports PPC conversion. Spanish sizes are well-defined and few (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm × 190/200 cm) → manageable variation tree if we start with just the top 2 sizes.
- **Demand signals:** Consistent daily demand, non-seasonal. Top listings show 4-figure to 5-figure review counts (very established niche) — but the mid-tier (rank 20–100) has plenty of listings with a few hundred reviews and price gaps.
- **Competition signals:** Present. Two/three established brands (Pikolin-adjacent, Velfont) anchor the top slots. However, private-label entry at €18–€28 is common and profitable if quality is real. Amazon Basics has a presence — **caution flag**, but a differentiated product can still enter around the €22–€30 band.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Impermeable" claimed but membrane type not disclosed (PU vs. TPU vs. cheap PVC — different feel, different noise, different durability).
  - Complaints about crinkly / plastic-feeling / noisy backing.
  - Complaints about heat retention (hot to sleep on) — solved by breathable TPU + cotton terry face.
  - Elastic skirt fails at deep mattresses (>25 cm) → returns.
  - "Antiácaros" / "hipoalergénico" used without certificates — a compliance risk Rosanube must **not** replicate.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Cotton terry face + breathable TPU back** (not PVC). State it plainly. Show a corner cross-section photo.
  - Deep-pocket elastic skirt fitted for 30 cm mattresses.
  - Only claim what is documented — "waterproof" and "quiet"; do **not** claim antiácaros/hipoalergénico without OEKO-TEX / certified lab test.
  - Care label + Spanish fibre composition + wash instructions in Spanish/Portuguese/English.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Start with **2 SKUs**: 90×190 cm (individual) and 150×190 cm (matrimonial). Skip 105, 135, 180 for the launch; add them only after the 2 anchor sizes prove out.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (bulkier than a napkin, membrane sourcing needs a real supplier check, brand incumbents exist).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (Amazon Basics presence + brand incumbents)
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (FBA fee eats more here — needs supplier < €5–6 landed for the 90×190)
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 32/50**

## Do Not Pursue (today)

Three products that surfaced during today's browsing but are being **rejected** for Rosanube's launch pipeline. These are recorded so the same rabbit-holes aren't re-explored next month.

### A) Memory-foam / oversized cotton bath mat (60×90 cm, high pile)

- **Why rejected:**
  - **Bulky FBA:** high-pile bath mats occupy oversized FBA size tier → fees eat margin at any realistic sub-€30 retail price.
  - **High return risk:** buyers expect "hotel plush", the delivered product almost always feels thinner than the photo, and colour perception varies under bathroom lighting.
  - **Sensitive to laundering:** shedding and mould complaints are common in Amazon.es reviews of this niche.
  - **Dominated at the top:** several established EU home brands (Vipp-adjacent, Zara Home outlet listings, Kela) sit in top slots — hard to enter at a premium.
  - **Weak Rosanube brand fit** vs. flat-woven towels/napkins that photograph in the "clean Iberian home" style.
- **Possible future revisit:** Only in a **flat-weave cotton** version (not memory foam), standard 50×80 cm size, if a Turkish or Portuguese supplier can hit a sub-€4 landed cost.

### B) Microfiber oversized beach towel with printed design (90×180 cm)

- **Why rejected:**
  - **Extremely seasonal:** demand collapses after mid-August; the report is being written on 2026-08-05 — buying now is chasing the tail of the season with 10 months of storage risk before the next peak.
  - **Design-heavy:** printed towels compete on aesthetics — needs a graphic designer, licence-safe motifs, and constant SKU refreshes. Not a Rosanube fit.
  - **Return risk on colour accuracy:** printed microfiber colour rarely matches the listing photo.
  - **Race-to-the-bottom pricing:** €10–€18 for 90×180 microfiber → thin margin even at €4 landed cost.
  - **Rosanube already has a cleaner path** into the towel category via peshtemal (last month's #2) and waffle hand towels (this month's #4).
- **Possible future revisit:** Only in a **plain-dye peshtemal / fouta** format sold as a "beach + bath + throw" multi-use towel (already covered by last month's report — no new work needed).

### C) Weighted blanket (manta con peso, 6–8 kg, 150×200 cm)

- **Why rejected:**
  - **Heavy → very high FBA fee** in Amazon.es; likely > €7–€9 per unit, which destroys margin at the typical €35–€55 retail band unless supplier cost is unrealistically low.
  - **Compliance / claim risk:** the whole category leans on "calmante", "sensorial", "reduce ansiedad", "mejora el sueño" claims — Rosanube's config explicitly forbids therapeutic / medical claims without documentation.
  - **Return risk:** buyers who dislike the weight sensation return frequently; heavy returns are painful to process.
  - **Size ambiguity:** buyers confuse individual (135) vs. matrimonial (150) vs. throw sizes → sizing returns.
  - **Poor brand fit** with Rosanube's practical / everyday-home positioning.
- **Possible future revisit:** Not planned. This niche is best left to specialised sleep brands with medical partnerships.

## Compliance / FBA / Logistics Notes (applied uniformly to all 5 picks)

- **EU Textile Regulation 1007/2011** fibre composition label required for every product (in Spanish + Portuguese + English is honest and low-cost since Rosanube is Portugal-based, shipping to Spain).

- **GPSR** responsible person + traceability code on packaging.

- **No claims allowed** without documentation on file: *organic, hipoalergénico, antibacterial, antiácaros, medical, therapeutic, chemical-free, eco, made in Portugal, OEKO-TEX*.
- **Amazon.es size / weight** tiers for FBA fee should be confirmed via FBA Calculator per real packed sample — the *estimates* in this report are qualitative.
- **Portugal seller** shipping to Amazon FBA Spain — standard EU intra-community flow; VAT / IVA handled at retail (selling price includes 21% IVA in the margin math).
