### 2) Waterproof Fitted Mattress Protector (Protector de colchón impermeable ajustable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón ajustable*, *funda colchón impermeable 150*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas y protectores de colchón
- **Why this product is worth researching:** True replenishment SKU (replaced after stains, moves, mattress upgrades, new babies, guest bed refresh). Standardised Spanish mattress sizes reduce SKU sprawl (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 — most demand concentrates on 90, 135, 150). Practical, non-decorative — reviews reward function over aesthetics, which suits a private label without model photography or influencer spend. Complements Rosanube's existing pillow-protector research from 2026-07-01 and points to a coherent "sleep protection" family.
- **Demand signals:** Very consistent daily demand with no meaningful seasonality; volume tends to concentrate on the 150×190 cm size (matrimonio) and 90×190 cm (individual). Repeat purchase behaviour when households refresh guest / kids' rooms.
- **Competition signals:** Present but fragmented — a few sleep-focused brands anchor the top spots, but slots 5–30 are held by private labels with 1k–5k reviews. Typical price band **€15–€30** for the 150 cm size in a cotton-terry-top / PU-backed construction.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Impermeable 100%" claimed without a documented waterproof test or certificate.
  - Noisy / crinkly PU backing — the single most common negative-review theme.
  - Fit failures on deep mattresses (buyers with a 30 cm+ topper expect the skirt to fit).
  - Care instructions unclear about tumble-dry temperature (buyers report shrinkage).
  - Multiple listings mixing "protector" and "cubre colchón" without clarifying whether it is waterproof or not.
  - *Antiácaros* / *hipoalergénico* claims used without a certificate — a compliance risk Rosanube should **not** copy.

  - Publish real cotton-top GSM, PU membrane grams, and honest fit depth in cm (e.g. "fits mattresses 20–30 cm deep").
  - Soft cotton-terry surface (no crinkle) with a quieter TPU membrane if the supplier can prove it.
  - Reinforced elastic all around (not just corners) — small, documented quality upgrade.
  - Guarantee correct Spanish mattress sizes (label in cm, listing images with a size chart in Spanish).
  - Do **not** claim *antiácaros* / hypoallergenic / medical — per config compliance rules, only make claims with a certificate.
  - Care card in Spanish with a clear "wash at 60°C, tumble low" line to reduce the shrinkage-complaint category.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket (*manta eléctrica calefactora*)
- **Why rejected:** Electronics + heating element → falls under the config's explicit "avoid electronics / batteries / dangerous-goods risk" rule. EU safety certification (LVD, EMC, RoHS, plug standard, thermal safety) is complex and expensive to document for a small private label. Return risk is high (buyers complain about heat distribution and burnt smells) and category is dominated by established EU electrical brands (Beurer, Imetec, etc.). Also heavily seasonal (Oct–Feb) which the config asks Rosanube to avoid. Zero brand fit with Rosanube's "clean, practical, everyday textile" positioning.

### B) Down / Feather Duvet Insert (*edredón nórdico plumas de plumón*)
- **Why rejected:** Bulky + oversized → high FBA size tier and freight cost, directly violating the config's "very heavy / bulky / high FBA storage cost" rule. Complex size sprawl (Spanish sizes 90 → 200 cm, plus fill weights for verano / entretiempo / invierno) — at least 6–12 SKUs to be credible, which the config explicitly warns against. Allergy return risk is real (feather down triggers complaints even from non-allergic buyers). Category anchored by established EU brands (Pikolin Home, Amazon Basics, Naf Naf, ForenTex) at prices that are already margin-compressed. Not the right entry point for a Portugal-based private label.

### C) Vacuum Compression Storage Bags with Pump (*bolsas vacío almacenaje ropa*)
- **Why rejected:** Not a textile — plastic film + valve product. Category is dominated by 2–3 generic brands racing prices to €10–€15 for a 10-pack, so margins are already compressed. High complaint rate around valve leakage and film puncture (real failure mode, not a photography problem — hard to differentiate). Poor Rosanube brand fit (plastic storage does not sit visually with cotton textiles on a storefront). Damage risk in transit (a punctured bag arriving to a customer is a guaranteed return). Also flagged by the config's "very low differentiation opportunity" rule.
