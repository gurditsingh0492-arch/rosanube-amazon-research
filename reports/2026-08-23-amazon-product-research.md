### 2) 100% Cotton Fitted Sheet — Spanish Sizes (*Sábana Bajera Ajustable*)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera 100% algodón*, *sábana bajera 135 algodón*, *sábana bajera 150 ajustable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas y fundas de almohada › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are a **utility replenishment** SKU with predictable, non-seasonal demand. Spanish bed sizes are simple and standardised (90, 105, 135, 150, 160 cm), which lets Rosanube start with **one hero size** and expand — avoiding the variation trap that kills margin on full sábana sets. Cotton (not "microfibra") in the €18–€30 band is under-supplied vs. cheap microfibre and premium percal.
- **Demand signals:** One of the highest-frequency search categories inside Ropa de hogar. Non-seasonal, buyer replaces every 12–36 months and often buys 2 per bed.
- **Competition signals:** Manageable in the cotton segment. Big brands like Pikolin / Belnou / Amazon Basics anchor the top, but the €18–€28 100% cotton band has many private labels with 300–2 000 reviews — enterable. Microfibre segment is a price war and should be avoided.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Composition ambiguous — "algodón" claimed but actually cotton-polyester blend, driving 1-star reviews.
  - Deep pockets not deep enough for Spanish 30 cm mattresses.
  - Elastic gives out after ~20 washes (repeat complaint).
  - Colour swatches inaccurate.
  - Multi-lingual titles with English/German keyword stuffing hurting Spanish CTR.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **True 100% cotton**, GSM stated, thread count optional but avoid inflated figures.
  - Pocket depth 30–35 cm labelled explicitly (matches modern Spanish mattress thickness).
  - Reinforced 360° elastic — small, provable quality signal.
  - Neutral Iberian palette (blanco natural, arena, salvia, gris piedra) — 3–4 colours only.
  - Clean Spanish-first listing (no keyword stuffing).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 135×190 cm (most-common Spanish "cama de matrimonio individual"), one colour = blanco. Add 150×190 cm next, then a 2-pack option for the "guest room" buyer.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (sizing errors and elastic failure are the top return drivers — sample QC critical)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable rizo*, *protector colchón 135 impermeable*

- **Why worth researching:** Boring, functional, high repeat-purchase. Bought by parents (kids' rooms), pet owners, and every buyer after a mattress purchase. Terry-top + TPU membrane is now the standard; the category is fragmented, sizing is simple (mirrors bajera sizes), and it fits Rosanube's "practical, reliable" positioning cleanly. **Note the compliance line:** we can say "impermeable" (functional claim, testable) but not *antiácaros* / hypoallergenic / medical without documented certificates.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**

## Compliance & FBA Notes (all 5 SKUs)

- **EU Textile Regulation 1007/2011**: fibre composition in Spanish on the label — mandatory for all five.
- **Care label**: Spanish, wash-cycle, temperature.
- **Country of origin**: on label + listing.
- **GPSR / EU responsible person**: required — confirm Rosanube's EU responsible-person setup covers these SKUs.
- **EPR (Spain + Portugal)**: packaging registration must be current before FBA shipment.
- **No claims** — do not write *antiácaros*, *hipoalergénico*, *antibacterial*, *ecológico*, *orgánico*, *made in Portugal*, *hecho a mano* on any listing unless the supplier delivers documented evidence (per config §Compliance).
- **FBA size tier expectations (to be confirmed with real packed dims):**

  - Fitted sheet → standard, moderate fee.
  - Mattress protector (135) → standard to slightly oversized depending on packed compression; the impermeable membrane inflates pack volume — get packed dims from supplier before committing.
