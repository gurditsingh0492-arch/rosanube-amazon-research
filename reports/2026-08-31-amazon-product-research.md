1. **Product name:** 100% Cotton Percale Fitted Sheet, deep pocket, single-SKU per size
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas y fundas de almohada › Sábanas bajeras
3. **Why worth researching:** Bedroom refresh SKU with a strong autumn uplift on Amazon.es. Fitted sheets are one of the highest-repeat bedroom purchases (worn edges, elastic failure, wanting a spare set). The Spanish market has clear standard bed sizes (90, 105, 135, 150, 160×200 cm) — variation complexity is contained, and the product is flat, light, and folds small (good FBA fee tier).
4. **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent replenishment, visible autumn refresh spike (September–October), high 4-figure review counts on the top listings, evergreen "sábana bajera 150" and "sábana bajera 135" keyword families.
5. **Competition signals:** Anchored by a few large bedding brands (Amazon Basics-style, Pikolin-adjacent, generic Portuguese/Spanish mills), but slots 5–30 are highly fragmented with small private labels and imports. Not brand-dominated in the same way silk pillowcases are.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - Fibre content ambiguous or misleading ("algodón" listings that are actually microfibre polyester).
   - Elastic strap only 3-sided or shallow pocket (fits 25–28 cm mattresses badly — recurring complaint on Spanish thick pocket mattresses).
   - Sizes stated in inconsistent formats (some cm, some UK/US).
   - Colour swatches inaccurate vs. delivered colour.
   - Weak GSM / thread-count transparency.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Publish honest **thread count** (target 200–250 percale) and **weight** (≈120–130 g/m²).

   - 3 neutral colours only (blanco, natural, gris piedra) — no 12-colour split, no seasonal prints.
   - Spanish size labels in cm on the packaging + care symbol legend on the insert.
   - Kraft belly-band with washing / ironing instructions (Spanish + Portuguese + English).
8. **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as **single-size SKUs** (start with 135×200 and 150×200 cm — the two biggest Spanish sizes). After 30-day sales prove demand, add 90×200 and 160×200. Later, cross-sell a **"sábana bajera + 2 fundas de almohada"** set — natural bundle path.
