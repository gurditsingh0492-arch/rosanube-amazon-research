## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Percale Cotton Fitted Sheet — Single SKU, Standard Spanish Sizes

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera ajustable algodón percal*, *sabana bajera 150 algodon*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Bajeras ajustables
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are the single most **replenishment-friendly** bedroom textile on Amazon.es. They wear out, stain, and get replaced every 12–24 months, and Spanish beds standardise around a small size set (90, 105, 135, 150, 160×200 cm — mostly deep 30–35 cm), which keeps the variation tree manageable. Percale (crisp, breathable, 200-thread-count-ish) is what most Spanish buyers actually want post-summer, and it's an easier product to describe truthfully than "microfibre" or "satin" alternatives.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Top listings in *bajeras ajustables* routinely show 4-figure review counts; the sub-category is consistently populated in the *Sábanas* best-seller ranks. Demand is essentially year-round with a modest lift in September (autumn refresh) and January (Reyes / white sales).
- **Competition signals:** Anchored at the very top by a handful of big brands (Amazon Basics, Pikolin, Utopia Bedding-style imports) but the **slot 6–30 range is highly fragmented** — plenty of small private labels with 200–1,500 reviews and mediocre listings. Price band €10–€20 for a single fitted sheet, €18–€35 for a bajera + 2 fundas set.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "100% algodón" claimed but composition tag ships as cotton-poly — common review complaint.
  - Thread count either not stated or wildly inflated (1800 hilos claims on obvious microfibre).
  - Depth ("bolsillo profundo") not stated in cm — mattresses in Spain vary 25–35 cm.
  - Elastic only around corners, not full-perimeter → poor fit reviews.
  - Colour drift after 3–5 washes.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Honest percale + honest thread count (e.g. 200 hilos, 100% cotton) with the composition label photographed in the listing.
  - Full-perimeter elastic + clearly stated pocket depth (up to 30 cm or up to 35 cm variants).
  - Muted Iberian neutrals (crudo, salvia, arena, blanco roto) — 4 colours only.
  - Kraft belt packaging with wash-care instructions in Spanish + Portuguese.
  - Include a **small size chart card** inside the pack that also lists Rosanube's other bedroom SKUs (funda almohada, protector) → future cross-sell.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as a **single-SKU fitted sheet** in 135, 150, 160 (three most common Spanish adult sizes) × 3 neutral colours. Once reviews stabilise (~200), add a "bajera + 2 fundas" set at €24.90 for the same three sizes.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (sheets have real return risk when sizing / fibre-composition claims miss).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 5/10 (top slots owned; slots 6–30 open)
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (cotton cost + fabric weight bump FBA fee)
  - Risk: 6/10 (return risk if size/composition claim is wrong)
  - **Total: 33/50**
- **Decision:** **Further Research** — highest-demand item on today's list, but needs Helium 10 to confirm the slot 6–30 opportunity is real, and a supplier quote to check if margin survives after FBA fee for a size-tier "large standard" polybag.

### 2) Cotton Dinner Napkins — 6-pack, Woven (not printed)

- **Spanish keyword:** *servilletas tela algodón*, *servilletas de tela lavables*, *set servilletas comedor*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Textiles de mesa › Servilletas
- **Why worth researching:** Cloth napkins are a **small-item, low-FBA-fee, high-repeat, gift-friendly** SKU. They pair naturally with the *linen-look table runner* (SKU #4 from the July 1 report) into a "Mesa Rosanube" gift bundle for Navidad / Reyes / anniversaries. Amazon.es napkin listings are dominated by either polyester restaurant-supply packs or over-decorated seasonal prints — a **clean, honest cotton woven napkin** in muted colours sits in an underserved slot.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round with a strong uplift Nov–Jan (Navidad table dressing) and a smaller lift around Semana Santa / mother's day. Napkins are also a "top-up" purchase after a runner/tablecloth sale — good AOV builder.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Top listings are a mix of polyester restaurant-grade packs (€8–€15 for 12) and imported cotton sets (€14–€24 for 4–6). Review counts commonly 100–1,500. No dominant Spanish or EU household brand.

- **Demand signals:** Steady, non-seasonal. Bath mats are replaced every 12–24 months (worn, discoloured, matted). Cotton chenille listings show consistent 4-figure review counts on Amazon.es for the leading options.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Backing peels / detaches after 3–5 machine washes — top complaint.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

- **Why rejected:** Electronic product with a heating element → **explicit violation** of config §"Products to Avoid" (electronics, batteries, safety-regulated goods). CE/EMC/LVD compliance burden, high return risk from any perceived hazard, and Amazon.es has category restrictions for electrical bedding items. No brand-fit with Rosanube's textile-only positioning.

### B) Blackout Curtain Panels (*cortinas opacas dormitorio*)
- **Why rejected:** Size / length variation tree is huge (135×175, 135×250, 145×260, 200×260, 250×260, etc. — Spain has no single default), each SKU carries its own FNSKU + stock risk. Bulky and heavy → high FBA size-tier and freight cost, plus wrinkle-return complaints. Category is anchored by a handful of large brands (AmazonBasics, Deconovo-style imports) with 20k+ reviews. Weak differentiation for a private-label first launch. Violates config's "too many sizes / variations" and "bulky products" rules.

### C) Baby Muslin Swaddle Wrap Set (*muselina bebé envolver recién nacido*)
- **Why rejected:** Baby / infant sleep-adjacent product → GPSR + safety-regulated (product-safety file, potential EN 71 / textile-safety documentation, suffocation-warning labelling, high return-and-complaint sensitivity). Even though the product is a textile, the compliance and reputational risk of any infant sleep item is significantly higher than Rosanube's current setup should absorb. Rosanube can revisit only with a documented supplier certification package and a full compliance review.
