### 2) Linen-Look Cushion Cover 2-pack, 45×45 cm (Fundas de cojín efecto lino)

- **Why worth researching:** Cushion covers are the classic low-cost home refresh — customers buy 2 or 4 at a time and update seasonally. The **45×45 cm** size is the Spanish/European standard, sidestepping the US 18×18 in confusion. Textile-only (no filler) means very light FBA parcel, low returns (no size fit anxiety), and easy bundling with the future Rosanube table runner SKU for a "Salón + Comedor" gift box. Fits the brand's "decor-adjacent but not fashion" boundary.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Lino" claimed for 100% polyester — recurring 1★ complaint driver, and a *Ley de Textiles* fibre-label risk.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Heated / Electric Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Electrical product — sits inside the config's **explicit "avoid electronics"** rule, plus REACH + LVD + EMC + Spanish energy compliance overhead. High return risk (thermostat complaints, cable smell, safety fears). Dominated by established brands (Imetec, Beurer, Medisana). Wrong fit for Rosanube's textile-only lane.

### B) Silk Bed Sheet Set (*juego sábanas seda*)
- **Why rejected:** Category dominated by beauty-adjacent premium brands with 5,000+ reviews and momme-count credibility. Extremely high return risk (colour, feel, "is this real silk" doubt). Certifications expected (OEKO-TEX, momme grade, origin) — per config compliance rules Rosanube cannot make these claims without documented certificates. Very high unit cost → thin margin after PPC / returns. Also crosses into fashion-adjacent complexity Rosanube is asked to stay out of.

### C) Bamboo Fibre Kitchen Towels (*paños de cocina fibra bambú*)
- **Why rejected:** "Bamboo fabric" claims on Amazon are widely misleading — most so-called bamboo textile is actually **viscose de bambú** under EU textile regulation, and mislabelling it as "bambú" is a live compliance risk (Amazon.es takedown risk, ES consumer authority risk). Category also has weak repeat behaviour vs. cotton or waffle, and high 1★ review density around "not real bamboo" and "smelly after 3 washes". Better options exist in the cotton and waffle sub-niches Rosanube is already targeting.
