## Executive Summary

Late September in Spain is the seasonal handoff — bedroom and bathroom textiles start pulling ahead of summer categories, and Christmas / Reyes gift patterns begin to bias table linen and giftable kitchen sets. Today's five picks lean into that window while staying inside Rosanube's core lane (bathroom, bedroom, kitchen, and table textiles). Shared traits:

- Non-fashion textile essentials, replenishment-friendly, low variation complexity.

## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Percale Cotton Duvet Cover Set — Cama 90 & Cama 135, plain colours

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda nórdica algodón 100%*, *funda nórdica cama 135 lisa*, *funda nórdica percal*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas nórdicas
- **Why worth researching:** Duvet covers enter their high-demand window in Spain from mid-September through February. Plain, colour-blocked percale (200 TC) is the "safe, adult, non-print" segment that big brands under-serve because they push their own printed designs. Rosanube's "clean Iberian home" positioning (natural, salvia, terracotta, off-white) fits an under-served slot between the character-print end and the €80+ premium end.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Duvet covers consistently sit in the Dormitorio best-seller lists; the "funda nórdica cama 135" keyword is one of the highest-volume bedding searches on Amazon.es (Spanish standard double).
- **Competition signals:** Present but fragmented. Amazon Basics and one or two mid-market Spanish brands anchor the top, but slots 10–40 are private label. Common price band €22–€38 for a Cama 135 set (funda + funda de almohada).
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thread count and fabric weight either exaggerated ("400 TC" on visibly thin percale) or not stated at all.
  - Colour on-photo does not match delivered colour (top review complaint).
  - Zippers or button closures fail after few washes.
  - Cama 90 vs. Cama 135 vs. Cama 150 confusion in the title.
  - Machine-translated Spanish bullet copy; care instructions in English only.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish honest 200-TC percale, 115 g/m², pre-washed to reduce shrink.
  - Reinforced YKK-equivalent zipper with a documented photo close-up.
  - 4 muted colours only at launch (blanco roto, arena, salvia, terracota); avoid loud prints.
  - Every listing photo shot on a real Spanish bedroom set (Cama 135, wooden headboard).
  - Bilingual ES/PT care label; kraft belly-band with cm dimensions.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch Cama 135 first as the volume SKU (funda nórdica 220×220 + 1 funda de almohada 45×110). Add Cama 90 (155×220 + 1 × 45×90) once first SKU has ≥50 reviews. Delay Cama 150 / 180 until unit economics are proven — too many variations early.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium**
- **Score /50:**

  - Competition: 5/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (FBA fee sensitive at this size)
  - Risk: 6/10 (returns can be high if colour photo is off)

### 2) Waffle Cotton Bathrobe — Unisex Kimono Style, Lightweight

- **Spanish keyword:** *albornoz waffle algodón*, *albornoz unisex ligero*, *albornoz kimono*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Albornoces
- **Why worth researching:** Traditional terry bathrobes are heavy, slow-drying, and hot for Spanish apartments and hotels. Waffle-weave, lightweight, unisex kimono style is the "spa / boutique hotel" segment — sits well with the Iberian-minimalist aesthetic Rosanube is trying to own, and demand climbs from October onward. Kimono cut avoids the "many sizes" trap: S/M and L/XL usually cover the market.
- **Demand signals:** Autumn uplift begins now; bathrobes are gift-friendly for Navidad / Reyes; strong repeat-purchase in households replacing an old terry robe.
- **Competition signals:** Terry bathrobes are crowded and brand-anchored; **waffle** bathrobes are noticeably thinner. Price band for waffle: €28–€45. Review counts on the top waffle listings sit in the 300–1,500 range — realistic entry.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fabric weight (GSM) never disclosed; buyers cannot tell "spa quality" from "beach cover-up".
  - Sizing wildly inconsistent between listings (S at one seller = L at another).
  - Waist-tie loops tear off after washes (frequent review complaint).
  - Photography leans on stock spa imagery rather than a real product shot.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 300–360 g/m² waffle cotton).
  - Only 2 sizes (S/M, L/XL) and 3 colours (blanco, arena, gris piedra) — deliberately narrow variation tree.
  - Reinforced belt-loop stitching, small documented change; call it out on image 3.
  - Real product photography on a mid-adult Spanish model + lifestyle bathroom shot.
  - Kraft-belt packaging with dimensions and washing icons; "spa-in-casa" positioning.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single SKU per size/colour at launch. Do **not** attempt a matching "his + hers" bundle until first SKU has ≥100 reviews (variation trees at launch murder ranking).
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium**
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 7/10 (seasonal uplift now, softer in summer)
  - Competition: 6/10 (waffle sub-segment less crowded than terry)
  - Differentiation: 8/10 (real GSM + narrow palette + real photography)
  - Margin potential: 7/10 (light fabric → lower FBA vs. terry)
  - Risk: 5/10 (sizing complaints can drive returns; needs sample first)
  - **Total: 33/50**

### 3) Anti-Slip Cotton Bath Mat — 2-piece set (rug + contour), tufted

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón antideslizante*, *alfombrilla baño 2 piezas*, *set alfombra baño*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Bath mats are a bathroom essential replaced every 12–24 months. The **2-piece set** (a 50×80 cm rectangular + a 50×50 cm contour for around the toilet) is a bundle format Spanish buyers actively search for — it saves them from buying two separately. Cotton tufted with a rubberised anti-slip backing is a mature format with clear differentiation levers (pile density, backing quality, palette).
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round; small autumn uplift as buyers refresh bathrooms.
- **Competition signals:** Amazon.es mid-list is dominated by generic Chinese microfiber "shaggy" mats. The **cotton tufted** sub-segment is thinner. Price band €18–€32 for a 2-piece set. Review counts often 100–800 — reachable.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Anti-slip backing fails after 3–5 washes (most-cited complaint).
  - Pile flattens quickly; density (g/m²) never stated.
  - Colours arrive different from photo.
  - Dimensions sometimes wrong (advertise 50×80, ship 45×75).
  - Missing washing instructions in Spanish.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real pile density (target 1,800–2,200 g/m²) and confirm it in listing image 4.
  - Better anti-slip backing (documented — TPR dot pattern, not painted spray).
  - 3 muted colours (arena, salvia, gris humo). Skip white — hides poorly, high return risk.
  - Bilingual care label; machine-washable at 30°C without backing damage.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as **one SKU = 2-piece set**. Later add a 3-piece set (adds a 40×60 cm) at a €5 premium. Do **not** launch single-piece — the 2-piece is the bundle differentiator.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium**
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 7/10
  - Competition: 7/10 (cotton tufted less crowded than microfiber shag)
  - Differentiation: 8/10 (real density + real backing + palette)
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 7/10 (low return risk if anti-slip holds; sample-critical)

### 4) Absorbent Microfiber Dish Drying Mat — 40×50 cm, kitchen counter

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombrilla escurreplatos*, *tapete escurridor cocina*, *alfombrilla secado platos microfibra*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y comedor › Textiles de cocina (crosses into Utensilios de cocina)
- **Why worth researching:** A "utility" textile Spanish kitchens increasingly buy to replace plastic dish racks or protect wood/quartz counters under a rack. Small footprint, light, ships flat, very low FBA fee. Repeat-purchase (they stain and wear). Under-served on Amazon.es — most listings are unbranded generic microfiber with weak photography.
- **Demand signals:** Steady demand year-round; small pre-Navidad uplift when buyers refresh kitchens; strong repeat pattern (stained → replaced).
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented, small private labels, few brand names. Price band €9–€18 per mat, or €14–€22 for a 2-pack. Review counts often <500 — clear entry.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Absorbency claims ("super absorbent") with no ml/g figure.
  - Backing is plastic PVC that curls after a few washes.
  - Colours limited to grey / white / black — no palette variety.
  - Ships folded into a small polybag with wrinkles that never fully flatten (common review complaint).
  - Dimensions in cm often inconsistent between the listing title and the delivered product.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real absorbency (measured in g water per m² retained), tested and stated.
  - Non-PVC rubberised backing that lies flat out of the box.
  - 3 muted colours (piedra, salvia, arena) + a natural cotton-loop edge for the "linen-look" segment.
  - Rolled (not folded) packaging in a kraft sleeve so it arrives flat.
  - Bilingual care label.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as a **2-pack of 40×50 cm** at €16.90 (hits the €15–€35 band even in single-mat territory), then add a large single 50×80 cm at €14.90 for buyers who want a counter-length runner. Skip small 30×40 — no headroom.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (light, non-fragile, low return, low compliance)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 6/10 (real but smaller absolute volume than duvet covers)
  - Competition: 8/10 (thin, weak listings)
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 8/10 (very low FBA, ships flat, low unit cost)
  - Risk: 9/10 (low FBA risk, low return risk, low compliance risk)

- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Cojines › Cojines de silla
- **Why worth researching:** A 4-pack matches the standard Spanish dining table setup. It's a low-glamour but high-repeat textile Spanish households buy in packs, then re-buy when covers stain or foam flattens. Fabric-only version (cotton cover + cotton-poly fill, no memory foam) is simpler to source, lighter for FBA, and avoids the memory-foam brand-dominated segment.
- **Demand signals:** Consistent year-round; small pre-Navidad uplift for hosting; gift-adjacent (Reyes for a new apartment).
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented but crowded — many low-priced 4-packs at €18–€28. A few brand-anchored premium options (memory foam) at €35+. The "cotton, plain, muted-palette" 4-pack slot is thinner than the printed-flower slot.

  - Kraft-band packaging for the 4-pack; bilingual care label.

## Do Not Pursue (3 rejected today)

### R1) Weighted Blanket (manta con peso) — 5 kg / 7 kg

- **Reason rejected:** Very heavy (bulky FBA tier → punishing fulfilment fee), high return rate (buyers pick wrong weight for their body), brand-anchored top-of-search (Kuddly, Levia, YnM), and product borders on "wellness" claims (sleep aid / anxiety) which are compliance-risky for a private-label seller without medical evidence. Also expensive to freight from China (weight-based cost destroys margin).
- **Decision:** NO GO for Rosanube's first year. Watchlist only for future once brand has premium-textile credibility.

### R2) Silver-Ion "Antibacterial" Gym / Sport Towel

- **Reason rejected:** "Antibacterial" and "silver-ion" claims trigger EU biocide / cosmetic regulations (BPR — Biocidal Products Regulation) and require documented substantiation Rosanube does not have. Also outside brand lane (sport / fitness, not home / kitchen / bathroom textile). High customer-complaint rate when the antibacterial effect wears off. Confirms as legal-risk category, not just marketing-risk.
- **Decision:** Rejected. Do not launch without a certified antibacterial substrate + registered biocide claim (and even then, off-brand).

### R3) Licensed / Character-Print Kids' Duvet Cover (Disney, Marvel, football clubs)

- **Reason rejected:** IP / licensing risk is severe — Amazon suspensions are frequent in this sub-category, and legitimate licences are prohibitively expensive for a small private label. Even "inspired by" designs draw takedowns. High return rate on kid-print sizing errors. Off-brand for Rosanube's "clean Iberian home" positioning.
- **Decision:** Rejected. IP-safe alternative would be plain / geometric kids' duvet covers, and even that stays on Watchlist until the adult Cama 135 SKU (Product 1) proves out.
