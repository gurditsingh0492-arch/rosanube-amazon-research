### 1) Cotton Terry Hand Towel Set (6-pack, 50×90 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *juego toallas de mano algodón*, *toallas mano rizo pack*, *toallas lavabo pack 6*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Toallas de mano
- **Why worth researching:** Hand towels are the highest-frequency-replaced towel size in a Spanish home (kitchen, lavabo, guest bathroom, WC). A 6-pack matches how Spanish buyers actually shop this SKU (one per bathroom + spares). Simple sizing (single dimension, no bedding fit issues) and low return risk make it a strong "workhorse" first SKU.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent presence in *Toallas de mano* top results year-round; steady demand tied to household replenishment, no seasonal collapse. Multiple listings with 4-figure review counts.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Amazon Basics anchors the low end (~€12 for 6-pack), but the €18–€28 band is populated by many small private labels with 200–2,500 reviews — realistic entry with a clear quality-and-brand story. Big established towel houses (Vossen, Möve) sit above €35 as premium.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM stated inconsistently or not at all (many list "500 g/m²" without proof).
  - Photos show only folded towels — no in-context bathroom shot, no scale.
  - Complaints about linting for the first 5 washes.
  - "Colour pack" listings ship 6 identical colours but marketed as assorted, or vice versa — angry reviews.
  - Machine-translated Spanish (*"toalla suave para uso"*).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish honest, verified GSM (target 500–550 g/m² for the mid tier).
  - Pre-washed / low-lint disclosure ("bajo pelusa desde el primer lavado").
  - Muted neutral palette: blanco, arena, terracota, salvia — no saturated primaries.
  - Clearly labelled pack composition (2× blanco + 2× arena + 2× salvia, for example) with a hero image showing exactly the 6 items delivered.
  - Care + fibre label in Spanish and Portuguese; hanging loop reinforced.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch one SKU = 6-pack, 50×90 cm, one colour combo. Later add: a "guest" 4-pack in a giftable kraft sleeve, and a matching 2-pack of bath towels (70×140 cm) once reviews stabilise.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "100% algodón" claimed but reviews report shrinkage after first wash (suggesting pre-shrink not done).
