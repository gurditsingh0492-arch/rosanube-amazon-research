### 1) Swedish Dishcloth Multi-pack (cellulose + cotton, reusable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas suecas*, *paños esponja reutilizables*, *bayeta celulosa algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y mantenimiento del hogar › Bayetas y esponjas (crosses over to Textiles de cocina)
- **Why worth researching:** Reusable cellulose-cotton dishcloths are one of the cleanest "eco-swap for paper towels" stories on Amazon.es and a genuine replenishment SKU — customers rebuy every 2–4 months. Extremely lightweight (≈15 g/unit), tiny FBA footprint, no size complexity, no colour returns. Fits Rosanube's "clean, practical, comfortable" positioning perfectly and pairs later with the waffle kitchen towel SKU from the 2026-07-01 shortlist.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Steady year-round; category has been building since ~2022 as Spanish shoppers migrate from paper towels. Not a fad — the same buyer keeps rebuying multi-packs.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — mostly small private labels and a few Nordic-origin brands (Wettex-style). Price band €9–€18 for 6–10 packs. Review counts commonly 200–3,000 → entry realistic.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Composition unclear: many list "cellulose" without stating cotton %; some are 100% viscose.
  - Print designs are loud / cartoonish — no clean adult-home aesthetic.
  - No mention of biodegradability evidence (avoid this claim unless certified — per config compliance rules).
  - Complaints about smell after 3–4 uses, stiffness out of the box, or falling apart in the dishwasher.
  - Pack size often 5 units — awkward for buyers who want a "one per week of the month" cadence.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real composition (target 70% cellulose / 30% cotton) on label and listing.
  - Muted / neutral palette (natural, salvia, terracotta, off-white) with subtle line pattern — matches the Rosanube waffle kitchen towel palette.
  - Pack of **8** (2 months at 1/week) or **6 + 2 bonus** → clearer value story than "5-pack".
  - Kraft-paper belt around the pack (no plastic sleeve), care instructions in Spanish + Portuguese + English.
  - Do NOT claim antibacterial, biodegradable, or compostable without a supplier certificate.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch a single hero SKU (8-pack, mixed neutrals). Later add a "kitchen refresh" bundle with 2 waffle kitchen towels + 8 Swedish dishcloths in a kraft box — cross-sell to Rosanube's kitchen textile buyer.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**

  - Competition: 7/10

  - Margin potential: 7/10

### 2) Waterproof Mattress Protector — Terry Cotton, Fitted (Spanish sizes)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubrecolchón impermeable rizo algodón*, *protector colchón 135 / 150 / 90*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas y protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** Boring, high-repeat, high-utility bedroom SKU with clear standardised Spanish sizes (90×190, 105×190, 135×190, 150×190, 180×200). Buyers replace them roughly every 12–24 months. Waterproof + breathable terry-cotton is the sweet spot: it solves the classic "plastic-crinkle" complaint that dominates negative reviews across the sub-niche.
- **Demand signals:** Consistent daily demand. Uplift around move-in season (Sep–Oct student rentals, Jun–Jul relocations) but not seasonal-dependent. Family buyers often purchase 2–3 at once (parents' + kids' beds).
- **Competition signals:** Present but not dominated — a few big bedding brands (Pikolin-adjacent, Belnou, Amazon Basics) hold the top of Best Sellers, but slots 10–40 are dense with private labels doing 4-figure reviews. Price band €14–€28 depending on size. Realistic entry point.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Impermeable" claimed but reviews report leaks after 5–10 washes → real quality gap.
  - PU membrane makes noise / crinkles ("suena como una bolsa").
  - Elastic corners tear after a few laundry cycles.
  - Sizing inconsistencies: some fit 135×190, some 140×200 despite same label.
  - Compliance risk: many use *antiácaros* / *hipoalergénico* without certificates → Rosanube must NOT copy those claims.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Terry-cotton top (200–220 g/m², documented), PU membrane, deep skirt (30 cm) with reinforced full-perimeter elastic.
  - Publish honest wash count (e.g. "waterproofing tested to 60 industrial washes at 60°C" — only if supplier provides a lab test).
  - Guarantee correct Spanish mattress dimensions (label + packaging in cm, in Spanish).
  - Do NOT claim *antiácaros* / *hipoalergénico* / *antibacteriano* unless a certificate is uploaded.
  - Show the "silent test" in listing images — no plastic sound.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one hero size = 150×190** (Spanish matrimonio standard); add 90×190 (single) and 135×190 next once reviews stabilise. Keep the variation tree ≤ 5 sizes, 1 colour (white). Never launch coloured variants of a protector.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (variation count is the main risk; keep it disciplined)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (moderate FBA fee at 150×190)
  - Risk: 7/10
  - **Total: 35/50**

Also rides the same "reduce paper waste" tailwind as the Swedish dishcloth (Product #1).

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Adult Bathrobe — Waffle or Terry (*albornoz adulto*)
- **Why rejected:** Introduces the very apparel-sizing pain the config tells Rosanube to avoid — 3–5 sizes × 2–3 colours × 2 fabrics = ≥12 SKUs from day one. Fit-related returns are the number-one complaint driver in Amazon.es bathrobe reviews. Sub-niche is anchored by established EU home-textile brands (Möve, Kinzler-style) with hundreds to thousands of reviews. High packed weight (500–900 g) → higher FBA fee tier than a bath towel. Model photography likely required. Fails the "low variation, low return risk" filter even though it's inside the core bath-textile lane.

### B) Duvet Cover Set 3-piece for 150-cm Bed (*funda nórdica 3 piezas cama 150*)
- **Why rejected:** Variation nightmare — Amazon.es duvet covers need 90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 180 / 200 cm × 4–6 colours × prints, easily 30+ SKUs before you have coverage. Category is dominated by big Spanish bedding brands (Barceló, Naf Naf Home, Pierre Cardin-licensed) and Amazon Basics. High packed volume → high FBA size tier and heavy freight cost per unit. High colour-return risk (buyers complain "not the colour in the photo"). Also carries IP risk with licensed prints. Explicitly violates the config's "avoid products with too many sizes / colours" rule.

### C) Ironing Board Cover (*funda tabla de planchar*)
- **Why rejected:** Very low ASP (€6–€12), thin margins, and dominated by Vileda / Leifheit / Rayen with strong brand loyalty and shelf presence beyond Amazon. FBA fee eats most of the margin at this price point. Multiple non-standard board sizes make sizing a real return-risk driver. Weak differentiation opportunity — buyers pick on price, not brand. Fails the config's "avoid selling price under €10 unless bundle economics are excellent" and "avoid products dominated by big brands" rules.
