## Executive Summary

Today's shortlist stays entirely inside Rosanube's core textile lane (kitchen, bath, bed, table). The five picks share:

- **Replenishment demand:** kitchen, bath, and bed textiles are consumables — buyers replace them 1–3× per year.
- **Fragmented competition:** many small private-label sellers, few dominant EU brands in the target sub-niches.
- **Concrete listing weaknesses** Rosanube can exploit: unstated GSM/composition, machine-translated Spanish copy, weak lifestyle photography, ambiguous pack composition, and repeat quality complaints (shrinkage, lint, colour drift).
- **Preferred price band €15–€35** with a simple size/colour variation tree.
- **Low compliance friction:** EU textile fibre labelling + Spanish care label + GPSR responsible person + EPR Spain packaging registration. No organic / OEKO-TEX / hypoallergenic claims until certificates are on file.

No product below is recommended for a purchase order today. All five are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist** pending Helium 10 verification, Keepa BSR stability, supplier quotes, and FBA calculator numbers.

### 1) Muslin (100% cotton gauze) Baby & Adult Bath Towel — 2-pack, 100×100 cm

- **Spanish keyword:** *toalla muselina algodón*, *toalla gasa bebé adulto*, *toalla muselina 6 capas*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Toallas de baño (secondary: Bebé › Baño)
- **Why worth researching:** Multi-layer cotton-gauze towels (typically 4–6 layers) are winning shelf share on Amazon.es away from classic terry: they are softer after wash, dry faster, and are much lighter — an important point for Spanish flats with limited drying space. The category is still dominated by baby-only listings, but a "family / adult-usable" muselina position is under-served and unlocks a higher price point.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to verify):** Muselina keyword growth over the last 12–18 months on ES; steady non-seasonal demand from baby-shower buyers with a genuine crossover into adult-buyer households; frequent reorder driven by baby-textile lifecycle (0–24 months).
- **Competition signals:** Moderate. Many small brands, some Turkish-origin, review counts often in the 200–2,500 band for the leading SKUs. Very few EU-brand household names in this specific gauze-towel sub-niche. Price band roughly €18–€32 for 2-pack of large gauze towels.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "6-layer muslin" claimed without cross-section photography or GSM.
  - Baby-only imagery — leaves the adult / face-towel / hair-wrap use case invisible.
  - Machine-translated Spanish ("toalla del bebé") vs. natural ES copy.
  - Colours drift after 2–3 washes (common review complaint).
  - Sizes vaguely stated (some list 90×90, some 100×100, some 110×110 — buyer confusion).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target 320–380 g/m² for 6-layer 100×100) with a cross-section macro shot.
  - Position it as a **family multi-use towel** (baby bath, adult face/hair, gym, travel, guest towel) — not baby-only.
  - Muted neutral palette (natural, sage, terracotta, soft grey) — no pastel baby prints.
  - Reinforced stitched border + hanging loop.
  - Kraft belly-band with Spanish/English/Portuguese care and use guide.
- **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch a single SKU = 2-pack, 100×100 cm, mixed neutrals, €24.90. Later: 4-pack "family" set €42.90 and a 70×70 cm "face & guest" 3-pack €19.90.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (low return risk; main risk is colour-accuracy complaints).

### 2) Fitted Bedsheet (sábana bajera ajustable) — 100% Percale Cotton, Single-size Focus

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera ajustable 90*, *sábana bajera algodón percal*, *sábana bajera 105 cama individual*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets sold **as a single SKU per bed size** (rather than full sheet sets) reduce return risk (buyer knows exactly what fits) and cut SKU complexity — a private-label-friendly angle. The Spanish market has heavy demand for cama individual (90 cm) and cama de matrimonio (135 / 150 cm) — the 90/105 cm individual segment is under-served by premium percal listings vs. cheap microfibre.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to verify):** Sábana bajera is one of the highest-volume search terms in Ropa de hogar year-round; strong Q4 restock buying; recurring purchase (customers rotate sheets every ~2–3 years).
- **Competition signals:** Crowded but fragmented. Amazon Basics is present but does not dominate the percal (as opposed to microfibre) niche. Most leaders have 1,000–15,000 reviews — meaning launch will require serious PPC and a differentiator. Price band €12–€28 for individual fitted sheet.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Composition ambiguous ("microfibra premium" vs. real cotton percal — buyers can't tell).
  - Depth of the elastic pocket (altura del colchón) often unstated → returns.
  - Colour drift after 5–10 washes.
  - Photography shows the sheet flat, never fitted on a real mattress corner.
  - Complaints about elastic slackening within months.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Percal 200-thread minimum, publish real thread count and gramaje.
  - Explicit mattress-depth compatibility (e.g., "colchones de 20–30 cm").
  - All-around elastic (not just corners) with reinforced stitching, documented.
  - Muted Spanish-home palette (blanco roto, arena, salvia, gris piedra).
  - Photography must show the corner-pull, tucked and un-tucked, on real mattresses.
  - Care label in Spanish + a QR code linking to a wash-and-fold video.
- **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **only sábana bajera individual 90×190/200 cm** first, single colour (blanco roto). Add 105 cm and 135 cm sizes once reviews stabilise. Avoid full sheet sets at launch (they multiply SKU count and return risk).
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (competitive; return risk if pocket depth is wrong).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 5/10
  - Differentiation: 6/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 32/50**
- **Decision:** **Further Research** — Keepa BSR + Xray revenue + supplier percal-200 quote required before advancing.

### 3) Ribbed / Waffle-Weave Bath Mat (alfombra de baño) — 50×80 cm, Single SKU

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño rizo*, *alfombra baño antideslizante algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombrillas de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Cotton-terry / waffle bath mats are a small, repeat-buy home essential with clear listing weaknesses (thin, slippery, quick to look worn) that a well-specced Rosanube version can beat. Compact and light — friendly FBA size tier, easy to bundle later with matching hand towels.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to verify):** Steady year-round; slight Q4 uptick around new-flat / renovation season; low return rate typical for the sub-category (buyers know what they're getting).
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Leaders sit at 500–3,000 reviews; several sub-€10 microfibre / polyester competitors are weak on quality complaints, which cotton-based Rosanube can leapfrog. Price band €12–€22 for a good 50×80 cm cotton mat.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Marketed as "cotton" but backing is polyester with unclear non-slip layer.
  - Complaints: slides on tiled Spanish floors; frays at the edges; lint on the first wash.

  - Bland photography — flat, white background.

  - Explicit non-slip solution (either latex-free grip dots or a documented anti-slip weave) with a stated tile test.

  - Muted palette + waffle texture close-up + tile lifestyle shot.
  - Optional matching hand-towel later — natural bundle path.
- **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Start with **one SKU** = 50×80 cm, colour "arena." Add "blanco roto" and "salvia" after 30 days. Bundle path: bath mat + 2 hand towels ("kit invitados") once single SKU reviews are stable.

### 4) Linen-Look Table Runner (camino de mesa) — 40×180 cm, Washable

- **Spanish keyword:** *camino de mesa lino*, *camino mesa comedor lavable*, *camino mesa navidad neutro*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de mesa › Caminos de mesa
- **Why worth researching:** Camino de mesa is a low-SKU-count, high-margin table-textile category with a Q4 tailwind (Navidad and family dinners) but genuine 365-day baseline demand. Rosanube can enter with a neutral "lino natural" position and a single hero SKU — no complicated size matrix.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to verify):** Steady baseline year-round with a Q4 spike; buyers replace runners more often than tablecloths (stained more, washed more).
- **Competition signals:** Moderate; many seasonal / "Christmas print" competitors with weak year-round positioning. Price band €12–€25 for a 40×180 cm linen-look runner. Review leaders in the 200–1,500 range — realistic entry.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fabric claimed as "lino" but is 100% polyester with linen weave.
  - Ironing complaints (creases badly), shrinkage complaints after first wash.
  - Colour drift on darker greys / greens.
  - Length options confusing (some 140, some 180, some 220).
  - Poor styling photography — competitor images look like stock.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Blend option: **55% linen / 45% cotton** (drapes better than 100% linen, less crease than pure linen, publishable composition).
  - Publish real gramaje and pre-washed status.
  - Two lengths only (180 cm and 220 cm) — one colour at launch (natural).
  - "Mesa mediterránea" styling: ceramic, olive branch, terracotta.
  - Include a small styling card in the package (Spanish + English + Portuguese) — cheap and elevates the unboxing.
- **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch single SKU 40×180 cm natural. Add 40×220 cm and a "salvia" colour after 45 days. Later: 4-napkin matching bundle (see Missing Opportunity note in Do Not Pursue #3 below).
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (needs anti-crease positioning to avoid negative reviews).

### 5) Waffle-Weave Duvet Cover (funda nórdica waffle) — Cama Individual 150×220 cm

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda nórdica waffle*, *funda nórdica algodón panal*, *funda nórdica cama individual 150*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas nórdicas
- **Why worth researching:** Waffle-weave duvet covers are a fast-growing aesthetic on Amazon.es Ropa de hogar, drawing off the same design current as the earlier waffle-kitchen-towel opportunity from July. The individual (150×220 cm) size is a much smaller sub-market than 240×220 but has less competition and is easier to sell into pisos de estudiantes / habitación de invitados.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to verify):** Growing search interest for "funda nórdica waffle" over the last 18 months; strong Q4 uptick as buyers refresh bedding for winter. Repeat rate is lower than towels (duvet covers replaced every 2–4 years) — needs stronger AOV to compensate.
- **Competition signals:** Under-populated at the individual size. Larger sizes are dominated by 4–5 well-reviewed brands, but the 150×220 sub-tree has few strong listings. Price band €28–€55.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Colour options limited to the ubiquitous "beige / grey / white" trio.
  - Photography over-styled with props, hiding real fabric texture.
  - Closure system (buttons vs. zipper vs. ties) inconsistent and often not shown.
  - Washing / shrinkage complaints (waffle shrinks up to 5% if not pre-washed).
  - Pillowcase inclusion inconsistent (some include one, some two, some none) — buyer confusion.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Pre-washed waffle (documented) → no post-purchase shrinkage.
  - YKK-style hidden zipper closure (not buttons) — a documented small-but-visible improvement.
  - One pillowcase (50×75 cm — standard ES) included and clearly stated.
  - Real fabric close-up + real-room lifestyle photography with a single-bed context.
  - Muted Iberian-neutral palette; avoid trend colours.
- **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch 150×220 cm + 1× 50×75 cm pillowcase in one colour (natural / arena). Add "salvia" after 30 days. Skip the 240×220 matrimonial size at launch — too competitive.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (higher unit cost, higher FBA fee tier, lower repeat rate).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 7/10

  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 33/50**
