### 1) 100% Cotton Loop-Pile Bath Mat (Non-Slip Backing)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño rizo antideslizante*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The Amazon.es "alfombra baño" top results are split between (a) memory-foam / microfibre mats already rejected in the July report and (b) generic cotton mats with weak photography and no GSM disclosure. A 100% cotton loop-pile bath mat (~1,500–1,900 g/m²) with a genuinely non-slip latex-dot backing is a "boring, giftable, replenishment" SKU that fits Rosanube's calm, practical brand — and cotton mats do not carry the odour/mould complaints memory-foam mats do.

- **Competition signals:** Fragmented at the private-label end. A few Amazon Basics-style anchors at the bottom (€8–€12) and a scattered premium tier at €22–€35 with weak photography. Middle band (€15–€22) is thin — exactly where Rosanube fits.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM / weight not stated (or only "extra grueso" with no number).
  - "Non-slip" claimed with a plain woven back, no latex dots — customer complaints report slipping on tile.
  - Colour looks nothing like the photo after two washes (common review complaint).
  - Only one size (typically 50×80 cm); 60×90 cm underserved.
  - Spanish copy machine-translated from English or Chinese.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**

  - Spanish care label, EU fibre composition label, hanging loop.
  - Kraft belly-band packaging (giftable without extra cost).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as **1 SKU × 3 colours × 1 size (50×80 cm)**. Add 60×90 cm 4–6 months in once reviews stabilise. Later: a **2-piece "juego baño" bundle** = 1 bath mat + 1 pedestal / toilet mat (60×50 cm) for a small AOV lift.

### 2) Reusable Swedish-Style Dishcloth (Cellulose-Cotton) — 10-pack

- **Spanish keyword:** *paños esponja reutilizables*, *bayetas cocina reutilizables ecológicas*, *paños cocina suecos*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Limpieza cocina › Bayetas y paños
- **Why worth researching:** The Swedish dishcloth (compostable cellulose + cotton, absorbs 15–20× its weight, machine-washable, replaces ~15 rolls of kitchen paper) is a genuinely useful eco-adjacent kitchen SKU with a real consumer benefit that photographs and demos well. On Amazon.es this niche is under-branded — most listings are 2–6 pack imports with weak Spanish copy. It's light, tiny, and slots into FBA at a very low fee tier.
- **Demand signals:** Steady, non-seasonal, replacement cycle 2–4 months. Growing "eco cocina" search intent on Amazon.es. Top listings show 300–3,000 reviews.
- **Competition signals:** Present but not dominated — mostly generic imports and one or two eco-branded lines. Common price band €8–€16 for 6–10 packs; a 10-pack at €14.90–€17.90 is workable if pack economics are right.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - No absorbency demo in images.
  - "Ecológico" / "biodegradable" claimed without any certificate → compliance risk to avoid.
  - Designs are loud/kitschy (florals, fruits, English-language slogans) — bad fit for a modern Spanish kitchen.
  - Complaints about the cloth staying wet / smelling if not wrung out; nobody addresses this in the listing.
  - Pack composition unclear — 10 identical vs 10 mixed designs.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Neutral, plain-colour** 10-pack (arena, salvia, blanco roto, terracotta, gris piedra × 2 each) — no prints.
  - Publish honest specs: material % (70% cellulose / 30% cotton), size in cm, absorbency ×, dishwasher/microwave-safe, compost note.
  - Only claim "compostable in home compost" if the supplier proves it; else use "reduce el uso de papel de cocina" as the honest angle.
  - Care tip on the packaging card ("cuélgalo para secar entre usos") to reduce the #1 review complaint.
  - Kraft envelope packaging — flat, letter-box-friendly, low FBA fee.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Hero SKU = **10-pack mixed neutrals**. Later: **cocina cero papel** bundle with waffle kitchen towels (SKU #1 from July report). A 20-pack "familiar" upgrade once traction is proven.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (tiny, light, cheap to sample, low return risk, no size complexity).

  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
