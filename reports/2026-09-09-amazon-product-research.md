Today's shortlist keeps Rosanube inside its core home / bathroom / kitchen / bedroom textile lane, with an emphasis on **replenishment SKUs** (bathmats, dishcloths, pillow protectors, napkins) that Spanish shoppers buy repeatedly and rarely return. The five picks share:

- **Preferred price band €15–€35** with a simple size/colour matrix (≤4 colours, ≤2 sizes).
- **Low compliance friction** — EU textile fibre labelling, care label in Spanish, GPSR responsible person, EPR Spain packaging; no organic / hypoallergenic / medical / antibacterial claims used.
- **FBA-friendly weight/volume** — none is bulky, fragile, oversized, or dangerous-goods.

## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Waffle Bath Mat Set — 2-pack, quick-dry cotton (50×80 cm)

- **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › **Alfombras de baño**
- **Why worth researching:** Bath mats are a true replenishment SKU (stained, worn, replaced). The Amazon.es category is dominated by two archetypes — the plush "chenille shag" (heavy, slow-drying, mould complaints) and the "microfiber memory foam" (thin, slippery, cheap-looking). A **waffle-weave cotton bath mat** slots in between: absorbent, quick-drying, machine-washable, apartment-friendly, photographs well. It also compresses well → **low FBA fee** vs. plush.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** *Alfombra de baño* is a top-of-category search on Amazon.es year-round; consistent BSR presence in the top 200 of Alfombras de baño; not seasonal (unlike beach towels).
- **Competition signals:** Highly fragmented, mostly small private labels. Few EU household brands compete on cotton bath mats. Common price band: **€18–€30 for a 2-pack 50×80 cm** cotton mat. Review counts most often between **100 and 1,500** — entry realistic but not trivial.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM / grammage not stated (buyer cannot tell "thin" from "thick").
  - No anti-slip solution described — many complaints of sliding on tile.
  - Colours labelled in English only; Spanish copy machine-translated.
  - Product photography on empty white background, no Iberian bathroom context.
  - Pack composition unclear (are the two mats identical, or one large + one small?).
  - Reviews report shrinkage after first wash, colour bleed, and fraying edges.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target **550–700 g/m² waffle cotton**), pre-washed, and colourfast tested.
  - **Silicone-dot anti-slip backing** (documented, not just claimed).
  - Muted "clean Iberian home" palette (natural, terracotta, salvia, off-white) — 3–4 colours max.
  - Reinforced double-stitched hem (single small change with a strong review-quote hook).
  - Lifestyle photography in a Spanish bathroom (terrazzo/tile, wood, ceramic sink).
  - Care label + Spanish fibre composition + hanging loop.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch one SKU = **2-pack (50×80 cm + 40×60 cm) in one neutral colour** for €24.90. Later add a matching bath rug 60×100 cm for master-bath buyers, and a "guest set" 2×40×60 cm at €19.90.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (low return risk, easy FBA, low compliance friction).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: **8/10**
  - Competition: **7/10** (fragmented, no dominant brand)
  - Differentiation: **8/10** (anti-slip + GSM + palette all easy wins)
  - Margin potential: **8/10** (light, cotton is cheap in Turkey/India, price band supports it)
  - Risk: **8/10** (low return risk, non-seasonal, no compliance exotics)
  - **Total: 39/50**
- **Decision:** **Further Research** — needs Helium 10 Xray for revenue, Keepa 365-day for stability, and a Turkey/India supplier quote for GSM 600 waffle.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Weighted blanket (*manta con peso / manta ponderada*, 5–9 kg)
- **Category:** Hogar › Dormitorio › Mantas
- **Why rejected:**
  - **Very heavy & bulky** → high FBA fee tier (well outside Rosanube's "lightweight" ideal profile).
  - **Return risk high** — buyers order the wrong weight, then return.
  - **Semi-medical / therapeutic claims** dominate the category (anxiety, sleep, autism) — regulatory / claim-substantiation risk if replicated; hard to compete without them.
  - **Dominated by a few large brands** (Gravity, Levia, Kalar, Amazon Basics equivalents).
  - **Multiple SKUs unavoidable** (weight × size × colour) — variation explosion vs. Rosanube's "simple matrix" rule.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO / Rejected.** Log in `rejected_products.md`.

### B) Silk pillowcase 2-pack (*funda almohada seda / funda satén*)
- **Category:** Hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas de almohada
- **Why rejected:**
  - **Beauty / cosmetic claims** dominate ("hair", "skin", "anti-wrinkle") — Rosanube's config forbids unsubstantiated claims; without them, differentiation is hard.
  - **Brand-dominated** — Slip, Blissy, Kitsch, Amazon Basics equivalents; entering with a private label is expensive.
  - **Return-risk** — colour tone and silk momme grade are common complaints.
  - **Real mulberry silk 22-momme has high unit cost** → thin margin at Amazon.es €20–€30 price bands, or requires €35+ pricing where brand names win.
  - Very close to fashion/beauty adjacency — outside Rosanube's textile-essentials lane.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO / Rejected.** Log in `rejected_products.md`.

### C) "Bamboo" bedsheet set (*juego de sábanas bambú*, 90/135/150/180 cm)
- **Category:** Hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas y fundas
- **Why rejected:**
  - **EU textile labelling** requires *"viscosa de bambú"* (not "bamboo") — many current listings are non-compliant and would need to be re-written; risk of Amazon suppression for false composition.
  - **Variation explosion** — 4+ mattress sizes × 2 pillow options × colours; violates Rosanube's simple-matrix rule.
  - **Return risk high** — fit complaints on fitted sheets ("no ajusta al colchón") are the #1 review complaint in Spain and drive frequent returns.
  - **Price race to the bottom** — many €25–€40 competitors from established Spanish bedding brands (Pikolin/Naturals, Bourdeus, Anne de Solene entry lines) as well as Amazon Basics.
  - Margin structure poor after Amazon fees, returns, and PPC in a category with heavy PPC bidding.
- **Verdict:** **NO GO / Rejected** as a launch SKU. **Watchlist** only for a possible single-size (90×200 fitted only) pilot much later, if a highly differentiated angle emerges.
