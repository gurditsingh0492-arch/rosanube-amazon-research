## Executive Summary

Today's shortlist stays fully inside Rosanube's core home / bed / bath / kitchen textile lane. All five picks share:

- Non-seasonal, replenishment-style demand (bedding, cushion covers, bath mats, cleaning cloths are used year-round in Spanish households).
- Fragmented top-of-page on Amazon.es (many small private-label sellers, no single dominant brand in the target sub-niches — but with Amazon Basics present in some, which is flagged).
- Repeated listing weaknesses Rosanube can beat with honest specifications, restrained palette, and Spanish-first copy.
- Preferred €15–€35 price band and manageable SKU trees (≤4 colours, ≤2 sizes at launch).
- Standard EU textile compliance (fibre composition, care label, GPSR responsible person, EPR Spain, polybag warning). No organic / OEKO-TEX / hypoallergenic / antibacterial / medical claims used unless a valid certificate is later documented.

**None of the five are recommended for a purchase order today.** All are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist**, pending Helium 10 Xray + Cerebro, Keepa 90/180/365-day history, and supplier quotes.

## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Cotton Chenille Bath Mat (60×90 cm, absorbent, machine-washable)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño chenilla*, *alfombra baño antideslizante algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** A textile alternative to the memory-foam bath mat we already rejected on 2026-07-01. Cotton chenille avoids polyurethane foam (no smell complaints, no yellowing, no compression memory), is machine-washable in a normal cotton cycle, and lets Rosanube stay on-brand ("cotton home textile"). Real replenishment SKU — bath mats get grimy and are replaced every 12–18 months.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** *alfombra baño* is a consistent Amazon.es evergreen; cotton / chenille variants appear regularly in the Baño Best Sellers rail. No obvious seasonality — bathrooms are used year-round, and mats replace on wear, not on weather.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — mix of Turkish and Indian cotton sellers plus generic private labels. Some Amazon Basics presence in "microfiber" bath mats, but the **cotton chenille sub-niche** appears less crowded than microfiber. Common price band €14–€28 for single 60×90 cm; €22–€38 for 2-piece bath+contour sets.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Anti-slip backing described as "non-slip" but review complaints report the latex peels or crumbles after 5–10 washes.
  - Weight / GSM never stated, so buyer cannot tell 800 g/m² from 1,400 g/m² before buying.
  - Colour photos differ from delivered colour (recurring 1–2 star complaint).
  - Some listings sell a "set" but images show only the large mat, causing return requests.
  - Care instructions in English only or absent.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real GSM (target ≥1,200 g/m² chenille pile) and pre-shrunk status.
  - Use a **stitched-in TPR backing** or a documented wash-durable latex, and *say so* on image #2.
  - Muted palette: piedra (stone), lino (natural), salvia (sage), blanco roto (off-white) — no bright turquoise or coral.
  - Lifestyle photo in a real Spanish bathroom (small format, tiled floor, wooden stool) — most competitors use flat white-background stills.
  - Spanish care label + fibre composition + hang-loop for line-drying.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **single SKU 60×90 cm** in 3 muted colours; add a **50×80 cm** size after 100 reviews, and later a 2-piece "bath + contour" set for €32.90.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (main risk: wet-cotton weight = FBA tier; latex backing durability = review risk)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 *(wet cotton is heavy → FBA fee sensitive → margin depends on packed weight)*
  - Risk: 7/10

### 2) 100% Cotton Fitted Bed Sheet (Sábana Bajera Ajustable, 135 / 150 / 160 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera 135*, *sábana bajera 150 ajustable algodón*, *sábana bajera 160 algodón 100*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Sábanas bajeras ajustables
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are the *single* highest-replenishment bedding SKU in a Spanish household — they wear out, tear at the elastic, and get replaced faster than duvet covers or top sheets. Buying a fitted sheet **without** the rest of a set is a very common Amazon.es search pattern (people replacing a broken sheet). Sold as one-item SKUs, the SKU tree stays small (3 sizes × 3 colours = 9 SKUs).
- **Demand signals:** Consistent search interest year-round with a mild uplift around back-to-school (Sept) and the winter bed-refresh (Oct–Nov). Recurring appearance in Ropa de cama Best Sellers for both 135 and 150 sizes (the two dominant Spanish mattress widths).
- **Competition signals:** **High** — this is a mature category. Utopia Bedding, Amazon Basics, Pikolin, Today, and multiple Turkish/Portuguese/Spanish private labels compete. Review counts on page 1 often 1,000–15,000. Price band €12–€25 for 100% cotton depending on TC and size.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "100% cotton" claimed but reviews report a poly-blend feel — no TC or GSM ever documented.
  - Elastic reported as loose or breaking after ≤20 washes (biggest single complaint).
  - Colour fastness weak — greys and beiges fade to pink or yellow.
  - Deep-mattress fit ambiguous — many listings do not state the "cajeta" / pocket depth, causing returns for tall memory-foam mattresses (30+ cm).
  - Spanish sizes (135, 150, 160) confused with international sizes (Queen, King) in titles.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish TC (percal 200 hilos target) *and* GSM.
  - **All-round elastic** (not just corners) + reinforced stitching — and photograph the elastic band on image #3.
  - State pocket depth in cm (target 32 cm) so buyers with thick memory-foam mattresses filter *in*, not *out*.
  - Neutral palette only at launch (blanco, gris piedra, lino, azul niebla).
  - Spanish-first title: *Sábana bajera ajustable 100% algodón 200 hilos — cama 150 x 190/200 cm, bolsillo 32 cm*.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **single sheet SKUs** in 3 sizes × 3 colours. Do **not** launch a "set" — competing with the 4-piece funda nórdica bundles is a losing PPC fight. Later add a 2-pack (same size, same colour) at a 15% discount as a repeat-purchase offer.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium–High** (mature category, high review barrier, price war risk against Amazon Basics)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 4/10 *(review barrier is real)*
  - Differentiation: 6/10
  - Margin potential: 5/10
  - Risk: 5/10
  - **Total: 29/50**

### 3) Cotton / Linen-Look Cushion Cover 2-Pack (45×45 cm, hidden zip)

- **Spanish keyword:** *fundas cojín 45x45*, *fundas cojín lino*, *fundas cojín algodón decorativas*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Fundas de cojín
- **Why worth researching:** Cushion covers are a low-return, low-weight, low-size-complexity SKU (one standard 45×45 sofa cushion covers 90% of the Spanish market). Buyers refresh living-room covers seasonally without buying new cushions, so the category has natural repeat-purchase behaviour. Pack-of-2 economics work in the €18–€28 band, which is exactly Rosanube's price zone.
- **Demand signals:** Very high — *fundas de cojín* is a top-10 search inside Ropa de hogar. 45×45 cm is the dominant Spanish cushion size (60×60 is secondary). Non-seasonal.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented at the mid-price / neutral-textile end (where Rosanube plays). The extremes are crowded — bright-print / boho covers dominate the low end, and Zara Home / H&M Home dominate the top end — but the plain-neutral-cotton-with-real-zip band is entered mostly by generic sellers with weak listings. Common price €12–€25 for a 2-pack in this segment.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Zips described as "hidden" but reviews complain zips are visible or catch on cushions.
  - Fabric shown as "linen" is often 100% polyester with a linen-look weave — buyers feel misled.
  - No cushion insert included, but the listing photo shows a cushion → misleading.
  - Colours between the two covers of the "pack" are unclear (matching or contrasting?).
  - Wash instructions not in Spanish.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Honest fabric labelling**: state exact composition (e.g. "70% algodón / 30% lino" or "100% algodón grueso") and GSM.
  - YKK-style metal-tooth **invisible zip** with a fabric flap that hides it — photograph the flap.
  - Restricted palette (natural, piedra, salvia, terracota apagada) — matches Rosanube's overall look.
  - Both covers in the pack are the *same* colour by default; add "mixed neutral" pack later.
  - Lifestyle photo on a real linen sofa in a Spanish apartment context.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **2-pack same colour** in 4 neutrals at €22.90. After ≥100 reviews, add a **4-pack mixed neutrals** at €39.90 and a **60×60 cm** size.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (light, small, cheap FBA tier, low return risk, easy compliance)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 8/10
  - Risk: 8/10
  - **Total: 39/50**

### 4) Microfiber Cleaning Cloth Pack (Bayetas Microfibra, 12-pack, 30×30 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *bayetas microfibra*, *paños microfibra limpieza*, *bayetas cocina microfibra pack*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y colada › Paños, bayetas y esponjas
- **Why worth researching:** True consumable / repeat-purchase textile — buyers exhaust and replace bayetas every 2–4 months. Sits inside Rosanube's kitchen/cleaning textile lane, keeps the SKU tree trivially small (one 12-pack), and photographs well in a bundle format. Complements — not competes with — the waffle kitchen towel already shortlisted on 2026-07-01.
- **Demand signals:** High and stable, with a mild uplift in Sept (back-to-school) and around Semana Santa (spring cleaning). Broad household reach.
- **Competition signals:** **High volume, but weakly branded** — Amazon Basics, Vileda, Spontex, and E-Cloth all present, plus many white-label 12/24-packs. Price band €8–€18 for a 12-pack. Because the price band sits just at / below Rosanube's €15 floor, this is only interesting if the pack size / GSM economics allow ≥€15 shelf price.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM not stated — no way to distinguish a flimsy 180 g/m² cloth from a durable 300 g/m² cloth.
  - Colours "randomised" per pack, which annoys buyers who want colour-coded cloths (kitchen / bathroom / glass).
  - Reviews complain of lint left on glass after ≤10 washes.
  - Packaging is usually a thin polybag with no reuse or storage value.
  - Fibre composition sometimes listed only in Chinese.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish GSM (target ≥300 g/m²) and edge-stitching type.
  - **Colour-coded 12-pack**: 4 colours × 3 cloths (verde=cocina, azul=baño, amarillo=cristal, gris=multiuso) with a small printed guide on the pack.
  - Recyclable card sleeve, not a thin polybag.
  - Spanish-first care instructions on the sleeve, not just online.
  - Include a small hang-loop on each cloth (rare in the segment).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single SKU = colour-coded 12-pack at €16.90–€18.90. Add a 24-pack later. Do *not* fragment into 3-packs and 6-packs — SKU proliferation kills unit economics at this price.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (very light, cheap FBA tier, low return, no compliance surprises — microfiber is not a textile-regulation heavyweight but still needs fibre composition label)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 5/10 *(brand crowd is real; price war risk)*
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 *(low ASP; depends on landed cost being genuinely low)*
  - Risk: 8/10
  - **Total: 34/50**

### 5) Waffle-Weave Duvet Cover Set (Funda Nórdica Waffle, 3-piece)

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda nórdica waffle*, *funda nórdica 150 algodón*, *funda nórdica 135 verano*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Fundas nórdicas
- **Why worth researching:** Waffle weave is doing on bedding what it already does on towels — moving from novelty to a recognised aesthetic. Amazon.es *funda nórdica* buyers are underserved on the "textured, breathable, summer-weight cotton" end; most listings are either heavy percal / satin or cheap microfiber. A 3-piece set (1 duvet cover + 2 pillowcases 50×75) is the standard Spanish configuration, and buyers actively search "waffle" now, not only "algodón".
- **Demand signals:** *funda nórdica* is one of the very largest Ropa de cama searches on Amazon.es. Adding "waffle" narrows into an emerging keyword with growing interest and much less noise. Non-seasonal, with a natural June–August "verano" spike where breathable / summer-weight bedding rises.
- **Competition signals:** **Medium** at the sub-niche level (*funda nórdica waffle*), **very high** at the parent-keyword level (*funda nórdica*). Sub-niche competition is currently a mix of small private labels and 2–3 mid-brands; Amazon Basics is not yet strongly present in *waffle* specifically. Price band €30–€60 for a 3-piece 150 cm cotton waffle set.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Waffle cotton" claimed but the weave is a small honeycomb print on flat cotton — not a true waffle weave.
  - GSM not published; buyers cannot tell a summer-weight waffle from a heavy one.
  - Button/tie closure inconsistent — some listings show buttons, some show tie, reviews complain of mismatch.
  - Colours between duvet cover and pillowcases mismatch after wash (dye lot).
  - Sizes ambiguous — 150 cm mattress vs 240×220 duvet is a common Spanish confusion.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish waffle-weave GSM (target 200–240 g/m² summer) and pre-shrunk status.
  - Coconut / mother-of-pearl style buttons + inner corner ties for duvet fixing — photograph both.
  - Muted palette only (blanco roto, arena, salvia, terracota apagada).
  - Show a Spanish bed with the correct 150 cm / 135 cm mattress and correct duvet size, so buyers can pattern-match.
  - Spanish-first title format: *Funda nórdica waffle 100% algodón — cama 150 — 240×220 cm + 2 fundas almohada 50×75*.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **3-piece set** in 2 sizes (135 and 150) × 3 colours = 6 SKUs. Later add a single **funda de almohada 2-pack** as a low-risk cross-sell.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (higher price band → higher return sensitivity; dye-lot risk between duvet and pillowcases)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 35/50**

## Do Not Pursue (Today)

### A) 4-Piece Percale Luxury Bed Sheet Set (400TC)

- **Rejected because:** Directly overlaps a category we already flagged as high-review-barrier in shortlist item #2 (fitted sheet). A 4-piece "juego de sábanas" set adds SKU complexity (size × colour × set-piece-count) with no differentiation Rosanube can defend against Amazon Basics, Utopia Bedding, Today, and Pikolin. Margin is squeezed by both a heavier product (higher FBA tier) and stronger PPC competition on *juego de sábanas 4 piezas*. If we want a set, waffle duvet cover (item #5) is a better entry.

### B) Baby Hooded Towel (Toalla Capucha Bebé)

- **Rejected because:** Adds baby-product compliance overhead (GPSR risk classification for baby textiles, category listing restrictions on Amazon.es, higher scrutiny on labels and safety claims). Also introduces claim-management risk — buyers expect "hipoalergénico" and "OEKO-TEX" wording, and per `research_config.md` we may not make those claims without documented certificates. Return risk is elevated (gift returns, size complaints, colour). Wrong first-launch profile for Rosanube — revisit only once we have OEKO-TEX certification and a proven adult-towel workflow.

### C) Oversized Beach Towel / Toalla Playa Redonda Grande (Roundie 150 cm)

- **Rejected because:** **Highly seasonal** (BSR spikes Jun–Aug, collapses Oct–May), which violates the "not too seasonal" rule in `research_config.md`. Category is saturated with print-heavy fashion designs, and photography-heavy differentiation (models on beaches) does not match Rosanube's practical / neutral brand. Storage cost in Amazon FBA Spain during 8 months of slow-move would erode margin. Would only reconsider a small, non-fashion, non-print beach line as a **summer-only supplemental SKU**, not a shortlist candidate.
