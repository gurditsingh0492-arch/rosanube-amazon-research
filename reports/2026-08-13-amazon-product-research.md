## Executive Summary

Today's shortlist stays inside Rosanube's core home-textile lane and deliberately avoids overlap with the 2026-07-01 report (waffle kitchen towels, peshtemal bath towels, etc.). The five picks are chosen for:

- **Steady, non-seasonal replenishment demand** — sleep textiles, kitchen textiles, table textiles are re-bought, not trend-bought.
- **Fragmented Amazon.es competition** — many small private-label sellers, few dominant EU brands in the target sub-niches.
- **Exploitable listing weaknesses** — missing GSM/thread count, poor Spanish copy, ambiguous pack composition, no lifestyle photography.
- **Preferred price band €15–€35** and low variation complexity (max 2 sizes × 3–4 neutral colours at launch).
- **Low compliance friction** — EU textile fibre label + Spanish care label + GPSR + EPR packaging Spain. No organic / hypoallergenic / antibacterial / medical claims used.

**None of today's five picks is a purchase-order recommendation.** All are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist** until Helium 10, Keepa, and supplier data are on file.

## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Sábana Bajera Ajustable 100% Algodón Percal (fitted sheet)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera ajustable 150*, *sábana bajera 135 algodón*, *sábana bajera percal*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** Fitted sheets are the most-searched item in the Ropa de cama sub-tree on Amazon.es and are a true replenishment SKU (they wear before flat sheets do). Percal (200-hilos plain-weave cotton) is a mid-premium sub-segment where most current listings under-communicate the fabric spec — a listing weakness Rosanube can exploit with a clean, honest technical bullet ("percal, 200 hilos/pulgada², 118 g/m²").
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Multiple listings with 3–5-figure review counts across the 90/135/150/180 cm sizes; visibility year-round on the "Ropa de cama" best-seller lists; commonly re-appearing in the "Los más vendidos" and "Los más deseados" carousels.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented in the €12–€28 band for single sizes; the €25–€45 band is thinner and more brandable. A handful of Amazon private labels (Amazon Basics / Pinzon) sit at the low end — beatable on fabric transparency, not on price.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thread count / gramaje not stated, or stated in marketing terms ("suave y sedoso") with no number.
  - Elastic bolsillo depth rarely stated in cm — a top-3 review complaint on almost every mid-price ASIN ("no cabe en mi colchón de 30 cm").
  - Colour renders inaccurate — "beige" that arrives yellow, "gris" that arrives blue.
  - Machine-translated Spanish copy; sizes shown in imperial or ambiguous ("Queen") for a Spanish buyer used to 90/135/150/180.
  - No Spanish care-label information in the images.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish **thread count + gramaje + bolsillo depth in cm** on the main image and A+ content.
  - 4 muted neutrals only (blanco, gris perla, arena, salvia) — no saturated colours.
  - Elastic all the way around (not just corners) + reinforced corners photo.
  - Lifestyle images in a Spanish bedroom context, not white-cyc studio.
  - Spanish + Portuguese + English care label sewn in.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one hero SKU** = 150×190×30 cm blanco. Once reviews stabilise, add 135 cm and 180 cm, then extend to gris perla / arena / salvia. Optional 2-pack (same colour) as SKU #2. Deliberately **do not** launch a full sheet set (bajera + encimera + 2 fundas) at start — it multiplies variations and inventory risk.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (multi-size variation tree; colour-accuracy return risk; mattress-depth mismatch return risk).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 9/10
  - Competition: 5/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10
  - Risk: 6/10

### 2) Set de Servilletas de Tela Algodón/Lino Lavables (fabric napkin pack, 6 or 8)

- **Spanish keyword:** *servilletas tela algodón*, *servilletas lino lavables*, *set servilletas tela 8*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Textiles de mesa › Servilletas de tela
- **Why worth researching:** Fabric napkins sit in the sustainability-adjacent "reduce paper" behaviour that has grown steadily in Spain over the last 3–4 years (also fits Navidad / Reyes / Comunión gifting). Sub-niche is small enough to be beatable, big enough to matter, and photographs beautifully — a rare combination for a private label without a big content budget.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Search suggest auto-completes on Amazon.es for *servilletas tela*, *servilletas lino*, *servilletas tela boda*; consistent review counts in the 200–1,500 range for the current top ASINs; predictable Q4 uplift (Navidad + Reyes) without being purely seasonal.
- **Competition signals:** Partly generic (bulk hemstitch imports from India / China) at €10–€18 for 12-packs, partly artisanal at €25–€40 for 4–6 packs. Middle band (€18–€28 for a well-photographed 6-pack) is under-served on Amazon.es.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fabric composition ambiguous ("efecto lino") — often 100% polyester marketed as linen.
  - Size not standardised; buyer confusion between coctel (25×25) and comida (40×40).
  - Hems machine-serged and photographed close-up in a way that shows the flaw.
  - Colours photographed on unrealistic backgrounds → high "no coincide con la foto" review rate.
  - Care instructions missing in Spanish.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **Real fabric spec** (e.g. "55% lino / 45% algodón, 180 g/m², dobladillo cosido a inglete"), stated in the first bullet.
  - Standard 40×40 cm size at launch, mitered corners, reinforced hem.
  - 3 muted colours only: blanco roto, salvia, terracota — no seasonal reds/greens at launch.
  - Full kraft belly-band packaging, giftable without extra unit cost.
  - Lifestyle photos: Spanish/Portuguese table setting (terracotta, ceramic, olive-wood).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch a **6-pack single colour** (blanco roto) at €22.90. Add salvia and terracota once reviews stabilise. Optional 4-pack "starter" and 12-pack "familia" later. Skip mixed-colour packs at launch — inventory + photography cost.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**.
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 6/10
  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 8/10

### 3) Alfombrilla Escurridor de Vajilla de Microfibra (dish drying mat)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombrilla escurridor vajilla*, *tapete secado platos microfibra*, *alfombrilla secar vajilla cocina*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Cocina y menaje › Accesorios de fregadero › Escurreplatos
- **Why worth researching:** Sub-€20 kitchen textile, small, light, flat-packs to almost nothing → excellent FBA fee tier. Solves a specific problem (protect the encimera under the escurridor, absorb water from hand-washed items) and has no direct big-brand dominance. Reviews on current listings show real "why did I wait so long to buy this" enthusiasm — a marker of a happy-path product.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Multiple listings with 500–3,000 reviews; auto-complete on Amazon.es for *alfombrilla escurridor* and *alfombrilla microfibra cocina*; steady year-round demand, mild uplift in mudanza / vuelta al cole windows.
- **Competition signals:** Highly fragmented, mostly cheap private labels at €8–€14 for a single mat; only a handful of listings in the €15–€22 "premium neutral" segment where a Rosanube-shaped brand fits.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Photos on white cyc, no kitchen context.
  - GSM not stated; buyers can't tell a thin mat from a thick one.
  - "Antideslizante" claimed with no photo of the back — many arrive slippery.
  - Size in cm printed only in the description, not on the main image.
  - Common complaint: mould after 2–3 weeks (mat kept permanently wet) — Rosanube can explicitly answer this in the listing (rotate, hang, wash weekly at 60°C).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - GSM ≥ 500 g/m² microfibra, silicona antideslizante en el reverso — both photographed, not just claimed.
  - 2 sizes: 40×46 (estándar) + 50×61 (grande) — nothing else at launch.
  - Neutral colours only (gris piedra, arena, blanco roto).
  - Include a **care card in the box**: "gira el tapete cada 2 días, lava a 60°C semanalmente" — kills the mould complaint pre-emptively.
  - Kraft-sleeve packaging, no plastic.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch 1 SKU (40×46 gris piedra). Add 50×61 after 30 reviews. Optional 2-pack "cocina + auxiliar" at the 3-month mark.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**.

### 4) Protector de Colchón Impermeable de Rizo Ajustable (waterproof terry mattress protector)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable 150*, *cubre colchón impermeable rizo*, *protector colchón ajustable impermeable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** Every mattress buyer is a candidate; every parent, pet owner, or renter is a repeat candidate. Sub-niche has real, replenishable demand and buyers who genuinely research (not impulse) — which rewards a listing that answers questions clearly. Terry-with-PU-backing is a well-understood, non-medical construction, avoiding the "antiácaros / hipoalergénico" claim trap.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Consistent presence in "Los más vendidos en Protectores de colchón"; strong review counts (4-figure) on the 90 / 135 / 150 sizes; steady non-seasonal demand.
- **Competition signals:** Amazon Basics / Pikolin / Velfont hold visible share. Beatable on transparency (real gramaje, real bolsillo depth, real breathability claim tested to a standard) and packaging (most competitors ship in a plastic bag that reviewers photograph negatively). Price band €14–€30 for single, €25–€45 for pack of 2.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Bolsillo depth vs. mattress thickness confusion — top-3 return reason.
  - "Transpirable" claimed without any spec or test standard.
  - Many arrive noisy / crunchy — the PU membrane isn't the soft breathable kind — customers complain.
  - Care instructions unclear on wash temp (PU membranes degrade above 60°C).
  - No Spanish care label documented in images.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish **bolsillo depth (30 cm), gramaje del rizo (150 g/m² algodón), tipo de membrana (PU respirable, sin PVC, sin ftalatos si documentado)** on the first image.
  - Emphasise **silent / non-crunchy** with a short video (if UGC budget allows) — this is the #1 differentiator vs. cheap PU protectors.
  - Avoid "antiácaros", "hipoalergénico", "antibacteriano" — all require documentation the config forbids using without proof.
  - 3 sizes only at launch: 90×190, 135×190, 150×190. All white.
  - Kraft box + reusable cotton bag packaging, giftable.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Single unit per size at launch. Add 2-pack (same size, same colour) at month 3. Do **not** add funda de almohada impermeable at launch — separate compliance / return profile.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (return risk if bolsillo/mattress mismatch; claim risk if language drifts into medical).

## Do Not Pursue (rejected today)

### A) Manteles impermeables PVC (PVC-coated wipe-clean tablecloths)
- **Why rejected:**
  - REACH / phthalate compliance is a real risk on any PVC-coated textile — using the config's own rule, no "chemical-free" claim can be made without documentation, and the category attracts scrutiny.
  - Existing category is dominated by cheap, print-heavy generics with a race-to-the-bottom price (€8–€14 for 140×140) that destroys Rosanube's target €15–€35 margin band.
  - Off-brand aesthetic for Rosanube (would need loud prints to compete visually).
  - Return / photo-mismatch risk is high (colour + pattern accuracy).
- **Verdict:** **Rejected** (add to `rejected_products.md`).

### B) Toallas de playa jacquard con licencia (character-licensed jacquard beach towels)
- **Why rejected:**
  - IP / licensing risk — the config forbids licensed-character products unless legally licensed, which Rosanube is not set up for.
  - Even the unlicensed "generic character" version invites Amazon takedowns and lawyer letters.
  - Highly seasonal (May–August), leaves 8 months of inventory idle.
  - Poor brand fit — Rosanube is "clean practical premium", not "Frozen / Disney / futbol".
- **Verdict:** **Rejected**.

### C) Fundas nórdicas 150 alta gama con 6–8 estampados (high-thread-count duvet cover sets, many patterns)
- **Why rejected:**
  - Variation explosion: 3 bed sizes × 6–8 patterns × 2 finishes = 36–48 SKUs at launch. Config explicitly warns against products with too many sizes/colours/variations.
  - Requires expensive lifestyle/model bedroom photography per pattern — Rosanube isn't set up for that content budget yet.
  - Category has strong incumbents (Pierre Cardin, Reig Martí, Sancarlos, Amazon Basics) with big brand recognition.
  - High-thread-count (300+) increases landed cost; margin is tight after PPC + returns.
- **Verdict:** **Rejected for now**. Revisit only as a **single-pattern, single-size hero** launch after Rosanube has ≥ 2 established sleep-textile SKUs and real cost data.
