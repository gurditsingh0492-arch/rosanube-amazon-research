### 1) Cotton Waffle Bath Mat (Alfombra de baño de algodón gofre)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombra baño gofre*, *alfombra baño rizo lavable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The Amazon.es *alfombra de baño* category is heavily anchored by two commoditised formats — cheap microfiber shag and memory-foam pads — both of which have well-known problems (odour, mould, shedding, non-slip glue peeling). A flat cotton waffle mat is a *third format* that photographs cleanly, matches Rosanube's existing waffle textile aesthetic (July's kitchen and bath towel picks), and lets the listing lead on honest cotton weight and machine washability instead of vague "memory" claims.

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM / cotton weight not stated.
  - Photos on white cyc, no bathroom context.

  - Real bathroom-context lifestyle photos (Spanish tile floor).

- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as one SKU = 50×80 cm, 3 colours. Once reviews stabilise, add a 60×100 cm size and a "conjunto" (mat + matching hand towel) as a giftable Navidad SKU alongside the July waffle bath towel set.

### 2) Waterproof Mattress Protector, Fitted, Terry Top (Protector de colchón impermeable rizo)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubrecolchón impermeable ajustable*, *protector colchón rizo transpirable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas de colchón y protectores
- **Why worth researching:** Waterproof mattress protectors are one of the highest-repeat SKUs in the bedroom textile category (post-illness, potty-training, spills, pet accidents, hotel/Airbnb operators). The category is functional — buyers do not shop by aesthetics, they shop by size + waterproof rating + noise. This narrows the differentiation surface, which is *good* for a private label with no fashion or model-photography exposure. Config-friendly: no colour SKU proliferation, one size can carry a launch.
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round; slight uplift around back-to-school and Rebajas. Spain has a very standardised mattress-size market (90×190, 105×190, 135×190, 150×190, 160×200) which caps SKU complexity.
- **Competition signals:** Mid-density, mostly small private-label sellers with 200–3,000 reviews at €12–€25. A few "Pikolin"-brand-adjacent listings hold premium prices at €25–€35. No single dominant EU brand at the mid tier. Amazon Basics is present but not overwhelming.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Rizo transpirable" claimed but grammage / fabric composition unclear.
  - Photos are studio-only, no on-bed lifestyle shot.
  - Complaints about *ruido a plástico* (crinkly PU membrane) — a real, addressable defect.
  - Complaints about shrinkage after first wash and elastic skirt losing shape.
  - Buyers complain about ambiguous size charts — Spanish beds are labelled by mattress width, not by "Twin / Queen".
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish membrane spec: TPU (quiet, breathable) not PVC/PEVA (crinkly, chemical smell).
  - Deep skirt (up to 30 cm) — one of the most-praised features in positive reviews across the category.
  - Size table in Spanish mattress widths (90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 160 cm) with the depth clearly stated.
  - Care label with real max wash temp (60 °C is meaningful for hygiene buyers).
  - Sober packaging: kraft sleeve, no aggressive "medical" claims, just "impermeable" and "transpirable".
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch one size first — **150×190 cm** (matrimonio, Spain's highest-volume mattress size). Add 90×190 and 135×190 once reviews reach ~50. Do **not** add colour variants — protector is under a sheet, colour is irrelevant.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (functional product with clear defect complaints in the category — needs real sample validation of the TPU membrane before scale)
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10 (return risk if the membrane defect is reproduced; needs sample QC)
  - **Total: 34/50**

### B) King-Size Embroidered Duvet Cover Set (*juego funda nórdica bordada 240×220*)
- **Why rejected:** Requires the full Spanish bed-size matrix (90 / 105 / 135 / 150 / 160 / 180 → 4–5 different duvet sizes), several colours, plus matched pillowcase sizes → too many variations for a controlled first launch, per config §"Products to Avoid" (too many sizes/colours/variations). Also requires model / lifestyle bedroom photography to compete visually — expensive per config §"Products to Avoid" (needing expensive model photography). Category is dominated by large brands (Barceló, LaMalinche, Amazon Basics) and low-cost prints from Chinese sellers, both of which are hard to displace. Buyer return rate on duvet covers is high due to colour and size complaints.

### C) Muslin Baby Swaddle Blanket (*muselina bebé arrullo*)
- **Why rejected:** Baby textile category triggers additional EU/EN safety expectations (EN 71-3 chemical safety, EN 14682 cords & drawstrings, textile flammability) and buyer scrutiny that a first-time private-label textile brand should not carry. Category is anchored on Amazon.es by well-known baby brands (Aden + Anais style incumbents, Amomi, plus large generic multipacks). Return / complaint risk is elevated because these are gift purchases for newborns, so buyer expectations on softness, size, and print accuracy are very high. Falls into config §"Products to Avoid" (products with unclear legal or safety restrictions, products dominated by big brands, high customer complaint rates).
