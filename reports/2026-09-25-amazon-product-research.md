### 1) Waterproof Fitted Mattress Protector — Spanish Bed Sizes

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable rizo*, *protector colchón 135*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas y protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** True replenishment SKU (customers replace every 12–24 months after mattress purchase, kids' bed accidents, spills, or move-in). Spanish bed sizes are simple and standardised (**90×190, 105×190, 135×190, 150×190, 160×200**) — no exotic US "twin XL / king" complexity. Waterproof rizo (terry) is expected to be quiet in-bed and machine-washable — clear improvement axis for weak listings.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** Consistent daily-purchase behaviour, non-seasonal, spikes gently in September (rentrée, university lets in Madrid/Barcelona/Valencia) and January (new-mattress purchases). Multiple listings show 4-figure review counts in the Dormitorio subcategory.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. A handful of established brands (Pikolin/Belnou/Velfont) anchor the top slots, but 60–70% of the first 3 pages are private-label sellers. Common price band €12–€22 for single 90/105 and €15–€30 for 135/150.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Impermeable" claim not backed by a stated PU-membrane spec (g/m² or waterproof rating).
  - Skirt depth (altura del cajón) not stated — a very common 1-star complaint on Spanish thick-mattress buyers (25–32 cm).
  - Photos are white-background CGI with no "wrinkle-free on bed" lifestyle image.
  - Machine-translated Spanish titles; keyword stuffing.
  - Complaints about crinkly / plastic-bag noise from the PU film.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish **skirt depth (30 cm)** and **PU-film weight** as headline specs; photograph the elastic on a 30-cm-thick mattress.
  - Rizo cotton top face (~150 g/m²) with quiet TPU membrane — call out "silencioso" honestly.
  - Spanish/Portuguese/English care label; Spanish fibre composition sticker per EU Textile Regulation.
  - Kraft belt packaging with a printed size guide (Spanish beds).
  - Launch **one size first** (135×190 — most common Spanish double) to keep the SKU tree simple.
- **Suggested bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as **single 135×190** at €19.90. Add 90×190 (kids/single) and 150×190 (queen) once reviews are stable. Consider a **kids' bundle (90×190 protector + waterproof pillow protector)** later.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (variation-count risk, and buyer sensitivity to depth / noise claims — but low return risk if specs are honest).
- **Score /50:**

  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10 (variation management is the main drag)

## Do Not Pursue — Rejected Today

### A) Weighted Blanket / *Manta con peso*
- **Reason:** Dominated by 2–3 large brands (Gravity-style, YnM-style), heavy shipping (5–9 kg → large FBA tier destroys margin), medical/therapeutic-adjacent claims risk under Spanish regulation, high return rate driven by weight-mismatch (7 kg vs. 9 kg preference), and buyer confusion about kids' safety. Fails "Not brand-dominated", "Lightweight or medium-weight", and "No medical/therapeutic claims" filters in `research_config.md`.

### B) Baby Muslin Swaddle Blanket / *Muselina bebé*
- **Reason:** Baby-safe textiles carry higher EU compliance exposure (**REACH**, **EN 71-3** where applicable, **GPSR** attention on infant items), and Amazon.es enforces baby-category listing requirements more strictly. Return risk is elevated (gift returns, allergy anxiety, "hipoalergénico" expectations Rosanube cannot certify). Adjacent to the current muslin **kitchen** idea (#2) but explicitly out of Rosanube's brand + compliance comfort zone right now.

### C) Bed Skirt / *Faldón para cama*
- **Reason:** Declining category on Amazon.es (Spanish design taste has moved to canapé/base tapizado, which doesn't need a faldón), size-matching complexity (bed size × mattress-to-floor height), high return risk from wrong size, and difficult to bundle. Low search volume vs. the pillow-protector / mattress-protector alternatives already in the pipeline.
