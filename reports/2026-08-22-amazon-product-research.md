## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) Cotton Waffle Bath Mat (Alfombra de baño algodón waffle)

1. **Product name:** Cotton waffle-weave bath mat, 50×80 cm, single unit
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombrillas de baño (Textiles de baño)
3. **Why worth researching:** Most Amazon.es bath mats are the "fluffy chenille" or memory-foam type — bulky, slow to dry, and heavy for FBA. A flat 100% cotton waffle mat is a genuine functional alternative (quick-dry, machine-washable at 60 °C, mildew-resistant, apartment-friendly), and it photographs beautifully in a "clean Iberian home" palette. It also compresses well → lower FBA fee tier than chenille.
4. **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** *Alfombra baño algodón* consistently returns pages of listings with 3–4 figure review counts; category is a year-round replenishment, not seasonal; renters and small-flat buyers (large Spanish urban segment) actively seek non-slippery, washable, low-profile mats.
5. **Competition signals:** Fragmented. Chenille / memory-foam dominates SERP; the flat cotton / waffle sub-segment is present but not crowded (mostly generic private labels, few household brand names). Price band **€14–€28** for a single 50×80. Review counts often 100–1,500, meaning entry is realistic.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - GSM / grammage not stated; buyers can't tell if mat is thin or thick.
   - "Antideslizante" (non-slip) claimed without describing the backing or a silicone dot pattern; complaints about sliding on tile.
   - Colour photos on white background only — no bathroom context.
   - Pack composition ambiguous (1 mat vs. 2 vs. mat + contour).
   - Shrinkage complaints after 60 °C wash.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - Publish real GSM (target ~ 800–900 g/m² waffle) and pre-washed status.
   - Small silicone-dot backing on a cotton mat (documented, not just claimed).
   - Muted "Iberian bathroom" palette (natural, sage, terracotta, off-white) — 4 colours max.
   - Real bathroom lifestyle photos (Spanish tile, ceramic sink, wooden stool).
   - Spanish care label + Portuguese/English secondary; hanging loop for drying.
8. **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **single mat 50×80 cm** at €19.90 in 4 colours. Add a **2-pack (mat + smaller 40×60)** as second SKU once first has 25+ reviews. Do **not** launch a 3-piece bathroom set on day one — variation complexity kills margin.
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (return risk low if colour photos are accurate; main risk is customer confusion vs. fluffy mats).
10. **Score /50:**
    - Demand: 7/10
    - Competition: 7/10
    - Differentiation: 8/10
    - Margin potential: 7/10 (flat cotton compresses well → decent FBA tier)

### 2) Microfiber Cleaning Cloth Set — Colour-Coded 12-Pack (Bayetas microfibra 12 unidades)

1. **Product name:** Microfiber cleaning cloth set, 12 pack, 30×30 cm, colour-coded by room (kitchen / bathroom / glass / general)
2. **Amazon category / subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Limpieza y mantenimiento del hogar › Bayetas y paños (also indexes under Textiles de cocina for the kitchen-labelled colour)
3. **Why worth researching:** Microfiber cleaning cloths are the archetypal replenishment SKU — cheap, consumed, re-ordered. Amazon.es is flooded with generic "10-pack" listings but very few brands sell them as a **system** (colour = purpose). System-selling is a proven private-label upgrade: same unit cost, higher perceived value, higher basket price, better reviews (customers report they actually use them correctly).
4. **Demand signals (qualitative — to be verified):** *Bayetas microfibra* is a high-volume Amazon.es keyword; sub-keywords (*paño microfibra cocina*, *paño cristal microfibra*) are steady daily searches; low seasonality; strong repeat-buy signal.
5. **Competition signals:** Very fragmented, dominated by very cheap generic 10–24 packs at €6–€14, plus one or two well-known German cleaning brands at premium prices. Middle ground (€12–€20, curated 12-pack, colour-system) is thin.
6. **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
   - Photos are stock — cloths pictured folded on white, no visible weave.
   - Colours in listing don't match delivered product.
   - No GSM / grammage disclosure; complaints about "flimsy" cloths.
   - No usage guidance in Spanish → customers use one cloth for everything and complain of streaks.
   - Some listings over-claim ("antibacterial", "kills 99% of germs") without any certification — a compliance risk to **avoid**.
7. **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
   - **Colour = purpose** system, explained on a Spanish insert card: rojo = baño, azul = cocina, amarillo = cristales, verde = general.
   - Publish real GSM (target ~ 300 g/m²) and edge-stitch (not laser-cut).
   - Neutral kraft box, not polybag — giftable at the same unit cost.
   - Honest listing: **no** antibacterial claim; instead "reutilizable, lavable a 60 °C, 300 lavados".
   - Spanish, Portuguese, English on the sleeve.
8. **Bundle / variation strategy:** One hero SKU: **12-pack colour-coded**, €16.90. Second SKU later: a **24-pack refill** (all one colour, chosen by buyer) €24.90 for heavy users. Do not fragment into single-colour SKUs — kills the system story.
9. **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (very light, small FBA tier, low return risk, no size complexity).
10. **Score /50:**
    - Demand: 8/10
    - Competition: 6/10
    - Differentiation: 8/10
    - Margin potential: 7/10
    - Risk: 9/10
    - **Total: 38/50**

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Weighted Blanket (Manta con peso, e.g. 7 kg / 9 kg)

- **Why rejected:** Heavy → high FBA fee tier, high freight cost, high damage rate in transit. High **return rate** in Amazon reviews (customer picks wrong weight, texture, or size). Adjacent to therapeutic / sleep-aid claims that Rosanube must **not** make without documentation. Compliance friction on filler (glass-bead vs. plastic-bead, GPSR).
- **Fit with Rosanube:** Poor — bulky, heavy, high risk of loss on returns/damage, weak brand fit.
- **Verdict:** NO GO.

### B) Silk Pillowcase / Funda de Almohada de Seda

- **Why rejected:** Category increasingly dominated by international established brands (Slip, Blissy, Kitsch and similar) with heavy marketing budgets; private-label entry needs premium photography, model shots (hair benefit angle), and influencer-style content that Rosanube's current brand model is not set up for. High return risk driven by colour-accuracy complaints and "not real silk" reviews. Certification friction (momme count, mulberry silk claim needs proof).
- **Fit with Rosanube:** Off-brand — Rosanube is practical/everyday, not luxury/beauty.
- **Verdict:** NO GO.

### C) Baby Muslin Swaddle Blanket (Muselinas para bebé, pack 4)

- **Why rejected:** Baby & nursery category triggers **higher compliance overhead** on Amazon.es (safety, labelling, sometimes gated). Category is dominated by strong global brands (Aden + Anais and equivalents). Review expectations are extremely high — parents leave 1-star reviews for minor issues (dye smell, loose threads). Return risk elevated. Rosanube has no baby-brand story to lean on.
- **Fit with Rosanube:** Off-brand; compliance-heavy for a first foray into baby textiles.
- **Verdict:** NO GO (Watchlist only if a dedicated baby sub-brand is ever created).
