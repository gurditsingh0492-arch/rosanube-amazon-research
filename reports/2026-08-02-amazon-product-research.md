## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Duvet / Nordic Down Filling (*relleno nórdico plumón / edredón nórdico plumas*)
- **Why rejected:** Bulky, heavy, high FBA size tier, expensive freight, complicated return handling (bedding hygiene). Category is anchored by established European brands (Pikolin, Velfont, El Corte Inglés house brands). Tog rating / down origin / duck-vs-goose expectations mean customers expect certificates Rosanube cannot document today (Downpass, RDS). Violates the config's "avoid very heavy / bulky / complex compliance" rules. Poor fit for a first-year private label.

### B) Baby Muslin Swaddle Blanket (*muselina bebé arrullo*)
- **Why rejected:** Baby textiles = higher compliance load (safety file, extra-strict GPSR, higher expected certificates, negative-review sensitivity extreme). Category dominated by Aden + Anais and a wall of European baby brands. Legal/reputational risk is disproportionate for a brand not positioned in the baby segment. Config §"Products to Avoid" flags medical / safety-restricted risk — baby textiles sit close to that line even if the product itself is textile-standard. Revisit only if Rosanube ever launches a dedicated baby sub-brand with its own compliance file.

### C) Electric Heated Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** **Not a textile product** in the FBA sense — it's electronics + textile. Requires CE marking with electrical scope, low-voltage directive, EMC directive, and Amazon.es electrical safety documentation. Config explicitly bans "Electronics" and "Batteries" and flags dangerous-goods / complicated certifications. High return risk (thermostat failure, cord wear, complaints of not heating evenly). Zero fit for Rosanube — reject outright, not even watchlist.
