The Amazon.es sub-category is dominated at the bottom by cheap memory-foam and PVC-backed mats with recurring complaints about odour, shedding, and mould. A **100% cotton chenille / rizo** mat with an anti-slip backing sits in a cleaner premium slot that Rosanube can occupy without competing on price with commodity foam mats.   - GSM / cotton weight not stated.

### 2) 100% Cotton Percale Pillowcase 2-pack (Spanish sizes)

- **Spanish keyword:** *fundas almohada algodón percal*, *funda almohada 45x110*, *fundas almohada 2 unidades algodón 200 hilos*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Fundas de almohada
- **Why worth researching:** Different from the July 1 pillow *protector* — this is the visible pillowcase (funda) with the classic Spanish sizes (45×110 cm for cama de 90/135 individual, 45×155 cm for cama de 150+, and 40×60 cm for cojín). Percale (thread count 180–200) with a crisp hand-feel is under-represented at a fair price on Amazon.es — dominated at the bottom by microfibre / satin polyester and at the top by two established bedding brands. Standardised sizes = low return risk. High replenishment behaviour (customers buy 2-packs and re-buy every 6–18 months).
- **Demand signals:** Steady year-round on Amazon.es; small uplift in September (back-to-home / student flats) and around Navidad (gifting linens).

- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fabric composition unclear or misleading — "algodón" listings that are actually 50/50 polycotton.

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Direct violation of config's "Avoid electronics / batteries / products requiring complicated certifications" rules. Electrical safety certification (CE + LVD + EMC + RoHS + Spanish REBT considerations), fire-risk liability, and Amazon.es category restrictions all apply. High return rate on electronics-adjacent home textiles. Also brand-dominated by established heat-blanket names (Beurer, Medisana, etc.). Not a Rosanube brand fit at all.

### B) Bamboo Bed Sheet Set (*sábanas bambú*)
- **Why rejected:** "Bamboo" textile marketing is heavily scrutinised in the EU — most so-called bamboo fabric is actually bamboo-viscose (rayon), and honest labelling is legally required (EU Textile Regulation). "Eco / natural / hypoallergenic" claims are also expected by buyers but not defensible without documented certificates (OEKO-TEX / FSC bamboo origin) — per config compliance rules, Rosanube cannot use these claims without documentation. Category also dominated by a few established bamboo-linen brands with heavy PPC spend. High return risk from feel / colour mismatch. Wrong compliance profile for a first launch.

### C) Round Microfibre Beach Towel (*toalla playa redonda microfibra*)
- **Why rejected:** Highly seasonal (May–July peak on Amazon.es) — by 31 July, the 2026 window is essentially closed and inventory arriving in September would sit through 9 months of storage before the next peak. Category also saturated with cheap Chinese generic offerings racing to €8–€12 with hundreds of colour/print variations (variation sprawl violates Rosanube's "avoid too many variations" rule). Weak differentiation opportunity for a private-label brand focused on year-round replenishment textiles. Better revisited in Q1 2027 for a proper May 2027 launch, if at all.
