### 2) Waterproof Mattress Protector — Fitted, Terry-Cotton Surface

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector colchón impermeable*, *cubre colchón impermeable ajustable*, *protector colchón rizo algodón*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** A boring, high-utility, high-repeat item that Spanish buyers replace often (kids, pets, apartments with older mattresses). Fitted design keeps the size tree simple — Amazon.es demand concentrates on standard Spanish mattress widths (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm). Terry-cotton top + PU membrane is the workhorse spec that survives washing. Rosanube can enter with **one width** and expand.
- **Demand signals:** Consistent daily demand; utility purchase, not seasonal. Reviews on top competitors accumulate steadily rather than in spikes — a healthier signal than trend-driven categories.
- **Competition signals:** Present but fragmented. A few household brands anchor the top (Pikolin, Belnou, Amazon Basics) but many private labels sit in slots 5–30 with 4-figure reviews.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Ruidoso / plastic feel" complaints — thin membrane, no terry.
  - Elastic skirt too shallow → doesn't fit modern 25–30 cm mattresses.
  - Sizing inconsistencies (labelled 150×190, ships 140×200).
  - Compliance-risky wording: *hipoalergénico*, *antiácaros*, *antibacterial* used without documented certificates.
  - Care instructions unclear on wash temperature; buyers report membrane cracking after hot wash.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Soft terry-cotton top (documented composition, e.g. 80% cotton / 20% PES) — quiet under sleep.
  - Deep 30 cm elastic skirt with reinforced corners, explicitly stated.
  - **Do not** claim *antiácaros* / *hipoalergénico* / *antibacterial* unless the supplier provides a valid certificate (per config §Compliance Rules).
  - Guarantee correct Spanish mattress sizes; label in cm, on the packaging in Spanish.
  - Care label with 60 °C max wash — visible in listing photos.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as **single SKU = 150×190/200 cm** (the most common Spanish double mattress). Add 90, 135, 180 cm widths only after reviews stabilise. Do **not** launch 5 sizes at once — inventory risk.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (compliance restraint matters; return risk manageable if sizing is accurate)

Return rate on heated blankets is chronically high (electrical failure, thermostat complaints). Not Rosanube's lane.

Not a Rosanube brand fit; would dilute the home-textile positioning without a strong reason.
