## Executive Summary

Late August into September is the Amazon.es *vuelta al cole* + autumn-bedroom-refresh window. Spanish shoppers are switching light summer linens for warmer bedding, buying replacement mattress protectors for kids' beds, and refreshing kitchen textiles after summer wear. Today's five picks lean into that seasonal pull while staying strictly inside Rosanube's core textile lane: bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom replenishment SKUs with fragmented (not brand-dominated) competition, weak-but-selling incumbent listings, and clear differentiation angles.

None of the five is recommended for a purchase order today. All are proposed for **Further Research** or **Watchlist** pending Helium 10, Keepa, supplier quotes, and FBA calculator results.

### 1) Waterproof Mattress Protector — Fitted, 90×190 cm (kids/single bed)

- **Spanish keyword:** *protector de colchón impermeable 90*, *cubrecolchón impermeable transpirable*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Fundas y protectores de colchón
- **Why worth researching:** Peak *vuelta al cole* replacement SKU — parents refreshing children's beds late August/early September. Small size (90×190) keeps FBA fee tier low; single-size focus removes variation complexity; strong repeat-purchase and cross-sell into 135 and 150 sizes later.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — Needs manual confirmation):** Consistently high review volumes across incumbent listings; category shows non-seasonal baseline with a clear late-August/early-September uplift and a second peak around January (post-Reyes). Replenishment cycle 12–24 months.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented — mix of Pikolin, Belnou, Velfont at the top plus a long tail of private-label sellers. Common price band **€12–€22** for a single 90×190. Review counts on top listings range 500–8,000. Big brands hold hero positions but leave room in the €14–€18 "quality private-label" middle.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Vague waterproofing claims ("100% waterproof") without membrane spec or hydrostatic-head data.
  - Complaints about crinkly TPU noise and heat/sweating under the sleeper.
  - Elastic skirt tears after 20–30 washes.
  - Photography focused on the packaged product, not the sleeping context.
  - Spanish copy machine-translated from English; no clear washing/tumble-dry instructions.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Cotton-terry top surface (breathable) bonded to a thin, quiet TPU membrane; state grammage of the terry layer.
  - Publish the hydrostatic-head figure and a real machine-wash cycle count (e.g. "tested to 60 °C, 50 washes").
  - Reinforced elastic skirt corners with a bar-tacked stitch — document the change in the A+ content.
  - Spanish-language care label + fibre composition; kraft-sleeve packaging, giftable-adjacent.
  - Neutral, calm brand presence — no "medical/hypoallergenic/antibacterial" claims (config forbids without certificates).
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **single SKU** = 90×190 cm fitted. After 60–90 days of stable reviews, add 105×190 and 135×190 as sibling ASINs; only add a 2-pack when returns and margin are proven.

### 2) Percale Cotton Pillowcase Pair — 50×75 cm, 200 TC

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda de almohada 50x75 algodón*, *fundas almohada percal 200 hilos*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada
- **Why worth researching:** True replenishment textile — pillowcases wear/stain and are replaced far more often than the pillow. Very small pack size = lowest FBA fee tier, easy to compress, low freight per unit. 50×75 is the Spanish default cushion size (distinct from UK/US), so listings that get the Spanish size + Spanish copy right can outrank imported generic listings.
- **Demand signals (Needs manual confirmation):** Steady year-round demand with a small September uplift alongside duvet-cover sales. Pair (2-unit) format dominates the buy-box winners.
- **Competition signals:** Very fragmented — dozens of small private labels; a few EU brands (Pikolin, Today, Vivaraise) present but not dominant in the 50×75 percale mid-tier. Price band **€9–€18** for a 2-pack. Review counts vary widely (50–3,000).
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thread count claims ("200 hilos", "300 hilos") not backed by GSM or weave photos — buyers complain the fabric feels thinner than promised.
  - Envelope closure gaps → pillow visible; buyers ask for zipper or wider overlap.
  - Colours shift between listing images and delivered product (grey turns lilac, "beige" is yellow).
  - Weak Spanish copy; "pillowcase" left in English in bullets.
  - Missing Spanish care/composition label complaints in reviews.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Honest spec block: GSM, thread count, weave (percal), Oeko-Tex only if a valid certificate exists from the supplier.
  - Deeper envelope flap (min. 20 cm) so pillow never shows.
  - Muted "casa mediterránea" palette: blanco roto, arena, salvia, gris niebla — 4 colourways only.
  - Photograph on a made bed with morning light; include a swatch macro so buyers see the weave.
  - Bilingual (ES/PT) care label; kraft belly-band packaging.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch as a **2-pack pair** per colour (4 SKUs total). Later, add a "hotel weight" 300-hilos version at €5–€7 higher price if the base version proves demand; consider a duvet-cover cross-sell listing bundle once cataloged.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low**
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 6/10 (low ASP compresses margin — needs tight supplier cost)
  - Risk: 9/10

### 3) Cotton-Reversible Bath Rug — Machine-Washable, 50×80 cm

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón lavable*, *alfombrilla baño rizo antideslizante*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** The bath-rug top-sellers on Amazon.es are dominated by microfiber shag/memory-foam variants that shed and can't go in the washer at 60 °C. A woven cotton reversible rug is a differentiated, honest alternative — repeat-purchase (they get stained/discoloured), simple size range, and low compliance friction. Weight tier still keeps FBA fees manageable at 50×80.
- **Demand signals (Needs manual confirmation):** Steady year-round; small September and January refresh peaks. "Bath rug" is a top-100 Amazon.es query in the Baño subcategory.
- **Competition signals:** Middle-fragmented — a handful of larger brands in memory-foam (Amago, InterDesign) but the cotton washable segment is a niche with many small sellers and few standout winners. Price band **€15–€28** for 50×80 cotton. Review counts often 100–1,500.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Antideslizante" claimed but latex backing peels or crumbles after 10 washes.
  - Colours fade heavily on first hot wash.
  - No stated GSM / pile height; buyers can't tell how thick it is.
  - Images all top-down on white — no bathroom-context lifestyle shot.
  - Tassels that shed or knot.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Backing: dot-latex printed in a grid rather than a solid coat — stays flexible after washing; state wash-cycle test result.
  - 100 % cotton chenille loop, reversible (two tone-on-tone sides), no tassels — reduces return risk from shedding.
  - Publish GSM (target 1,700–2,000 g/m²) and pile density.
  - Same "casa mediterránea" palette as the pillowcase line so buyers can build a cohesive bathroom set later.
  - Kraft-sleeve packaging with Spanish + Portuguese wash instructions on the sleeve.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Start with 50×80 in 4 colours. If reviews stabilise, add a 60×100 sibling and a 50×80 + 40×60 (bath + basin) 2-pack; hold off on U-shape toilet contour rugs — high return risk.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (backing durability is the make-or-break; sample QC critical)

  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 8/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10

### 4) 100% Linen Tea Towel Set — 3-pack, 45×70 cm

- **Spanish keyword:** *paños de cocina lino*, *trapos cocina 100% lino*, *paños lino natural*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina
- **Why worth researching:** Complements — not competes with — the waffle-weave kitchen towel idea already in the pipeline (July report). Linen is a premium tier (€18–€28 for a 3-pack) where the buyer explicitly wants natural fibre, photographs beautifully, is lint-free (major cotton-towel complaint), and lets Rosanube take a higher ASP without moving out of the €15–€35 sweet spot.
- **Demand signals (Needs manual confirmation):** Smaller absolute volume than cotton kitchen towels but rising trend in "natural fibre / slow home" Spanish content; steady demand from home cooks and gift buyers. Peaks around Reyes (Jan) and Navidad (Dec) — plenty of time to launch for the seasonal uplift.
- **Competition signals:** Thin — a handful of European linen brands (Linen Tales, LinenMe) plus imported Baltic private-label listings; not a Spanish-branded winner. Price band **€16–€32** for a 3-pack. Review counts modest (50–800) meaning the review-entry barrier is low.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Lino" listings that are actually linen/cotton blends — buyer complaints on being misled.
  - Wrinkled photography that reads as "unironed" rather than "natural" — puts off buyers.
  - Stitched hems fray after 10–15 washes.
  - No dimensions in cm on the main image.
  - Colours limited to natural + white; no mid-tone.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - **100 % pure linen**, stated GSM (target 180–220 g/m²), origin (EU flax vs. Chinese linen) — disclose honestly.
  - Mitered corners with a small hanging loop (Belgian-style) — real, documented change.
  - Style the photo set: three towels folded on a cutting board with olive oil, tomatoes, ceramic. Photographs like a lifestyle magazine, sells the premium.
  - 3-pack with three tone-on-tone colours (natural, sage, terracotta) instead of three identical.
  - Kraft sleeve with a printed "how linen softens after each wash" note in Spanish.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch one SKU = 3-pack mixed neutrals, 45×70 cm. Later add a linen apron cross-sell and a linen napkin 4-pack once reviews cross ~150 and the audience is defined.

### 5) 200×200 cm Duvet Cover Set — Cotton Percale, 3-piece (funda + 2 fundas almohada)

- **Spanish keyword:** *funda nórdica 200x200*, *juego funda nórdica cama 135*, *funda nordica algodón percal*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Ropa de cama › Fundas nórdicas y juegos
- **Why worth researching:** Highest-ASP idea on today's shortlist (€30–€45 range) and squarely inside the autumn bedroom-refresh window. 200×200 is the Spanish standard for a 135 cm bed — the most common bed size in Spain. A 3-piece set (1 duvet cover + 2 pillowcases) simplifies the buyer's decision and lets Rosanube capture what would otherwise be three separate SKU sales.
- **Demand signals (Needs manual confirmation):** Strong late-August through November uplift as Spanish households swap thin summer covers for warmer autumn/winter bedding; second peak in January (Reyes/rebajas). Non-seasonal baseline in top-100 Ropa de cama results.
- **Competition signals:** Split — a top layer of Pikolin, Sancarlos, Barceló Hogar occupy premium slots; below them a dense midfield of imported private labels €25–€40. Review counts on winners 500–5,000; midfield 50–500.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Buttonhole closure at the foot rips after 3–5 wash cycles — very common complaint.
  - Corner ties missing → duvet slips inside the cover.
  - Fabric described as "algodón percal" but feels like polycotton — no composition label attached.
  - Photography over-saturated; colour on delivery is a different tone.
  - No size guide showing which bed the 200×200 fits (Spanish buyers confuse 90/135/150 bed → duvet size).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Real 100 % cotton percale, GSM stated (target 110–130 g/m² for percale).
  - Coconut-shell buttons (10-button closure) + internal corner ties in all 4 corners — both are visible, credible differentiators.
  - Include a small on-listing infographic: "cama 135 → funda 200×200" so the buyer never picks the wrong size.
  - Same "casa mediterránea" palette across pillowcase + duvet cover + bath-rug lines so the brand feels like a collection, not scattered SKUs.
  - Kraft-belly-band packaging with EU fibre composition + Spanish care label sewn in.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 200×200 3-piece in one hero colour first (e.g. blanco roto). If reviews stabilise, add 2 more colours in the same 200×200 size before opening 240×220 (cama 150) or 155×220 (cama 90 individual) — resist launching all sizes at once.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (highest ASP + highest per-unit inventory tie-up; wash-durability is the review make-or-break)

## Do Not Pursue — 3 Products Investigated and Rejected

### A) Weighted Blanket 7 kg (*manta con peso*)
- **Reason:** Heavy (7 kg) and bulky — pushes into oversize FBA tier, high fulfilment fee eats margin. Sleep/anxiety-related buyer expectations create a high return risk if fill distribution isn't perfect. Adjacent to medical/therapeutic claims that `research_config.md` forbids without documentation. Dominated by Levia, Gravity, YnM at the top with heavy PPC competition. **Decision: NO GO.**

### B) Silk Pillowcase 50×75 cm (*funda almohada seda*)
- **Reason:** Beauty/hair claims ("anti-frizz", "reduces wrinkles") sit uncomfortably close to cosmetic claims Rosanube cannot substantiate. Real 22-momme mulberry silk is expensive at supplier level (€8–€14 landed) yet Amazon.es ASP is €12–€22 — margin is thin, and cheaper polyester "silk-touch" competitors drive price collapse. Very high review sensitivity to authenticity — one "not real silk" review kills the listing. **Decision: NO GO.**

### C) Electric Heated Throw Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Reason:** Electrical product — falls into the config's "Avoid: electronics / batteries" bucket and requires CE + LVD + EMC + Spanish plug type certification and an appointed EU importer with a technical file. GPSR responsibility is heavier than for a textile. High return risk if the heating element fails under warranty. Excellent seasonal demand (Oct–Feb) does not offset compliance load for a Portugal-based textile private-label brand. **Decision: NO GO — outside Rosanube brand and compliance envelope.**
