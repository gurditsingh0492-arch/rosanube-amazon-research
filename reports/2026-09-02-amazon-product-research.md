## Top 5 Product Opportunities

### 1) 100% Linen Table Runner (140–220 cm, natural / neutral)

- **Spanish keyword:** *camino de mesa lino*, *runner mesa lino natural*, *camino mesa lino lavado*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de mesa › Caminos de mesa
- **Why worth researching:** Linen table runners sit in a small but persistent Spanish home-décor niche. Search intent skews toward "cena elegante", "mesa navideña", and "boda/comunión" — three overlapping demand pulses that keep the SKU non-seasonal. Runners are dimensionally simple (one length × one width), photograph beautifully, and let a private label anchor its brand tone with fabric quality rather than complex artwork.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Steady long-tail demand around table-setting keywords; uplift in Nov–Dec (Navidad) and Apr–Jun (comuniones, bodas). Multiple listings sustain 3-figure review counts, which suggests reachable review parity in 6–12 months.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented. A handful of small brands from Portugal, Spain, and Poland compete on "washed linen" positioning, alongside generic Chinese "lino" listings whose reviews complain the fabric is polyester/cotton blend, not real linen. Price band **€18–€32** for a single 140–200 cm runner in real linen; polyester "lino aspecto" runners sit at €9–€15.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Fibre composition ambiguous or mislabelled ("lino" that is actually 100% polyester) — a real EU-Textile-Regulation-compliant label is a differentiator.
  - GSM never stated (linen buyers care — 180–240 g/m² is the sweet spot for runners).
  - Photography is white-background only; buyers want to see the drape on a real dining table.
  - Sizes buried in the title (needs a clear cm chart).
  - Complaints about wrinkling being "excessive" — no listing sets expectation that linen wrinkles are the aesthetic.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish honest fibre composition (100% flax linen), origin region, GSM, and pre-washed status.
  - "Stone-washed" softness with a clear photo of the fabric hand.
  - Three neutrals only: natural undyed, off-white, sage or terracotta.
  - Spanish-language listing copy that treats wrinkles as intentional (Iberian, mediterranean styling).
  - Kraft belly-band with washing icons in ES/PT/EN + a small "how to iron linen" insert card.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = natural 45×180 cm. Add a 45×220 cm long-table version once reviews > 50; test a 2-pack (runner + 4 napkins) as a Navidad giftable once the base SKU is stable.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low–Medium** (main risk = supplier fibre honesty; sample QA is critical).

  - Competition: 7/10
  - Differentiation: 9/10

### 2) Cotton Chenille Tufted Bath Mat (50×80 cm, non-slip backing)

- **Spanish keyword:** *alfombra baño algodón*, *alfombrilla baño antideslizante algodón*, *alfombra baño chenilla*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Alfombras de baño
- **Why worth researching:** Cotton bath mats are a true replenishment SKU (worn, washed, discarded within 12–24 months) and a common add-to-cart companion to bath towels — Rosanube's core lane. Chenille tufted construction is soft, absorbent, machine-washable, and photographs well. Non-slip backing solves the #1 review complaint in the category ("resbala en el suelo mojado").
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Consistent, non-seasonal, with a small uplift each Sept–Oct as households refresh bathroom textiles. Multiple mid-tier listings sit around 500–3,000 reviews — the category clearly moves inventory.
- **Competition signals:** Two overlapping cohorts: (a) budget microfibre mats at €7–€12 with heavy Chinese-brand presence, and (b) mid-tier cotton mats at €14–€24 that are fragmented across small brands. The mid-tier is where Rosanube belongs; it is contested but not dominated.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - "Antideslizante" claimed but the backing is a thin rubber-dot pattern that peels after a few washes — a persistent 1–2-star complaint.
  - GSM/pile weight not stated (buyers can't tell "hotel plush" from "flat and thin").
  - Colours look one shade in photos, arrive another (colour-accuracy complaint).
  - Sizes buried; buyers want to see the mat on a full bathroom floor for scale.
  - No care instructions in Spanish.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real pile weight (target 1,600–1,800 g/m²) and backing type (latex-dot or full-latex, tested to survive ≥ 30 washes).
  - Three muted neutrals: piedra (stone), lino (linen), verde salvia (sage). Skip saturated colours.
  - Lifestyle photo on Spanish bathroom tile with a real bath towel folded beside — cross-sell composition.
  - Care label in ES/PT/EN + a small "primer lavado" (first wash) tip card to reduce return risk.
  - Rounded corners and reinforced edge binding as a small documented durability change.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **one SKU** = 50×80 cm stone. Add sage + linen colours after 30 reviews. Test a 2-piece set (large + contour mat) only after the anchor SKU is profitable.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (backing durability drives review sentiment; sample QA and ≥ 30-wash test are non-negotiable).
- **Score /50:**
  - Demand: 8/10
  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 7/10
  - Margin potential: 7/10
  - Risk: 6/10
  - **Total: 34/50**

### 3) Microfibre Hair Turban / Quick-Dry Hair Wrap (2-pack)

- **Spanish keyword:** *toalla turbante pelo*, *toalla pelo microfibra*, *turbante secar pelo*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Baño › Toallas › Toallas para el cabello
- **Why worth researching:** Small, light, and cheap to ship — a very FBA-friendly form factor with a low landed cost. Steady replenishment demand from anyone with long or curly hair, and a natural bundle companion to Rosanube's future bath-towel range. Also a common gift-with-purchase or upsell.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Consistent evergreen demand with mild Nov–Dec gift uplift. Multiple listings in this sub-niche run 4-figure review counts, indicating real category volume.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented but dense at the €6–€10 single-pack level (generic private-label). The **2-pack at €12–€18** and **3-pack at €15–€22** are less contested and where a brand can defend price.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - GSM never stated (300–350 g/m² microfibre is the comfortable range).
  - Loop / button closures fail after a few uses — a repeated 1–2 star complaint.
  - Photos on white background only, no head model → buyers can't judge fit or size.
  - Colours arrive different from listing (dye-lot complaints).
  - Care label missing or English-only; some listings claim "antibacteriano" without any basis (avoid).
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Reinforced button + elastic loop with a documented pull test (small, honest durability claim).
  - Two-fabric variant option — microfibre for speed, or bamboo-cotton blend for sensitive scalps (positioned as gentle, not medical).
  - Three neutrals + one accent (piedra, lino, blanco roto, terracota).
  - Lifestyle photos with a diversity of hair types (curly, long, short, wet-out-of-shower).
  - Kraft belly-band packaging, care in ES/PT/EN, **no** unsubstantiated "antibacteriano/hipoalergénico" claims.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Anchor SKU = **2-pack microfibre piedra + lino at €14.90**. Later add a mixed 3-pack (2 microfibre + 1 bamboo-cotton) at €19.90. Cross-sell with any future Rosanube bath towel.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (small parcel FBA tier, low return risk, cheap sampling, low sourcing risk).

  - Competition: 6/10
  - Differentiation: 6/10

### 4) Percale Cotton Pillowcase 2-Pack (50×75 cm, 200 TC)

- **Spanish keyword:** *fundas almohada algodón percal*, *fundas almohada 50x75*, *fundas almohada percal 200 hilos*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de cama › Fundas de almohada
- **Why worth researching:** Pillowcases are the **entry SKU** to the bedroom category — cheaper to sample, cheaper to store, and cheaper to fail than duvet covers or full sheet sets. They let Rosanube prove supplier quality and Spanish listing/PPC skill in the bed lane before committing to full sheet sets. Standard Spanish pillow size is 50×75 cm (or 45×110 cm), so variation complexity is manageable.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Very steady, non-seasonal, with mild Sept–Oct back-to-routine uplift. Category depth is real (many listings with 4-figure review counts).
- **Competition signals:** Contested but fragmented. Big players (Amazon Basics, Utopia Bedding, Pikolin Home) hold volume at the low end (€8–€12 for a 2-pack); the **€14–€22 mid tier** is where small brands with real percale and honest thread counts can defend a price. Not brand-dominated at the mid tier.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Thread count claimed (300 TC, 400 TC) without any weave detail — buyers who know percale can see through this.
  - Sizes sold in "estándar" without cm — a persistent buyer complaint.
  - Cotton percentage often 50/50 poly-cotton disguised as "algodón".
  - Envelope closure poorly stitched — pillow comes out of the case.
  - Colour drift between listing photo and delivered product.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish real weave (percale), real thread count (200 TC is fine — don't inflate), and 100% cotton composition on the main image.
  - **50×75 cm** clearly stated in the title (the actual Spanish standard).
  - Reinforced envelope closure (small, documented change).
  - Three neutrals only: blanco roto, piedra, gris humo.
  - Bilingual (ES/PT) care label, GPSR-compliant packaging, EPR Spain registered.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Anchor SKU = **2-pack blanco roto 50×75 cm at €16.90**. Add piedra + gris humo colours after 30 reviews. Only after the pillowcase SKU is profitable, consider a full percale sheet set as a separate ASIN.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Medium** (competes with Amazon Basics on price floor; must hold €15+ ASP with quality proof, not races to €9).

### 5) Cotton Muslin Multipurpose Cloth (6-pack, 70×70 cm)

- **Spanish keyword:** *gasas algodón cocina*, *paños muselina cocina*, *paños algodón uso múltiple*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Textiles de cocina › Paños de cocina (crosses into Limpieza)
- **Why worth researching:** Muslin cotton cloths are a category "orphan" on Amazon.es — most muselina listings are baby-focused (baby gasas), but there is a growing home-use audience: straining broth, covering dough, drying herbs, polishing glass, and lint-free cleaning. A small brand can own the "cocina" positioning that baby brands ignore. Very light, very cheap to ship, very forgiving on returns.
- **Demand signals (qualitative — verify):** Modest but growing search volume around the "muselina cocina" and "gasa algodón cocina" long tail. Adjacent category (baby muslin) is large — a sign the fabric itself is understood by Spanish buyers.
- **Competition signals:** Almost no dedicated **kitchen-muslin** brands on Amazon.es. Most competing SKUs are baby muslin repurposed by buyers. Price band **€12–€22** for a 6-pack of 70×70 cm home-use muslin.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Baby-branded muslin dominates → the "for cooking" use case is invisible in images.
  - Fibre composition often ambiguous (100% cotton vs cotton-poly).
  - Size varies wildly (60×60, 70×70, 80×80) with no clear reason.
  - No use-case guidance in Spanish (the buyer has to figure out "what do I do with this?").
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Positioning: "paños de muselina para cocina y hogar" — cooking, straining, wiping glass, herb drying, cheese pressing. Not baby.
  - Publish real GSM (100–140 g/m² is standard) and 100% cotton composition.
  - 6-pack of 70×70 cm in natural undyed and off-white.
  - A small use-case insert (kraft card) in Spanish: 5 usos en la cocina — turns the listing into a mini "how to".
  - Kraft banded packaging, hanging loop on each cloth, care in ES/PT/EN.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Anchor SKU = **6-pack 70×70 cm natural at €16.90**. Add a mixed 6-pack (3 natural + 3 off-white) as a variant. Later, cross-bundle with the waffle kitchen towel SKU from the 2026-07-01 shortlist as a "Rosanube Cocina" giftable.
- **Estimated risk level:** **Low** (small parcel FBA tier, low return risk, cheap sampling, low compliance load — no baby claims).

## Do Not Pursue (rejected today)

### A) Weighted Blanket (Manta con peso, 5–9 kg)

- **Why rejected:** Bulky and heavy → high FBA fulfilment tier + high storage cost + high freight cost per unit; damage-in-transit risk; wellness/therapeutic claims risk (avoid "ansiedad / dormir mejor" wording). Dominated by a few larger brands in Spain (Levity, YnM). Return risk on weight-fit mismatch. Fails Rosanube's "lightweight, easy FBA, low compliance" ideal profile.
- **Decision:** NO GO.

### B) Silk / Satin Pillowcase (Funda almohada seda)

- **Why rejected:** High "hair/skin benefit" claim exposure → EU consumer-law and Amazon claims risk if we cannot prove momme count and dermatological benefit. High colour/quality expectations drive returns. Real mulberry silk supplier costs are high → thin margin at the €20–€30 price band where competitors have already conditioned buyers. Better revisited only after Rosanube has a proven percale pillowcase listing and a certified silk supplier.
- **Decision:** Rejected today, revisit in 6 months.

### C) Electric Heated Blanket (Manta eléctrica)

- **Why rejected:** Electrical product → CE/UKCA + LVD + EMC + RoHS certification burden; heavy and bulky; seasonal demand (Nov–Feb only); dominated by established brands (Imetec, Beurer, Daga, Medisana); safety-recall exposure. Explicit fail against config: "Electronics" and "products requiring complicated certifications" are on the Avoid list.
- **Decision:** NO GO (permanent — outside Rosanube brand + skill fit).
