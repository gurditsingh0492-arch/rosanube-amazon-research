## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket / Manta Eléctrica
- **Why rejected:** Electronic product — violates config §"Products to Avoid" (electronics, batteries/power, safety-certification burden). Requires EU low-voltage / EMC certification, and Amazon.es enforces category compliance strictly. Return risk is high (safety concerns, overheating complaints). Bulky FBA size. Poor Rosanube brand fit (textile brand, not electronics). Automatic NO GO regardless of any demand signal.

### B) Full Duvet Cover Set (funda nórdica juego completo, cotton percale)
- **Why rejected:** Requires many sizes (90 / 135 / 150 / 180 / 200) × many colours to be viable → violates config's "too many sizes/colours" rule. Return risk is high (colour, feel, sizing complaints). Top slots on Amazon.es are anchored by strong bedding brands with 5k–20k reviews. Model / lifestyle bedroom photography is expensive to produce well. Better first-launch categories exist that don't force Rosanube into apparel-like variation trees.

### C) Bamboo Fitted Sheet Set (juego sábanas bambú)
- **Why rejected:** "Bamboo" fibre labelling is a **regulatory trap** in the EU — under EU 1007/2011, most "bamboo" textile is legally *viscose de bambú* and must be labelled as viscose. Making a "bamboo" claim without correct labelling risks Amazon compliance takedown *and* consumer authority action. Also has the same size-complexity problem as the duvet cover set. Combined with size variation and premium-brand competition, this is a very poor first launch for Rosanube.
