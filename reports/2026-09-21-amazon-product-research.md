## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### R1) Silk Pillowcase ("100% Mulberry Silk")

- **Why rejected:** High return / complaint risk (buyers dispute weight in *momme*, dye transfer, hair-frizz claims). "Mulberry silk" and "100% seda de morera" are heavily fake-claimed on the marketplace — Rosanube would either enter a truth-in-labelling risk zone or price out of the mass market. Product needs specific claims (momme weight, OEKO-TEX certificate) that would require documented supplier certificates before any listing. Dominated by beauty-adjacent brands with strong social proof. **Poor brand fit** for the "practical, clean, everyday" Rosanube positioning.
- **Config triggers:** Products with strong IP/labelling risk; high customer complaint rates; requires documented certification; weak differentiation without expensive photography.

### R2) Bamboo Bed Sheet Set

- **Why rejected:** In the EU, "bamboo" fabric must be labelled *viscosa de bambú* / *viscose derived from bamboo* — many Amazon listings use "bamboo" as a marketing shortcut in a way that risks EU Textile Regulation non-compliance. Historic FTC / EU action on misleading "bamboo" claims. Softness/breathability claims are largely qualitative and drive returns when buyer expectations mismatch. Fragmented competition already races to the bottom on price. Compliance risk + labelling risk + return risk together push this below Rosanube's threshold.
- **Config triggers:** Legal / labelling uncertainty; unclear compliance; weak margin after PPC/returns; potential eco-claim risk without documented evidence.

### R3) Weighted Blanket (5–10 kg)

- **Why rejected:** Confirmed rejection from the July 1 report; re-affirmed today. Heavy (5–10 kg) → poor FBA fee tier and freight cost; frequent return risk when weight is wrong for the buyer; dominated by established brands with strong review moats; adjacent to "therapeutic" / "anti-anxiety" claims that Rosanube must not make. Not a fit for the brand's clean home-textile lane and not a fit for the size/weight profile the config prefers.
- **Config triggers:** Very heavy products; bulky / high FBA cost; dominated by big brands; medical/therapeutic claim adjacency; high return risk.
