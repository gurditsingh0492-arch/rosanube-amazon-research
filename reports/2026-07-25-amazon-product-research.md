### 3) Deep-Pocket Fitted Sheet — 30 cm+ (Sábana bajera ajustable altura extra)

- **Spanish keyword:** *sábana bajera ajustable 30 cm*, *sábana bajera colchón alto*, *sábana bajera 135 altura extra*
- **Category / Subcategory:** Hogar y cocina › Ropa de hogar › Dormitorio › Sábanas › Sábanas bajeras
- **Why worth researching:** A very specific customer pain: modern Spanish mattresses (viscoelástico + topper) frequently exceed 25 cm, and standard fitted sheets pop off the corners. The top of the Amazon.es search results is packed with generic 15–20 cm fitted sheets; deep-pocket variants exist but are **poorly signposted** (advertised depth doesn't match delivered depth, complaint driver). A private-label SKU that promises — and delivers — a 30 cm elastic pocket solves a concrete, repeated complaint. Sizes on Amazon.es are simple and standardised (90, 105, 135, 150, 180 cm mattress widths).
- **Demand signals:** Constant year-round demand for fitted sheets in the top three Spanish sizes (90, 135, 150). Deep-pocket sub-search is a growing modifier.
- **Competition signals:** Fragmented at the mid-tier (€12–€22 for a single fitted sheet); a few large bedding brands anchor the top of the category but not the "deep pocket" modifier specifically. Review-entry barrier is **medium-high** because the parent category is huge — but the *deep-pocket* sub-slice is thinner.
- **Common weaknesses in existing listings:**
  - Advertised pocket depth (30 cm) not matching real elastic drop (often 20–22 cm).
  - Elastic dies after 20–30 washes.
  - Pilling of microfibre / poly-cotton after 2 months.
  - Colour swatches inaccurate.
  - Sizing photos too small to read → wrong size ordered → returned.
- **Differentiation ideas for Rosanube:**
  - Publish **real** elastic drop (measured, photographed with a ruler in the listing).
  - Full-perimeter elastic (not just corners).
  - Choose a small, defensible size matrix (start with **135×190 + 150×190** only — the two most common Spanish sizes).
  - Percale 100% cotton (target 200 TC / ≥110 g/m²) or a clearly labelled cotton-rich blend.
  - Muted neutral palette (off-white, stone, sand) — avoid the 15-colour trend.
- **Bundle / variation strategy:** Launch **1 colour × 2 sizes** (135, 150). Add a matching pillowcase 2-pack after reviews stabilise. Deliberately **do not** launch a full sheet set on day one (avoids size-complexity trap flagged in config §"Products to Avoid").
