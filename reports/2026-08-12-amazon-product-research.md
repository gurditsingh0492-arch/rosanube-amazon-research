### 2) Reusable Cellulose / Cotton Kitchen Cloth ("Swedish Dishcloth") — 10-pack (Paños esponja / Bayetas suecas)

## Do Not Pursue (Rejected Today)

### A) Electric Heated Blanket (*manta eléctrica*)
- **Why rejected:** Electronics — violates the config "Products to Avoid" list on multiple counts: electronics, batteries/power, safety certification (LVD, EMC, CE electrical), high return risk (thermostat failure, wire complaints), seasonal (Nov–Feb), and dominated by established brands (Beurer, Medisana, Imetec) with 5k+ reviews. Also outside Rosanube's textile brand fit. NO GO.

### B) Anti-Mite Mattress Encasement (*protector colchón antiácaros / hipoalergénico*)
- **Why rejected:** Category depends on **antiácaros / hipoalergénico** marketing claims. Per config compliance rules, Rosanube cannot make such claims without documented certificates (allergen barrier testing, ISO 22197 or equivalent). Making the claim without certification is an EU consumer-protection risk and Amazon compliance risk. Even with certificates, the top of the category is anchored by big-brand bedding sellers with 4-figure–5-figure review counts. High return risk on fit/size mismatch. Better to stick to a plain zippered pillow protector (01-Jul SKU #3).

### C) Down / Feather Duvet Insert (*relleno nórdico plumón*)
- **Why rejected:** Very bulky → high FBA size tier + storage fees; extremely expensive to ship (compressed vacuum bag or oversized carton). High return risk (weight/warmth mismatch, allergy complaints, feather leakage). Category dominated by Pikolin, Velfont, Amazon Basics and established bedding brands with strong brand loyalty. Requires 3–4 sizes (90/135/150/180) × 2 warmth grades = variation complexity outside config guidance ("too many sizes/variations"). Compliance on animal-origin material (down provenance / RDS-style certification) adds friction. NO GO for Rosanube's first bedding push — a flat cotton duvet **cover** would be a safer future exploration.
