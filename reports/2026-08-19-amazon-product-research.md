- **Why worth researching:** Boring, high-utility, thin competition. Ironing board covers wear out fast (scorch marks, elastic dies, foam flattens) and buyers replace them without much brand loyalty. - **Demand signals:** Steady year-round; peaks slightly in *rebajas* (January, July) when household refresh purchases climb. Consistent 4-figure review counts on top listings signal real demand.

  - Elastic that dies after 5–10 uses.
  - Foam padding that flattens immediately.
  - "Universal fit" that doesn't actually fit common Spanish board sizes (120×38 cm, 130×45 cm).
